Heat Central:Stan Van Gundy is OUT on the Miami Heat and says Bam Adebayo is overrated.

“I’m not as high on Miami as people are… The shooting they added compromises them badly on the defensive end of the floor. Even though he was great last year, I’m not sold on Davion Mitchell as a full time starting PG… I’d actually be surprised if they were in the top 6… I’m not a fan of their roster. — I think Wiggins is underrated but I think Bam is a little overrated, particularly at the offensive end of the floor. I’m not sold on them.”
SGStan Van Gundy on Davion MitchellOct 16, 2026x.com

Giannis Antetokounmpo on being in Miami:
