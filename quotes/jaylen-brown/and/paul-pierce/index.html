What Jaylen Brown and Paul Pierce have said about each other

Jaylen Brown on Paul Pierce, and Paul Pierce on Jaylen Brown: every quote in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

Paul Pierce on the comments saying Jaylen Brown analytics wise he’s the 7th best player on the Celtics:

“I mean this is getting ridiculous. It’s gotten to the point where I’m no longer going to start watching any of the networks about sports. Like seriously this is just come on. I just feel like it’s clickbait. It doesn’t make sense. Like don’t even make that public. If an analytic dude said that and he told you, why would you make that public? Because it sounds silly. Jaylen Brown was a MVP candidate. Now you’re saying, uh, does he do the things that Luka and Wemby? Well, okay. Yeah, the stats on these guys going to look a little different, but there’s one thing you can’t say about Jaylen Brown he influences winning.”
Paul Pierce on Jaylen BrownJun 29, 2026x.com

Paul Pierce on the inevitability of the Jaylen Brown trade:

“It's like if you have a family member who is dying and it's like when they die you saw it coming. It sucks, but you know, when it happens it's like out of the blue, it's different. You know what I'm saying? So it was like, all right. So I came to acceptance of it that was going to happen although I don't like it.”
Paul Pierce on Jaylen BrownJul 4, 2026YouTube

Paul Pierce on the Jayson Tatum–Jaylen Brown situation:

“You got to understand these guys, they’ve been together for quite a while now. I’ve been around them. They’re good friends. They truly don’t care who gets the credit. I mean, we saw Brown had a magnificent run a couple years ago. He won MVP honors of the Eastern Conference and the NBA championship. And there was no jealousy. They like, “Hey, okay, if it’s time for Brown to lead them right now, if that’s what it’s going to take for them to win, Tatum’s willing to sacrifice that.”
Paul Pierce on Jaylen Brown, Jayson TatumMar 9, 2026x.com
