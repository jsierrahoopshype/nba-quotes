Jaylen Brown on who was first to reach out to him when he got traded:

“Who was the first one? I’m not even sure when I found out when I first got traded, I just threw my phone across the room. Who was first? It might have been Max I think Maxey reached out to me first. He reached out and said I know it’s a lot of stuff going on right now but we excited to have you stuff like that. Maxey and V.J was probably around the same time. We talk everyday we got group chats, multiple now and we all trying to figure this thing out. LeBron is active in the group chat so we just all trying to get on the same page.”
Jaylen Brown on LeBron James, Tyrese MaxeyAug 6, 2026x.com
