Best quotes

Al Horford:

“We have the 23rd pick and I know the team will draft the best player available. I would like to pick a shooter like Kentucky's Doron Lamb. But it wouldn't bother me if they picked a big who could help me like Festus Ezeli(Vanderbilt). Anyway, I doubt they will pick Jeff Teague's brother, Marquis Teague.”
Al Horford on Jeff Teague, Doron Lamb, Marquis Teague, Festus EzeliJun 21, 2012Primera Hora

Drew Shiller:Steve Kerr on Festus Ezeli trying to make an NBA comeback:

“I love Festus. I love his zest for life, his desire to play. I sure hope it happens for him. It's such a shame when a guy so young suffers from injuries that keep him from playing.”
Steve Kerr on Festus EzeliJan 30, 2021Twitter @DrewShiller
