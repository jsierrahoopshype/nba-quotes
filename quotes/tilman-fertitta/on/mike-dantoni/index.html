Every quote from Tilman Fertitta about Mike D'Antoni in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

Tilman Fertitta:

“I think I have one of the NBA’s great coaches in Mike D’Antoni. And I think I have one of the best basketball ops groups, Daryl and his whole team. Personally, I love Mike. But I’m going to leave it up to the general manager. I surely would like to have Mike back. If Daryl wants to have Mike back, I’m sure Mike is going to be back.”
TFTilman Fertitta on Mike D'AntoniJun 6, 2020Houston Chronicle

Jonathan Feigen:Tilman Fertitta:

“I think that Mike (D’Antoni) is one of the great offensive minds, but it takes chemistry to win 65 games. Jeff is one of the missing pieces and we’re just glad to have him back. Mike’s thrilled to have him back. Mike is excited.”
TFTilman Fertitta on Mike D'AntoniNov 5, 2018Twitter @Jonathan_Feigen

Fertitta:
