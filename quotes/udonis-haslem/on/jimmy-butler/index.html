What Udonis Haslem has said about Jimmy Butler

Every quote from Udonis Haslem about Jimmy Butler in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

Udonis Haslem on Jimmy Butler:

“Everybody asks me this, about what happened with Jimmy in other places. I don’t know what you want me to say. You put him in the cage with a bunch of cats, he’s gonna growl. You put him in a kennel with a bunch of dogs, he’s gonna be right at home. That’s really all I got to say about that. I don’t know what people want me to say. I don’t understand what they’re asking, about where he was. He fit in right here perfectly.”
Udonis Haslem on Jimmy ButlerJan 24, 2020Five Reasons Sports Network

Haslem said earlier this month.

“The guys want me around. Bam [Adebayo] tells me every day, ‘I don’t know what the hell I’m going to do when you retire.’ Jimmy [Butler] always has me around. We’ll see. My kids are getting older. We’ll see.”
Udonis Haslem on Jimmy Butler, Bam AdebayoFeb 24, 2020Miami Herald
