Best quotes

LeBron James on Jayson Tatum:

“He's 25 years old, and I believe he has been to the conference finals four times and to the NBA finals once. He’s 25. I didn't win my first one until I was 28. I think Nikola Jokic won his first one at 27. I think Michael Jordan was 28 as well… I think we have a lot of expectations on JT, but he's experienced a lot of winning in his career so far. And obviously, everyone wants to see him get over the hump, but four conference finals and a finals appearance before the age of 26 is elite.”
LeBron James on Michael Jordan, Nikola Jokic, Jayson TatumMar 19, 2024YouTube
“It's my first year in the league. I grew up watching LeBron and asking him to follow me back on Twitter, going to his camps. So just to be able to compete against a player like him and be a few shots away from beating him and his team to go to a championship is something I will always remember.”
Jayson Tatum on LeBron JamesMay 28, 2018ESPN

Jayson Tatum:

“Now I don’t hold back with older guys in the league now, like ’LeBron James. ’Bron is somebody that I text and call a lot, and it could be from basketball to how to take care of your body to business decisions off the court. People are willing to give you answers, you just have to ask.”
Jayson Tatum on LeBron JamesJan 26, 2024Andscape
