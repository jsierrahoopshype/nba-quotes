Pippen wrote in a letter to the team posted on the Bulls website Tuesday.

“You're still the best team in the NBA until an opponent proves otherwise. So go out there and play like it. You've earned that much with Derrick along the way, but you also won a lot of games without him. It's time to meet the challenge.”
