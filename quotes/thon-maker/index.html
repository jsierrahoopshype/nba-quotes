Howard Beck:John Hammond on Thon Maker:

“In the long term we hope that ... (at minimum) he's going to at least be a rotation player, and you hope that some day he can be a starter. I don't know if you could ever look and someday see that maybe consistently have some kind of consistent minutes on the floor with Thon and Giannis and Jabari. I think it could be a really interesting group, all three guys that can score and all three guys that have good size and good length. time will tell on all of these things.”
JHJohn Hammond on Thon MakerJun 26, 2016Twitter @HowardBeck

The Greece-born Antetokounmpo is the son of immigrants from Nigeria, while Maker made his way to the NBA from the war zone in South Sudan via Australia, the Carlisle School in Martinsville, Virginia, and Canada.

“Giannis is definitely proud about his Nigerian heritage, with his mother and father being from there and immigrating to Greece, and ... Thon's a guy who's bounced all over the world. Thon's been through a lot, and he came out alive. Looking at these kids and knowing that one of them could be the next Thon Maker or Giannis Antetokounmpo is great,”
Khris Middleton on Giannis Antetokounmpo, Thon MakerAug 3, 2018ESPN

Howard Beck:John Hammond on Thon Maker:

“He is still 7 feet tall, and he has the ability I think almost immediately to step out and stretch the floor. So with those two things I think that would maybe give hi rn an opportunity to get on the floor. I know he's going to do everything he can to put himself in that position. So we'll see if he can do that in the short term.”
JHJohn Hammond on Thon MakerJun 26, 2016Twitter @HowardBeck
