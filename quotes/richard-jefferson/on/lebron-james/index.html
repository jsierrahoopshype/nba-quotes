What Richard Jefferson has said about LeBron James

Best quotes

Richard Jefferson:

“There is no GOAT in basketball because there's multiple levels. Kareem Abdul-Jabbar I think had the greatest career, Bill Russell won the most championships, Michael Jordan is the icon, and I think Kobe Bryant and what he did mentally for the game and really for athletes all around the world. ButI would just say if you were to build a basketball player, you could not build a better basketball player than LeBron James. There's no skill set he doesn't have, there's no physical ability. So for me, if I was building a basketball player, mentally and physically, he would be the person that I would build.”
Richard Jefferson on LeBron James, Kobe Bryant, Michael Jordan, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Bill RussellSep 12, 2026YouTube

Richard Jefferson went on the Bill Simmons podcast at the Ringer and talked about the Finals:

“We lose 4-1, right. We didn’t play great. But KD hits that huge shot. Kyle (Korver) — who is one of the great shooters in the history of basketball — he has a good look, it doesn’t drop. That’s part of it. And then KD hits a tough shot over LeBron on a dribble-up. That close. You win the next game and it’s 2-2. People don’t understand: ‘Oh 4-1, they killed you.’ It’s like dude, it’s literally one shot here, one shot there. If it’s 2-2, you go there it’s Game 5. There’s zero chance — very similar to the year before — we do not lose on our home court in a Game 6 situation,”
Richard Jefferson on LeBron James, Kyle KorverSep 16, 2017NBC Sports
“…You get complacent with excellence, and they’ve seen it so long, they’ve seen him make everyone around him better, they’ve seen him go to the Finals six straight years …LeBron is the beacon of consistency, and it’s like, (do we need) nobody to have a great year in order to look at his numbers — 25, 8 and 8, shooting 50 percent, 40 percent from three?“People have nothing else to talk about so they make stuff up,”
Richard Jefferson on LeBron JamesMar 17, 2017FoxSports.com
