Best quotes

Michael Malone on LeBron James attacking in transition:

“A guy like LeBron, who averages 5 fastbreak points per game by himself - against us that number jumps up to 6.5 - he’s a great passer, he’s a one-man break, he’s a freight train. “So, you have to have pickup points, but there’s not one player in the league who can guard him, so there’s gotta be a crowd around. What we call it, Ryan, is we gotta stop and LOAD to the ball. He’s gotta see bodies. “And then, that’s the hard part because when you put bodies on him, he’s a great passer.”
MMMichael Malone on LeBron JamesApr 18, 2024Twitter @NBABlackburn

Michael Malone on LeBron James:

“It's year 21, and the guy is getting better... I hope he gets tired. I hope he wears down. But I've seen no signs of LeBron James slowing down by any means.”
MMMichael Malone on LeBron JamesApr 18, 2024Twitter @HarrisonWind

Malone said Tuesday in a Zoom call with reporters.

“There are not many Michael Jordans out there. I coached LeBron James for five years and have a great relationship with him. LeBron did not have the same mindset or killer mentality that Michael Jordan is supposed to have had. But LeBron James is arguably the greatest of all time as well.”
MMMichael Malone on LeBron James, Michael JordanApr 22, 2020USA Today Sports
