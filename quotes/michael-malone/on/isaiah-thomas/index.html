What Michael Malone has said about Isaiah Thomas

Every quote from Michael Malone about Isaiah Thomas in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

11 quotes·2013 to 2019·Latest quote Mar 18, 2019

Best quotes

Michael Malone said about the player-coach bond.

“Isaiah and I remain close. Love him as a person and as a player. A guy I did not want to lose while I was in (Sacramento), in our second season. He's got a chip on his shoulder. Last pick in the draft, he has that keep on doubting me attitude. He loves being in the gym, he's a guy you love being around, and that's why I think he's worthy of an All-Star reserve spot this year, which I hope he gets. He's worked very hard, you look at his numbers and he's a guy who deserves to be on that team.”
MMMichael Malone on Isaiah ThomasJan 27, 2016Booth Newspapers
“For us, I wanted him to be the best Isaiah Thomas possible. Be that scorer, 20 points a night (which is what he averaged for Malone's Kings during the 2013-14 season). But also look at ways to make your teammates better. I think that's the thing he's probably gotten better at along the way. He can impact the game with scoring, we all know that. But I think first three quarters of the game he'll look to get guys involved. Then the fourth quarter he's going to try to cut your throat out and kill you.”
MMMichael Malone on Isaiah ThomasMar 10, 2017Booth Newspapers

Adam Himmelsbach:Mike Malone:
