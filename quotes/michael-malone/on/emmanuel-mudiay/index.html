Nuggets coach Michael Malone said.

Chris Dempsey:#Nuggets coach Michael Malone on Emmanuel Mudiay:

“Just trying to find way to get a win. Winning is a great cure-all for anybody, like it was for (the Clippers) tonight, coming in having lost three in a row. By no means is there an issue with Emmanuel or anybody else on this team. We are together, we are unified and we're going to continue to fight to stay together to get this thing turned around.”
