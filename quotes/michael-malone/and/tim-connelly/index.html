Michael Malone on Tim Connelly:

“I wouldn't have this job without Tim Connelly. Tim Connelly after my brief tenure in Sacramento and our time working together in New Orleans for many years, saw something in me, where he believed in me to tell Josh Kroenke I was the right guy for the job.”
