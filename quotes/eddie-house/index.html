Kendrick Perkins:

“Was I the only 2008 member missing from the duck boat? No, I wasn’t. (Eddie) House, my guy, he was there. Leon Powe, great friend of mine as well, he was there. Scal was there and Paul (Pierce) was there. There was still 11 other guys missing. And to be honest with you, if I would’ve gotten an invite, which I didn’t — I didn’t know we was supposed to — but anyways, nine times out of 10, I would’ve turned it down.… That’s not our moment. That’s 2024′s moment. Let those guys have their moment.”
Kendrick Perkins on Paul Pierce, Eddie House, Leon PoweJun 25, 2024Boston Globe

Doc Rivers on Tony Allen, Eddie House, Glen Davis, Leon PoweDec 18, 2022Sixers Wire
