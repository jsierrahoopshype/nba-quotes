What NBA players and coaches have said about Kentavious Caldwell-Pope

13 quotes about Kentavious Caldwell-Pope from 13 players, coaches and executives, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

13 quotes, 9 of them from players·2014 to 2026·Latest quote Oct 6, 2026

Best quotes

Luke Walton:

“A lot of it is what they’ve [Kentavious Caldwell-Pope and Corey Brewer] brought to the table those two. Being a hard-nose, being able to make the right read and switch and get after people, be the aggressors on that end of it, and they play like that in practice all the time too. I thought our young guys see what that looks like and they get a feel of what that’s like playing against each day in practice. Obviously there’s a lot of other reasons too, but that’s a big part of what’s helped change the way that we’re playing defense.”
Luke Walton on Corey Brewer, Kentavious Caldwell-PopeNov 21, 2017Lakers Nation

Jaylen Brown:

“I have played against Kentavious Caldwell-Pope for a long time and every single one he has been a dog. Guards, takes the tough matchups, guards the best player. He has guarded me a lot of times. His shots when he is open and also just is a great connector for your team, so I am looking forward to being on the same side of him this year and we look forward to his leadership.”
Jaylen Brown on Kentavious Caldwell-PopeOct 6, 2026YouTube

LeBron James:

“I think more importantly than what I need to share with the younger guys, I think what a lot of people are missing is how hungry for knowledge that the young guys are. Kuz and Zo and Josh Hart, and Brandon Ingram, and Kentavious Caldwell-Pope, Slavi (LeBron definitely meant Svi Mykhailluk but this is hilarious) and Moe Wagner, we have young guys that are extremely excited not only about playing the game, but also learning the game. That’s going to help out even more because those guys are just hungry for knowledge, so I’m looking forward to that.”
LeBron James on Kentavious Caldwell-Pope, Brandon Ingram, Josh HartSep 18, 2018lebronwire.usatoday.com
