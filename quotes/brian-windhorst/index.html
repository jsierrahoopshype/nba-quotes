Magic Johnson:

“First, Brian [Windhorst] got it wrong. This is all about making sure that they can say something on ESPN and everybody can just talk. It can’t be because you want to pass it around, you get into your pick-and-roll plays. But hey, we’re the Lakers. People are going to be talking about us, but that’s not how it’s going right now.”
