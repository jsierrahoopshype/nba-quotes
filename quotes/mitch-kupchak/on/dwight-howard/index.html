What Mitch Kupchak has said about Dwight Howard

Every quote from Mitch Kupchak about Dwight Howard in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

5 quotes·2013 to 2018·Latest quote Jul 6, 2018

Best quotes

Kupchak:

“A player a has a right to be a free agent. It bothers me that there seems to be some media attention and criticism early in the process about Dwight and the coach and Dwight and free agency. Don’t get me wrong. The sooner he makes his mind up, the better for everybody. That’s my take on it. But he can’t sign until July 10 anyway. I told him when he first got here, ‘I’m not going to bug you. I believe this is the place for you. When you make your mind up, let me know.’”
MKMitch Kupchak on Dwight HowardMay 23, 2013Los Angeles Daily News

Asked about his remaining options to salvage this season, Kupchak said,

“We will not make a trade. We will not trade Dwight Howard. We have no intention of making a trade. It's unlikely that we'll make any trade with any of our principal players. To make another change at this time of the year being behind the eight-ball like we are, I think that would just make it more difficult. The talent is there. We have to find our way.”
MKMitch Kupchak on Dwight HowardFeb 4, 2013Newsday

(Con't)Kupchak said

“I haven't talked to Danny Ainge in weeks. I made the statement a week or 2 ago that we're not going to trade Dwight Howard and that hasn't changed.”
MKMitch Kupchak on Dwight HowardFeb 15, 2013Twitter @Chris_Broussard
