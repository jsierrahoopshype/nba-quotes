Best quotes

Anthony Edwards on the job Karl-Anthony Towns did guarding Nikola Jokic:

“I think the main thing today was his ass stayed out of foul trouble, like I told him in Phoenix. That was all that was. Yeah, I mean I cussed him out. I cuss him out every chance I get. Bro, stop fucking fouling. I think you guys see it, if KAT don’t foul we pretty much can win the game every time. Tonight he had three fouls, but he only had three, he didn’t have five. I told him today, ‘We thankful that you didn’t foul because if you foul we lose, because you the best matchup we’ve got for Jokic, like you do the best job on him. I mean we’re coming to double but you do the best job standing up to his first dribble.’ He did that tonight.”
Anthony Edwards on Nikola Jokic, Karl-Anthony TownsMay 17, 2024Twitter @DaneMooreNBA

Anthony Edwards on Karl-Anthony Towns' swag:

“KAT last year didn’t say a word on the court, now he talking crazy to people. It’s because he got swag. He got swag. He kill the drip coming into the game, showing off the watch. Yeah, he’s swaggy. For sure. Love dat. Love KAT.”
Anthony Edwards on Karl-Anthony TownsMar 17, 2022Twitter @DaneMooreNBA

Anthony Edwards on the impact of Karl-Anthony Towns in Game 1:

“He played the game clean. He played it the right way. He defended very well, and he did everything we needed him to do. He rebounded. He spread the floor. He got to the corner. He wasn’t cutting when he didn’t need to. He did everything tonight, and like I said, to me, he’s still the reason we won. First half, I was nonexistent. He carried us on his back, so, yeah, thank you KAT.”
Anthony Edwards on Karl-Anthony TownsApr 21, 2024Twitter @DaneMooreNBA
