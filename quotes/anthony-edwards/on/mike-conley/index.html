Anthony Edwards on Mike Conley:

“Mike Conley was an All-Star in the NBA. I think people forget about that. Once upon a time, he was one of the best point guards in the league. I always tell him that you used to be one of my favorite players. When I played 2K, I played with you.”
Anthony Edwards on Mike ConleyMay 5, 2026Bluesky

Anthony Edwards on the late bank shot:

“I told Mike [Conley] when they made the [previous] shot to just give me the ball and move out of the way, because I work on it every day. I knew once I got to that spot, I was going glass.”

Anthony Edwards on what Mike Conley said to him late in the game:

“Mike just tells me, ‘No more 3s, man. Get inside the three-point line. It ain’t falling.’ He pretty much just told me to get to the line. ... My middy started to fall late. That helped me a lot.”
