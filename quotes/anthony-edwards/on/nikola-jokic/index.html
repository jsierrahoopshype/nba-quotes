Anthony Edwards on the job Karl-Anthony Towns did guarding Nikola Jokic:

“I think the main thing today was his ass stayed out of foul trouble, like I told him in Phoenix. That was all that was. Yeah, I mean I cussed him out. I cuss him out every chance I get. Bro, stop fucking fouling. I think you guys see it, if KAT don’t foul we pretty much can win the game every time. Tonight he had three fouls, but he only had three, he didn’t have five. I told him today, ‘We thankful that you didn’t foul because if you foul we lose, because you the best matchup we’ve got for Jokic, like you do the best job on him. I mean we’re coming to double but you do the best job standing up to his first dribble.’ He did that tonight.”
Anthony Edwards on Nikola Jokic, Karl-Anthony TownsMay 17, 2024Twitter @DaneMooreNBA

Anthony Edwards:

“That might’ve been the best game of my life I’ve been a part of. Nikola Jokic, bruh, oh my God,he might be the best ... player I’ve ever seen closeup. Besides myself to myself. He’s incredible. The MVP race is tough, man. I don’t know. He had 60. It's crazy.”
Anthony Edwards on Nikola JokicApr 2, 2025Twitter @ChristopherHine

Ben Golliver:Timberwolves’ Anthony Edwards on Nuggets’ Nikola Jokic’s Game 5:

“I just laugh. That’s all I can do. I can’t be mad. … He’s the MVP. He’s the best player in the NBA. He showed it the last three games. … He was special tonight. I have to give him his flowers. He was that guy.”
Anthony Edwards on Nikola JokicMay 15, 2024Twitter @BenGolliver
