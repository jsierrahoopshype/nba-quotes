Michael Scotto:Anthony Edwards on Timberwolves coach Chris Finch:

“He's just a great coach, and he don't sugarcoat anything with anybody. If KAT's f****** up he's gonna get on KAT. If I'm f****** up, he's gonna get on me. If Rudy f****** up, he’s gonna get on anybody messing up throughout the game. Ithink that’s what makes him the best coach in the NBA to me because no matter who it is or how high the player is on the pole, he’s going to get on you from start to finish. It starts with the head of the snake, and he’s the head of our snake. We all listen to him, look up to him, and he does a great job of making sure we’re ready to go every night.”
Anthony Edwards on Chris FinchMay 20, 2024Twitter @MikeAScotto
