Nonetheless, Devin Booker knows that it's the Suns who had the last laugh in their demolition of the Kyrie Irving-less Mavericks, with Booker even going as far as to say that Williams only had one mission entering the game, and that mission wasn't to play basketball.

“Hell yeah, it's not necessary. I don't think Grant Williams wanted to play basketball tonight. He wants to do the antics, man. We're out there trying to hoop. So big props to Nurk for holding that down. If I would've seen that, I would do the same thing. That's how we get down. We're not with the nonsense,”

“The one person that talks trash to me always, for some reason, is Devin Booker.
