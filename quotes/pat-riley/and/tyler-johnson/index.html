Tyler Johnson:

“I remember [Pat] Riley telling us after the trade deadline he wanted to keep the team together just to give us a chance. He wanted to give us the opportunity to compete and try to make the playoffs. Obviously we fell short of that goal. But with the way that team was headed and the way we bonded and the way things have been going this summer, all I was hoping for during free agency was that we brought back the guys who were in the trenches last year.”
Tyler Johnson on Pat RileyAug 1, 2017Palm Beach Post

2016-17

1 quote
“But at the same time, Coach's attitude and my attitude is we want to win. And we don't know what we really have. But we love Hassan (Whiteside), we love Josh (Richardson) and Justise (Winslow) and Tyler (Johnson) and a lot of the other young players that we have, so I think from that standpoint, it's a wait and let's see what happens. And then we'll go from there. We're rebuilding now. We're not tweaking, like we did with the Big Three team. Or retooling, like we had to do right after LeBron left. We're rebuilding, but we're rebuilding to win now.”
Pat Riley on Hassan Whiteside, Josh Richardson, Justise Winslow, Tyler JohnsonSep 26, 2016CBSSports.com
