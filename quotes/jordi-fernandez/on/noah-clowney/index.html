Jordi Fernandez on areas the Nets are looking for Noah Clowney to improve next season:

“His physicality, especially on the defensive end... His drive decisions. If he's not going to shoot it... how can he get to the rim with one or two dribbles and be efficient? Whether it's an easy drive and kick, or finish at the rim. I think in the last few games, I know the sample size was small, but he's been working on it. And that's what he's going to focus on throughout the summer. Once he's on the court and plays minutes consistently, I have no doubt that he's going to continue to get better. So we believe that he's part of our future, and he's had a really good season. I know that with the injuries, it's been a little up and down... but we're very, very happy with him.”
