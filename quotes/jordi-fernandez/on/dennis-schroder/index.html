Jordi Fernandez on what he's seen from Dennis Schroder as a leader:

“That’s who he is... He does it in his own way, and he’ll let you know it, and I like it... It’s important because when we go back to: Who do we want to be and how do we want to do it? How do we represent the Brooklyn grit and The Brooklyn Way? Dennis can definitely help us with that part with his toughness, with the way he competes... All his teammates see it, so that’s part of leadership. Walking the walk instead of talking the talk.”
JFJordi Fernandez on Dennis SchroderOct 27, 2024Twitter @erikslater_

Jordi Fernandez on the Nets’ starting point guard battle between Dennis Schroder and Ben Simmons:

“I want to be put in a tough spot. These are two really good players. They both want to start, and that's what I want. I want to create healthy competition within the group, and then from there, make me decide... If they don't meet expectations, they won't be the starting point guard.”
