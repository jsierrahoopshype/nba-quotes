Knicks GM Scott Perry and Steve Mills were asked if they felt as if Kristaps Porzingis can be a draw for free agents in future summers:

“I think Kristaps is going to be an excellent player in this league. I think he's gaining the respect of many of his peers around this league so we're excited about that,”
SPScott Perry on Kristaps PorzingisJul 11, 2018ESPN

Scott Perry on Porzingis’ durability:

“With the success he has, there’s going to be some failures, too, and how he responds to that adversity ultimately will determine how impactful he’ll be down the line. “He bounces back pretty quick. If he has a setback, a tough game, an injured ankle or elbow, he has the drive to get back and compete.”
SPScott Perry on Kristaps PorzingisDec 10, 2017New York Post

Scott Perry on if it's a risk letting Porzingis become free agent:

“I’m not going to get into predicting anything about the future. I would just reiterate that I think as a group and we have a shared goal of making the Knicks a very good basketball team going into the long term.”
