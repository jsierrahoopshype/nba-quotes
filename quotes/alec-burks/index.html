Best quotes

“He’s one of those players that over the years Spo and Pat have always said, ‘Hey, we like Alec Burks,’ and just never could find something that works. It was always in my group of players in that list that came through. So it’s been for a number of years having that conversation. So when the opportunity came this year that he was willing to come to us and willing to come to us at a minimum, we could finally get Alec Burks here.”
AEAndy Elisburg on Alec BurksJul 15, 2024South Florida Sun-Sentinel

Williams said after practice on Tuesday.

“You have to have some really good vets who know how to play. They settle us down a little bit, their voice is good on the floor, they know how to space, they know how to make the right plays. I told (Burks) today he got it going a little bit. I told him that’s who you have to be. You have to be yourself. He’s been defending and competing, and that’s something that we value.”
Monty Williams on Alec BurksOct 11, 2023Detroit Free Press

Here's the full quote from head coach Snyder on where Burks' health currently stands:

“He's making really good progress. We're doing the same thing with Alec that we did with Dante. I think Dennis had a really good way of putting it, 'We're going to focus on the next 12 years over the next 12 months.' It's true with Alec. We just want to get him where he's comfortable and confident, and now's the right time to do that. He's going through everything that we're doing except he's not involved in contact stuff. I feel like he's getting more comfortable. And it's going to be the same thing where he's going to have to dust it off and get his timing back and get his habits back. But really everybody's in the same place with the habits.”
Quin Snyder on Alec BurksSep 28, 2016KSL.com
