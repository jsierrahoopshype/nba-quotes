Best quotes

Michael Malone:

“We were a young team last year and we got a little bit younger through the draft which we’re excited about. It’s tough to win big in the NBA when you’re this young, but we feel we have a lot of good young players and assets. And you balance that with guys like (Danilo Gallinari) and (Kenneth) Faried, Darrell Arthur. People forget but we consider Wilson Chandler a free agent signing (since he missed all of last season).This is a long-term play. This is an opportunity to show improvement, for our players to get better and we have a chance to be here for a while.”
MMMichael Malone on Wilson Chandler, Danilo Gallinari, Kenneth Faried, Darrell ArthurAug 15, 2016BSN Denver

Bogut told reporters Saturday.

“I'm just playing physical like they are. If someone steps up and wants to get into it, I've got no problem with that. It’s nothing personal. They’re physical. Kenneth Faried runs around and hits people. We just have to match their intensity. If it gets personal during games and guys want to have a crack at you, that’s playoff basketball. I’m not sitting here saying I hate McGee or I hate Faried. But if they want to be physical, we’re going to be physical back. We’re not going to take a step backward.”
Andrew Bogut on Javale McGee, Kenneth FariedApr 27, 2013NBA.com

“The expectation level with Kenneth was never as a go-to guy. He was never a guy we expected to be able to throw the ball into and say: 'Go get us 20 points every night.'... It's probably unfair for people to expect that,”

3 quotes

Austin Rivers on Kenneth Faried:

“I don't know how he has gotten to this point (bouncing around the league)... Because in today's modern day (NBA), a big is not the big of like 10 years ago. They just want bigs to just rebound, roll, pick... That's what he does well.”
Austin Rivers on Kenneth FariedJan 26, 2019Twitter @SalmanAliNBA

Mike D'Antoni on Kenneth Faried's energy:

“That's kind of his trademark. He gets you excited to play.... Diving on the ball for 50-50 balls, hard rebounds, running the floor... That to me was the most satisfying thing.”
Mike D'Antoni on Kenneth FariedJan 26, 2019Twitter

James Harden on persevering:

“A rough stretch. Chris goes out. We get Austin luckily. Clint goes out. We get Kenneth luckily. Lose D-House. As long as we can keep our head above water until these guys get back, ... we’re good. We know as a healthy unit, we can beat anybody.”
James Harden on Austin Rivers, Kenneth Faried, Danuel HouseJan 22, 2019Twitter @Jonathan_Feigen

2017-18

3 quotes

#Nuggets coachMichael Malone on why Kenneth Faried is not currently part of the rotation:

“Our job is to win games, and we’re winning games right now... Why would I play him over Nikola (Jokic) or Trey Lyles with how well they’re playing right now?”
MMMichael Malone on Nikola Jokic, Trey Lyles, Kenneth FariedDec 27, 2017Twitter @ginamizell

Richard Jefferson:

“Next man up. We have 19 power forwards on our team. You start Kenneth Faried who’s been on a USA team. He’s been on the verge of being an Olympian and he’s in the prime of his career. Not many teams can take out a four-time All-Star and add a guy like Kenneth Faried who on more than half the teams in this league would probably be starting.”
Richard Jefferson on Kenneth FariedNov 30, 2017BSN Denver
“He’s been a starter in the NBA. Mason Plumlee’s been a starter on a playoff team, as well … if what’s best for our team is to bring Kenneth off the bench,right now, hey, go out there and play to the best to your ability.”
MMMichael Malone on Kenneth Faried, Mason PlumleeSep 26, 2017Denver Post

2013-14

1 quote

Tim Connelly:

“I tell guys all the time, ‘We want to pay you. Give us a reason to pay you.’ Certainly Kenneth’s energy and toughness is something our team feeds off of. It embodies who we’re trying to be. We’re in a great spot. He has another year on his rookie-scale contract. We’ll try to come to some agreement this offseason. Hopefully we do. If not, he’s still on our team and we have restricted rights the following year. It’s certainly a good spot to be in. We have to be an organization – and we have been – that will reward good play. And Kenneth’s played well – really well.”
TCTim Connelly on Kenneth FariedMay 9, 2014NBA.com
