Mike Conley on Mitchell/Gobert relationship:

“Between Donovan & Rudy, if they feel a certain way about each other, which I honestly don't think that's the case at all, they're grown men and they handle it. They go out there, compete and try to win. You'd never notice on our end.”
Mike Conley on Donovan Mitchell, Rudy GobertApr 17, 2020Twitter @andyblarsen

And Conley on how they will have to guard Nikola Jokic if there is no Gobert:

“By committee. We got to throw a lot of bodies at him. We got to show him a lot of different looks. Can’t just give him one read. He’s so smart, has the ability to pick you apart if you show him the same thing multiple times. It’ll be a lot of junk. A lot of different things we have to implement. Something we’ve done throughout the year on different teams. We’ll have to implement that tonight.”
Mike Conley on Rudy Gobert, Nikola JokicMay 6, 2024Twitter @DaneMooreNBA

Jon Krawczynski:Conley on missing Rudy Gobert:

“We were a step slow because we’re used to having him pull us out of there and say hey you go to the top and I’ll take the paint. Just habits that are tough to break in one game.”
Mike Conley on Rudy GobertMar 11, 2024Twitter @JonKrawczynski
