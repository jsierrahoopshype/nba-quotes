Best quotes

Rafael Stone on Amen Thompson:

“I think he's a very, very good basketball player. Since about halfway through his rookie year, we've been a materially better team when he's on the court. And he keeps getting better. I thought he had a really good year last year. He just got better and better and better and better. And he helps our team on the court in literally every way you can. We turn the ball over less, we turn the other team over more, we rebound better, we shoot a higher percentage, we shoot free throws better. Everything's better with Amen Thompson. So yeah, we're very, very high on him.”
RSRafael Stone on Free throws, Amen ThompsonOct 9, 2026YouTube

Draymond Green on Joe Johnson comments about Amen Thompson getting paid $200 million with no jump shot:

“I do think number one, you get paid for what you’ve done. I think there’s always this idea of you get paid for what you’re going to become. No, no. In this league you get paid for what you you’ve done. Amen Thompson has put together a body of work that says if you want to keep me, this is going to be the going rate. Now, yes, he needs to improve. But on the flip side, I do get a little frustrated sometimes when people come out like, “Yo, this guy can just gain a jump shot.”
Draymond Green on Amen Thompson, Joe JohnsonSep 9, 2026x.com
