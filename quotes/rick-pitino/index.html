Best quotes

Jermaine O'Neal:

“Rick Pitino was my guy. Shout out to Rick Pitino, man. He recruited me himself. Sometimes it’s just the assistant coaches—but he came every time to the house. He sat in my house and told my mother: 'Look, I’ve seen everything your son went through. I’ve got relationships in the NBA. They say he’s projected between 10th and 17th.' Then he said:'We’re going to be good with you—and we’ll be good without you. We’d love to have you. But knowing what I know: Go pro.'”
JOJermaine O'Neal on Rick PitinoMay 30, 2025YouTube

Speaking on the Str8 to Da League podcast, JermaineO'Neal shared that Kentucky was his top choice coming out of high school and that Pitino had heavily recruited him, making multiple in-home visits with O'Neal and his family.

“I was going to go to Kentucky. Shout out to Rick Pitino, man. Like he recruited me, and this was like the realest thing, man. My mother loved him, man. She loved him,”
JOJermaine O'Neal on Rick PitinoJul 13, 2026Yahoo! Sports

Terry Rozier on Rick Pitino:

“I’m not surprised. He’s put in his time, he’s had fun with it. He’s done the things he needed to do. He’ll always be a legend in my book and he can always coach me if he don’t want to coach any more. I’m happy for him in his retirement.”
Terry Rozier on Rick PitinoSep 6, 2018Twitter @JaredWeissNBA
