What Bojan Bogdanovic and Donovan Mitchell have said about each other

Bojan Bogdanovic on Donovan Mitchell, and Donovan Mitchell on Bojan Bogdanovic: every quote in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

Bogdanovic:

“Before Victor got hurt, everybody considered me a shooter but I knew what I was capable of doing. I can play pick-and-roll, iso. Here with Donovan and Mike, two great offensive players, I will just try to fit right in with them. Having Donovan and Mike playing with the ball and creating off the dribble is going to be easy for all of us to be on the perimeter. There are a lot of combinations for us. It’s going to be an exciting year.”
Bojan Bogdanovic on Donovan MitchellJul 11, 2019NBA.com

Bojan Bogdanovic, on Donovan Mitchell's growth:

“It’s just amazing how mature he’s become this season. He was really reading the game. … We were missing great shots, and then in the third quarter, he took the game over.”
Bojan Bogdanovic on Donovan MitchellJun 9, 2021Twitter @tribjazz

Donovan, on Bogdanovic's defense:

“When you got a guy like him making that commitment that early in the game — he didn't tell us he was going to do it, he just did it — you don't want to let him down. That intensity sparked us. … We fed off that.”
Donovan Mitchell on Bojan BogdanovicApr 23, 2022Twitter @tribjazz
