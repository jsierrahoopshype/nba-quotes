Tim Connelly:

“Donte (DiVincenzo) looks great. As you guys all know, Donte attacks everything like he attacks the court. I thought our medical staff and his agents did a great job of quickly getting it addressed. He was under the knife 24 hours after the surgery. He looks as good as we'd hoped. We don't want to put any added pressure on him, butwe're cautious and optimistic that we're going to see Donte this season and we know how much he brings to our team.”
TCTim Connelly on Donte DiVincenzoSep 30, 2026YouTube

Curry said at the American Century Championship.

“I know he [Donte DiVincenzo] was coming off of an injury and trying to find a situation that could help him establish who he is as NBA player and how much value he brings to winning teams. “And he proved that above and beyond with us all year. He knows how to play the game. You can tell he won at the highest level in college. The Knicks got a good one and I’m happy that he got his fair share of that CBA, too. So it was good.”
Stephen Curry on Donte DiVincenzoJul 13, 2023New York Daily News
