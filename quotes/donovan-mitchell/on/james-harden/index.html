Best quotes

Donovan Mitchell on James Harden:

“I just truly believe the only reason why he’s not talked about in every room is because of championship conversation. But I’ve always known it being around him, but when you’re his teammate, I think it grew to a whole different level. Seeing him in camp, seeing his influence on the group, seeing his conversation, seeing his passion behind winning, and passion behind intention and trying to be the best of what he does, really puts in perspective how BS a lot of narratives are, and how you really don’t truly understand something until you see it from the other side.”
Donovan Mitchell on James HardenOct 7, 2026YouTube

Oh No He Didn't: Donovan Mitchell on James Harden:

“I'm appreciative of him because he helped me get somewhere that I've never been before. We live in a world where it's just so much easier to knock and hate. To see it firsthand, the work ethic, the passion, the IQ, the leadership, it's just like man just gets a bad rep”
Donovan Mitchell on James HardenMay 29, 2026x.com

#CavsDonovan Mitchell on James Harden:

“I hope he is back. He helped myself and this group get somewhere we have never been”
Donovan Mitchell on James HardenMay 25, 2026x.com
