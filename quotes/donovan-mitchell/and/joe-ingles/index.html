Mitchell told The Athletic over the weekend.

Joe Ingles, on the Donovan Mitchell rumors:

“I don't think Donovan wants to leave. We see him every day, and he loves where he's at, he loves our team. If they were … around us every day and heard him say he wants to leave, that's completely different. But they're not.”
Joe Ingles on Donovan MitchellJan 28, 2022Twitter @tribjazz
