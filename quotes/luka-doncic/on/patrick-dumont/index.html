Nico Harrison said he was not told by #Mavs governor Patrick Dumont that he had to trade Luka Doncic:

“Not at all. Patrick reminds me of the leadership that I had at Nike and a really good leader doesn't tell the people that work for him what to do. It's a collective, well thought out process to make a big move like that. Also, unfortunately, I'm super stubborn so someone telling me to do something doesn't work too well for me.”
Luka Doncic on Patrick DumontApr 21, 2025Twitter @MikeACurtis2

Asked Nico Harrison about his conversation with #Mavs governor Patrick Dumont when he introduced the idea of trading Luka Doncic:

“The first time? He laughed at me. No, I mean Patrick, he's the owner, obviously he's the ultimate decision maker, but he entrusts in J-Kidd & I to lead this team and he's putting the trust into us. And then obviously you gotta get judged on the performance that you've done as a leader. And at some point, if it doesn't work out, then I'll be judged for that.”
Luka Doncic on Patrick DumontFeb 2, 2025x.com
