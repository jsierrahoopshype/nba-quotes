Best quotes

Gilbert Arenas:

“Javaris got mad and threw one of the guns. That’s what scared everyone. When the gun flew, the trainer freaked out. Even after all that, me and him were sitting in the jacuzzi, talking like, ‘Man, you play too much.’ He was mad over $1,100, but he said it wasn’t about the money—itwas the principle. He told me, ‘You didn’t have to get in the game and mess it up.’ And I said, ‘Hey, you messed it up too, bringing out those two big guns with no bullets.’”
Gilbert Arenas on Javaris CrittentonDec 27, 2024YouTube
“The plane lands and now Javaris says to JaVale, ‘So you just gonna let me lose my money like that? You ain’t even gonna be a real n—- and give me a chance to get my money back? Aw hell naw, this is the type of shit that gets you fucked up in these streets.’ Iwas like, ‘Javaris, I will burn your car, while you’re in it. Gilbert Arenas:

“How many guns did I bring? Four. I had the gold Desert Eagle, and then I had a Smith & Wesson .500. Only Shaq could shoot that one. I knew Javaris wasn’t going to pick it up. It was a revolver—a Dirty Harry gun. There’s no way in hell he was going to pick that one up. That thing would hurt your arm. My thought was simple: They don’t sell bullets in D.C., and he lived in Virginia. The only gun store is in Maryland. Was he really going to go through all that just to get bullets? It was an empty threat anyway.”
