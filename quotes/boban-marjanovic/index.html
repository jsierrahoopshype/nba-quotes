Mark Cuban:

“Well, I mean, it just depends on what they’re good at. If you have a specific unique skill that adds value to our team, then there’s a chance we’re going to be interested in you. Now you may not play 30 minutes a game you may be situation might be like Boban [Marjanovic] is very situational, right? You can’t play him every game, but when he plays it because the matchup is good, and he can be impactful.”
MCMark Cuban on Boban MarjanovicApr 24, 2021Dallas Morning News
“Especially because they basically tease each other most of the time. They go back and forth. We were just laughing all night. “I even told (Marjanović), ‘Bobi, I know what you’re gonna do after the career. You’re gonna work on TV, for sure.’”
Goran Dragic on Boban MarjanovicSep 11, 2020The Athletic

Sasa Djordjevic on Milos Teodosic, Boban Marjanovic and Nikola Jokic not playing for Serbia:

“As the windows pile up, the less of us there are. I was optimistic in front of this window, when I put up the preliminary list, hoping the big names will be here, that they’ll receive permission from their clubs, that they won’t have any problems. But, unfortunately, some cancelled, some had to listen to their club’s opinion.”
SDSasha Djordjevic on Milos Teodosic, Boban Marjanovic, Nikola JokicSep 2, 2018TalkBasket
