Kwame Brown:

“Stackhouse one day told (Michael Jordan), I didn't come here to be your Scottie Pippen. He thought that he was going to be the number one option and then MJ would be backing him up, but he found out very quickly that that wasn't the case and MJ was the number one option and Stack had to get in where he fit in.”
KBKwame Brown on Michael Jordan, Scottie PippenSep 21, 2026YouTube

Kwame Brown:

“I think Kobe worked a little bit harder on defense, and he was obsessing over Michael Jordan a little bit. I remember he would practice carrying the little 45-pound dumbbells all around the court because his hands weren't as big as Michael Jordan. I think that is the only thing that set them apart. Michael Jordan's hands are like mitts. Kobe's hands are regular. So he had to work on his hand strength, so he would walk around the court with these 45-pound dumbbells on each hand, and every morning you see him doing that just to make sure he had his hand strength together. I am like, damn, let me try that too. He would pick up different traits. I never seen somebody do that.”
KBKwame Brown on Michael JordanSep 19, 2026YouTube
