When a reporter asked if he saw any of himself in Oklahoma City guard Russell Westbrook's sometimes reckless style of play,Bryant nodded and said,

“Not only reckless but ultra-aggressive. Russell's one of those types that either you're going to help him win or he's going to do it without you. People can criticize all they want, but s---, it worked for me. I got five of them [championship rings].”
