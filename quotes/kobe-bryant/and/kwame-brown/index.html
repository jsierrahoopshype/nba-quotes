“He was terrified of girls after that situation. And he is Kobe Bryant, so he can't even get close to a female because if they do, the tabloids can make it into something that is not. I am the bus, but I can talk to all these girls and have a good time. And I am just sitting there chopping it up with him and I am just listening to his life and I am like, man, that is terrible.”
