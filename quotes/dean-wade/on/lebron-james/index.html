Dean Wade on the high basketball IQ of new 76ers teammates:

“It is completely different. You know their tendencies playing against them, but when you are on the same team, you can pick their brain. My biggest thing is the way they think the game is the most impressive thing. Tyrese Maxey, Joel Embiid, LeBron James, Jaylen Brown, everyone, the way they think the game is at such a high level. As an NBA player, you think you know the game well, and then you get on a team like this and it is night and day. You see the way they think about plays, think about defenses, the way they think about rotations. It is similar to some things I thought, but they go a little more in-depth and it is super impressive.”
Dean Wade on LeBron James, Joel Embiid, Jaylen Brown, Tyrese MaxeyOct 1, 2026YouTube
