Naz Reid on Brandon Miller:

“His patience. His consistency. I think his leadership is starting to kinda form, and that’s big in a younger guy. I was with Ant [Edwards], who I started to see his shift and his change of going from his third year to his fourth year, and I think Brandon can do the same. I think I’m coming here to help out. This is his team, him and Kon [Knueppel], and I’m here to help in a big way. But I think the growth for him would be to, like I said, lead on and off the court and just be consistent and do the things that you preach.”
Naz Reid on Brandon Miller, Anthony EdwardsOct 5, 2026TalkBasket
