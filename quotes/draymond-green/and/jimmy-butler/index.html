Draymond Green:

“The beef that you never knew you needed in your life that's not a beef by the way. It's not a beef at all actually, because one of these guys would never beef with anyone. The other one would beef with someone, but there's absolutely no reason for them to beef with each other. And that is the beef that is not a beef at all, Jimmy Butler versus Malevy Leons. Malevy, there's, if you know anything about Malevy, number one is he is the greatest person you could ever meet in your life. He walks in with a smile every day. He is a hard-ass worker, absolutely incredible person. However, if you also know Malevy, you know he raw dogs everything.”
Draymond Green on Jimmy Butler, Malevy LeonsOct 4, 2026YouTube

Draymond Green:

“I've also seen that Mike Dunleavy and Joe Lacob have gotten a lot of criticism for their lack of risk taking over the last few years, playing it for the post-Steph era. And listen, there's always going to be backlash when you're not winning. It happens to everyone. That's just what comes with the territory. But I don't think going to trade for Jimmy Butler would be considered lack of taking risks, especially signing him to a two-year $121 million extension. I don't think that would be considered a lack of taking risk. I also don't believe that trading for Dennis Schroeder, trying to make something happen, is a lack of taking risk.”
Draymond Green on Mike Dunleavy, Joe Lacob, Jimmy Butler, Dennis SchroederJul 28, 2026YouTube

Butler told reporters when asked what he told Green following the block.

“Like I always say, I respect Draymond so much. I definitely respect him because he took 14 [3-pointers] tonight. I love that. He'll do anything to make sure this squad gets a dub. Whatever you ask him to do, he's willing to do that. So, I'm glad I can follow suit in that sometimes.”
Jimmy Butler on Draymond GreenMar 21, 2025NBC Sports Bay Area
