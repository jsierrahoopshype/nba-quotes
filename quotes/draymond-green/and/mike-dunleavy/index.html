Best quotes

Draymond Green:

“I've also seen that Mike Dunleavy and Joe Lacob have gotten a lot of criticism for their lack of risk taking over the last few years, playing it for the post-Steph era. And listen, there's always going to be backlash when you're not winning. It happens to everyone. That's just what comes with the territory. But I don't think going to trade for Jimmy Butler would be considered lack of taking risks, especially signing him to a two-year $121 million extension. I don't think that would be considered a lack of taking risk. I also don't believe that trading for Dennis Schroeder, trying to make something happen, is a lack of taking risk.”
Draymond Green on Mike Dunleavy, Joe Lacob, Jimmy Butler, Dennis SchroederJul 28, 2026YouTube
“This summer was no different with his ability to opt out of the deal;it gave us some wiggle room, some financial flexibility. And we worked together on that, and I don’t think there was ever a doubt he’d be back, but he’s always been about the team … he’s no different than Steph in the sense of we hope he retires a Warrior. We want to make that happen, he wants to make it happen, so we’ll see where things go. Our No. 1 priority right now, Draymond will say this, Steph will say this, everybody here will say this, is to win.”
Mike Dunleavy on Draymond GreenAug 11, 2026New York Times

Mike Dunleavy:

“We're in a time zone here of maximizing our window with Stephen Curry, Draymond, Steve as our coach. As proven here in the first day we could make trades, we did that. We'll continue to look at things... I want to evaluate and see, but we'll always be looking at stuff.”
Mike Dunleavy on Stephen Curry, Draymond GreenDec 16, 2024Twitter @anthonyVslater
