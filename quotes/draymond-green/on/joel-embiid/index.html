Best quotes

Draymond Green:

“Really funny story is Joel Embiid tweeting, 'Did the Bucks give them the championship?' Them being Boston. And the answer to Joel's question is yes, they actually did, because they traded Jrue away. And like, I just think even in that trade, like you got to find and do all you can to like keep Jrue because Jrue can guard bigger. So like, there's Jrue can play off the ball or on the ball. Like, there's no fit mismatch with like Jrue and Dame. And I think like, I don't think Portland was hell-bent on getting Jrue Holiday.”
Draymond Green on Jrue Holiday, Joel EmbiidJun 14, 2024YouTube

Draymond Green on Joel Embiid:

“My thing to him is: you may never win one. The hope is to win one, and I'm not saying he can't win one, butyou may never win one. Then you shorted yourself on the thing we know for sure you can do, which is go get an MVP,which is cook everybody in the league. We know you can do that. So, to sit out saying, 'I'm trying to maximize everything so I can get the championship,' you still have to know how to win a championship, and he don’t because he hasn’t done it.”
Draymond Green on Joel EmbiidOct 28, 2024YouTube

Green said he believes the people stating that it was a legacy performance are the same ones who will be ready to blame Embiid if the 76ers fail to reach the conference championship – somewhere Embiid has never been since entering the league in 2014.

“I hate when people try to set guys up like, ‘Aw man, this is a legacy game for Joel Embiid,’ only to set him up to try to tear him down in the weeks to come,”
Draymond Green on Joel EmbiidMay 5, 2026NBC Sports Bay Area
