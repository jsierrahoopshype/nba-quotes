What Draymond Green has said about Steve Kerr

Every quote from Draymond Green about Steve Kerr in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

Draymond Green:

“For the first time in my career, Coach (Steve) Kerr told me towards the end of last year, he pulled me to the side, he's like, Draymond, I want you shooting the three. And I was like, oh shit. Like it was like a, it was like a out of body out of body experience,because I had never really heard him like confidently look me in my eyes and say to me like, yo, I want you shooting the basketball. Even when I was shooting the ball great, it was just something that I had never heard. And so when he told me, like the confidence level raised immediately, like, oh man, like Coach want me to shoot.”
Draymond Green on Steve KerrSep 18, 2026YouTube

Draymond Green:

“When you have a leader like Steve Kerr, who always knows the right thing to say, and when you have a leader like Steph Curry, that you can always rally around,that you know is going to be there giving everything he’s got—and also one of the greatest players we’ve ever seen—when you’re following two guys like that, things like this are possible.”
Draymond Green on Steve Kerr, Stephen CurryApr 16, 2026YouTube
“(Kerr) and Gentry sat me down and they said listen, ‘We know the situation you’re in. We know you’re in a contract year, and you better believe we’re going to do everything we can to help you in your contract year.’ Coach Kerr said, ‘I’ve played in this league 15 years, I’ve been a GM, I understand all that stuff.’That kind of helped me settle down. It was like, alright don’t go out there worrying about that. It’s the completely wrong thing to be worried about.”
Draymond Green on Steve KerrMay 19, 2015Basketball Insiders
