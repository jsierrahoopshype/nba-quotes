What Draymond Green has said about Kawhi Leonard

Every quote from Draymond Green about Kawhi Leonard in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

6 quotes·2019 to 2026·Latest quote Sep 18, 2026

Best quotes

Draymond Green:

“I think wishing that the NBA took $50 million from a player is disgusting. I think it's purely disgusting. I like Gallo, I like Gallo a lot actually, but this is, this is disgusting to me. And the reason this is disgusting because number one, I don't, I don't want anyone getting fined. I think that, I think that shit is whack. You can go back and look where I said if this is true, I hope the punishment is they dismantle the CBA, not take $50 million out of Kawhi Leonard's pocket. That's so whack and I can't believe he said that.”
Draymond Green on Kawhi LeonardSep 18, 2026YouTube

Draymond Green:

“Kawhi Leonard does not have the power to trade a player. He can request, hey, I want y'all to go out there and get this guy, but he don't have the ability to make that trade. That franchise have to agree, just like they had to agree that they were going to circumvent the cap. He can't make them do that. These are decisions that that franchise made. So in turn, you must have left a big enough impact on that franchise. But to blame another player and say he should have to pay off $50 million back, that shit is whack.”
Draymond Green on Kawhi LeonardSep 18, 2026YouTube

Draymond Green:

“We all get a licensing check. LeBron James, Steph Curry, Kawhi Leonard, Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, Nikola Jokic, they all get the same jersey sales as the rookie. Excuse me, as a rookie, you don't get a license check because the license check is from a year before. But say a second-year guy who didn't play much on a team, and the license check was 500 grand, that's jersey sales. That's all of the licensing things, right? That same 500 grand that that second-year guy gets, Steph Curry gets. It's divvied up, it's even split. And so, you could make 300,000, you could make 500,000, but every player makes the same amount.”
Draymond Green on LeBron James, Kawhi Leonard, Nikola Jokic, Shai Gilgeous-AlexanderSep 11, 2026YouTube
