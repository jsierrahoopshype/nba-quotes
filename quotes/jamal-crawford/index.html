Best quotes

Paolo Banchero:

“Seattle, I think Seattle is pound-for-pound the best basketball factory in the country. When you look at just the depth we have in the history, we got years on years—whether it’s Doug Christie, one of the first OGs, then you got Jason Terry, Isaiah Thomas, Jamal Crawford. You know, the list goes on and on and on from generation to generation, year by year. And, um, I feel like I’m kind of new generation. Both my mom and dad are both from Seattle, so, you know, it’s in my blood, and yeah, I just take it with me wherever I go.”
Paolo Banchero on Jamal Crawford, Jason Terry, Isaiah Thomas, Doug ChristieOct 25, 2024YouTube
“Crawford still doesn’t play like anybody else, nor does anyone really play like him. He skips as much as runs, his dribble nearing chest height, where coaches teach you not to keep it. When he crosses over, he does so precipitously and with devastating speed. From afar he appears to be all limbs upon which a giant round head bobs, as if the character from those life is good T-shirts came to life. “Damn, you got a lot of juice left in those legs!”
Dirk Nowitzki on Jamal CrawfordOct 25, 2017Sports Illustrated

Are you basically saying you don’t want to go back to a rebuilding phase: Paul Allen:

“I think when you sign a Jamal Crawford, and you have LA ... you’ve got potential to be very, very competitive. There’s obviously a tipping point where you say Oh, I don’t have an All-Star caliber player to build around, and then you say it’s time to reduce salary, go back and go young and go through rebuilding process. But obviously with the moves we’ve made that’s not in the cards for this year.”
PAPaul Allen on Jamal CrawfordDec 20, 2011Oregonian
