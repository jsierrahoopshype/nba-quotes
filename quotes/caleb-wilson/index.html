What NBA players and coaches have said about Caleb Wilson

Best quotes

Draymond Green on people calling him a idiot for saying Caleb Wilson’s floor is Kevin Garnett:

“I said that with all due respect to Kevin Garnett, but just saying, yo, this kid can be that special. So if I say Kevin Garnett’s the floor, by no mean am I dimming Kevin Garnett’s light. I’m not capable of dimming Kevin Garnett’s light. He’s Kevin Garnett. What I’m saying is I think this kid can be that good.”
Draymond Green on Kevin Garnett, Caleb WilsonJul 16, 2026x.com

coach Tiago Splitter said of Hield’s role.

“You’re open, shoot it. When you surround your team with gravity like those guys (Hield and Powell), it opens it up for Caleb (Wilson), Matas (Buzelis) to get to the paint, for Nic (Claxton), for Tre (Jones), Josh (Giddey) – they’re all going to play in the paint.”
Tiago Splitter on Buddy Hield, Tre Jones, Nic Claxton, Josh Giddey, Matas Buzelis, Caleb WilsonSep 30, 2026Chicago Sun-Times

Josh Giddey on Caleb Wilson:

“I think the thing about him is he doesn’t have one part of his game. He can kind of do everything, and he obviously showed the way he can shoot the ball at summer league, and he can get downhill. He’s athletic, so there’s no real holes in his game, so to speak. He’s obviously young, and he’ll polish a lot of his game up as he gets older and matures.“But I think being able to guard multiple positions, he can rebound, he can run the floor. He doesn’t have a lot of weaknesses in his game, and as I said, he’s a young guy in the league. There’ll be learning curves for him, but he comes in with a pretty well-rounded game and someone that can impact the floor at a high level on both ends.”
Josh Giddey on Caleb WilsonSep 3, 2026casino.org
