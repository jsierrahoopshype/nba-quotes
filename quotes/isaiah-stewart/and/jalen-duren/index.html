What Isaiah Stewart and Jalen Duren have said about each other

Isaiah Stewart on Jalen Duren, and Jalen Duren on Isaiah Stewart: every quote in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

“We want to be the best players we can be so the Pistons can be the best organization they can be. I’m a winner. Isaiah is a winner. We all want to win. That’s my mindset and I know that’s his mindset, too. It’s not a competition. We all just want to be great. He pushes me to be great. I try to do the same for him.”
Jalen Duren on Isaiah StewartOct 22, 2022The Athletic

Jalen Duren on playing alongside Isaiah Stewart:

“That’s my brother, man. He’s probably one of the best teammates I ever had. And it’s not even for like the probably the extra stuff y’all see and all that goes on. It’s just the simple fact that who he is as a man. Like man to man, he’s my guy dog. He’s that guy you can talk to. He’s a guy that’s always going to be there for you. He’s a guy that as a teammate you wouldn’t want nobody else. He’s a guy that you hate to play against but love to play with. I wouldn’t ask for nobody else to be on my squad.”
Jalen Duren on Isaiah StewartApr 17, 2026x.com

Asked Weaver how Duren fits with Isaiah Stewart:

“They’re different. (Duren) is bigger. His presence is more pronounced. Isaiah has shown to be able to shoot the 3 more. They can play together. Horford and Robert Williams played together … I don’t see it as a challenge.”
Isaiah Stewart on Al Horford, Robert Williams, Jalen DurenJun 23, 2022Twitter @omarisankofa
