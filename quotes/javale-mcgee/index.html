Best quotes

Bogut told reporters Saturday.

“I'm just playing physical like they are. If someone steps up and wants to get into it, I've got no problem with that. It’s nothing personal. They’re physical. Kenneth Faried runs around and hits people. We just have to match their intensity. If it gets personal during games and guys want to have a crack at you, that’s playoff basketball. I’m not sitting here saying I hate McGee or I hate Faried. But if they want to be physical, we’re going to be physical back. We’re not going to take a step backward.”
Andrew Bogut on Javale McGee, Kenneth FariedApr 27, 2013NBA.com

George Karl:

“I’m sure that McGee will sign. That’s what our management is working on daily basis and I trust them. We don’t want to do a mistake about the free agents and try to do the best. The evidence is Fournier can be a NBA player. I think he played pretty good here, in Vegas. He showed us his style, his excitement to play here and hopefully he can follow our program”
George Karl on Javale McGee, Evan FournierJul 18, 2012EuroHoops.net

Jason Kidd shared, via ESPN.

“… [But] We believe we’re going to be a bigger team. Nothing against JB’s height. With Spencer (Dinwiddie) starting, our starting five is gonna be big with JaVale (McGee)at center. We believe that we’re going to be having a lot more offense coming off the bench.”
Jason Kidd on Javale McGee, Spencer DinwiddieJul 8, 2022Clutch Points
