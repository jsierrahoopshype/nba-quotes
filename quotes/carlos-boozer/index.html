Best quotes

Deron Williams on playing for the legendary Jerry Sloan and his toughness and coaching style:

“Do you know how much dummy offense we did? Man, so we would do it...some days we would come in, and he’d put me at the five and he’d put Booze [Carlos Boozer] at the one. You had to know, he wanted you to know every position so that everything was interchangeable. And that’s how good we run it. That’s what made it so successful, is how hard we ran it and how well we ran it.”
Deron Williams on Carlos Boozer, Jerry SloanDec 8, 2020The Players' Tribune

Millsap is confident that he’s ready.

“Carlos Boozer is a great player, and he’ll be tough to replace, no doubt. Him leaving is a tough loss. But I think I can help this team win, period,”
Paul Millsap on Carlos BoozerJul 9, 2010Salt Lake Tribune
“That's going to make a big difference for the offense—when we get into our halfcourt sets, we are going to be much tougher to guard. Teams won't be able to focus on Derrick. If you can have a big man who runs the pick-and-roll like Carlos, you are going to have a big advantage. That's going to make Derrick a much better player.”
Tom Thibodeau on Carlos Boozer, Derrick RoseAug 19, 2010Sporting News
