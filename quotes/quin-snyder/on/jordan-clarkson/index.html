Eric Walden:Quin Snyder, on Jordan Clarkson’s fit:

“I love him. I love him. Write it down — I love him. Sometimes a system needs to be malleable. ... I want him to be instinctive.... The opportunity to continue to get better is there for him offensively, which is a heck of a statement.”
Quin Snyder on Jordan ClarksonJan 31, 2020Twitter @tribjazz

Quin Snyder on Jordan Clarkson's struggles:

“I don't want to overreact to one or two games for any of our guys. We know who Jordan is, he's been terrific, and he's had far more games where he's made shots.”
