What NBA players and coaches have said about Lamar Odom

22 quotes about Lamar Odom from 17 players, coaches and executives, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

Earl K. Sneed:Dirk Nowitzki:

“It's just an incredibly sad story. I think (Lamar Odom) was a great guy, even though stuff didn't work out for him here. I've got nothing but positive and nice things to say about him. He was always cool to us -- his teammates. Sometimes, he was just a little frustrated because it wasn't going his way, but (Lamar Odom) was a fun guy to be around. It's just a sad story, and we all obviously wish (Lamar Odom) well. We're thinking about him. Hopefully he'll pull through.”
Dirk Nowitzki on Lamar OdomOct 15, 2015Twitter @EarlKSneed

Brand told The Undefeated.

“If you see the No. 3 pick getting off, it’s like, ‘I need to get mine.’ I went through that with Steve Francis and Lamar Odom. Steve would have 25 points and 15 assists and Lamar would have a triple-double. And I’m looking at the pressure like, ‘Now, I got to do something all day long.’ What I learned is that you don’t get too high if you have a good game and you don’t get too low if you had a bad game.”
Elton Brand on Lamar Odom, Steve FrancisJul 4, 2017The Undefeated

Gentry recalled Wednesday at Pelicans training camp.

“He said, ‘I want to apologize because I feel like I am as responsible as anybody for getting you fired because I didn’t play the way I supposed to and I didn’t do what I was supposed to.’ I been in the league 27 years, and that’s happened once, OK? And it was Lamar Odom who did it.”
AGAlvin Gentry on Lamar OdomOct 15, 2015Sporting News
