Andre Iguodala:

“For LeBron to be the first athlete to become a billionaire while he’s still playing, that’s something. He did it by doing things differently, being more than just endorsements and endorsements checks. He got equity in his deals, and that equity has grown. To see him realize the gains from his deals, that’s unique. A lot of players are following him, seeing how he did it, but LeBron was the one with the foresight to take control of his business and his brand in a way we hadn’t seen before. It’s hard to be the first one, to be the guinea pig, and actually have it work.”
