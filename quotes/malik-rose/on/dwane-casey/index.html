Malik Rose:

“I really do think coach Casey has the ability to coach at a championship level and alter his coaching style to fit his team's personality and ability. That's what I think separates the good coaches from the bad coaches, the coaches that can adjust and they don't have to coach just one way. Coach Casey can coach in a myriad of ways, and he'll find the best way to coach the Pistons and put them in position to win as many games as possible, and really have a special season.”
