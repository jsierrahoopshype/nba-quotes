Best quotes

Daryl Morey:

“Carmelo had a tremendous approach during his time with the Rockets and accepted every role head coach Mike D’Antoni gave him. The fit we envisioned when Carmelo chose to sign with the Rockets has not materialized, therefore we thought it was best to move on as any other outcome would have been unfair to him.”
Daryl Morey on Carmelo Anthony, Mike D'AntoniNov 15, 2018NBA.com

Daryl Morey:

“We're gonna look for better players next year. Whatever it takes to get over that final hump to get a championship. On the coaching front I told Mike (D'Antoni),I recommended that we make some changes... We are all on the same page & we knew what the next steps were”
Daryl Morey on Mike D'AntoniMay 30, 2019Twitter @MarkBermanFox26

Daryl Morey:

“They're different players (Paul and Westbrook)... I think in our structure and what Mike (D'Antoni) is going to want (Westbrook) to do, he's going to be great.”
Daryl Morey on Chris Paul, Mike D'Antoni, Russell WestbrookAug 1, 2019Twitter @SalmanAliNBA
