Best quotes

Ian Begley:Tom Thibodeau was asked about NYK's young players:

“Based on film, I really think RJ (Barrett) has a very bright future as does Mitchell Robinson. Those are 2 who stand out. But we have a lot of young talent. I don’t like to make qualifications on people until I get around them.”
Tom Thibodeau on RJ Barrett, Mitchell RobinsonJul 30, 2020Twitter @IanBegley

Fred Katz:Tom Thibodeau:

“The thing about RJ Barrett is he's very steady. He doesn't get rattled.... We gotta get him into the open floor. If we do that, he's gonna make shots. He gets downhill, he's tough to guard.”
Tom Thibodeau on RJ BarrettApr 21, 2023Twitter @FredKatz

Asked about RJ Barrett playing two minutes in the second half, Tom Thibodeau said,

“We just got behind by so much that we were just looking for life. And that group that was in there (after Barrett subbed out) gave us a little bit of a spark. So that's what we went with.”
Tom Thibodeau on RJ BarrettNov 13, 2022Twitter @IanBegley
