Jalen Brunson on why he went to hug Spurs HC Mitch Johnson before his father after the win:

“I hugged and said what's up to Coach Johnson from the Spurs first, just to show respect…It was just kind of instinct, like how I was raised. I think win or loss, you show respect regardless of the outcome. I’ve got a lot of respect for them over there.”

Green said.

Gregg Popovich:

“I’ve decided not to return to the sidelines this season.
