Some of it is time together and the other part is just matchups. Orlando finished the game [Saturday] with four 6-9, 6-10 guys. We don’t have a lineup right now that can match that. A lot of the NBA is matchup and being able to compete through stuff because a lot of stuff is going to be out of your control. You just got to go with the flow.”
