Kai Jones:

“Mitch Kupchak, he believed in me from the first day. He told me that he really thought I was going to be a great player in the NBA. And even when I was waived and a free agent, I called him up and I said, Mitch, thank you for everything you did for me. And he told me, I still think you have a chance to come back into the NBA and be a great player. And I think that I did, showing my preseason with the Clippers and my time with the Clippers and the Mavs. I think that I showed that I can really play at a high level in the NBA and help a team win.”
