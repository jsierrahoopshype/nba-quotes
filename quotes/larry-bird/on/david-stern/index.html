Larry Bird:

“My family and I send our sincere condolences to David Stern’s family. There are no words that can really describe the far-reaching impact of Commissioner Stern's brilliance, vision, fairness and hard work over so many years. When you think of all that he accomplished worldwide on behalf of thousands of players, so many fans, all of the jobs he created for team and arena employees and all of the people that benefitted from the many layers of growth in the sport and industry that David spearheaded and then passed on to others, there is no doubt Commissioner Stern lifted the NBA to new heights and he will be greatly missed by all of us.”
LBLarry Bird on David SternJan 1, 2020NBA.com

Pacers President of Basketball Operations Larry Bird on Retirement of David Stern:

“I want to thank Commissioner Stern for all the contributions he has made the last 30 years. He took a struggling league to a very high level while also taking the NBA globally instead of just nationally and we have all benefited from it. There are thousands of families that he has helped to lead a comfortable life and gave us the opportunity to do more than expected. I know every player is grateful for that. I would like to wish David and Dianne the best in retirement.”
LBLarry Bird on David SternJan 31, 2014NBA.com
