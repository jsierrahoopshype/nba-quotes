What NBA players and coaches have said about Dean Smith

7 quotes about Dean Smith from 5 players, coaches and executives, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

7 quotes, 3 of them from players·2015 to 2022·Latest quote May 26, 2022

Best quotes

Karl told USA TODAY Sports.

“The combination of his humility, and just to do the right things and treat people the right way. He was a big meal guy. He liked sharing ideas over a good dinner at a good restaurant, and fortunately I was blessed to have many of those opportunities with him. I’m tearing up a little bit, to be honest with you. That’s Coach Smith right there.”
George Karl on Dean SmithMar 27, 2015For The Win
“Coach Smith used to get every article on any one of his Carolina players and coaches. Every day. There would be times when I would use the word 'I' in a quote. And he would send me every article with 'I' in it, and circle it. And he'd say, 'don't you mean 'we?' Don't you mean 'the team?'”
George Karl on Dean SmithFeb 9, 2015NBA.com

Mark Medina:Lakers GM Mitch Kupchak on Dean Smith:

“Coach Smith was one of the most influential people in my life, & his passing brings me great sadness”
MKMitch Kupchak on Dean SmithFeb 8, 2015Twitter @MarkG_Medina
