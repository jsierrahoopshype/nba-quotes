What Bob Myers and Kobe Bryant have said about each other

Bob Myers on Kobe Bryant, and Kobe Bryant on Bob Myers: every quote in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

Bob Myers:

“Kobe, my first day in that office with Arn (Tellem), I went in to interview to be an intern, and Kobe was sitting in that little lounge area they had. You know this, and he was sitting on the couch, and he had a Joe Montana retro jersey, 49ers, number 16. And I literally walked in, he was the first person I saw, because you know that little lounge was right there, there was no security, there was just Kobe Bryant. And they had just lost to Utah when he missed those shots. And this dude was so confident. I said, hey, I'd like to meet you, and he started talking. And he said,I'm going to win 10 NBA championships, and then I'm going to finish my career in Italy.”
Bob Myers on Kobe BryantAug 27, 2026YouTube

Bryant said while laughing.

“I’ve known Bob Myers, the (general manager) over at Golden State, forever…My last all star game we had a chance to catch up, as we were staying at the same hotel. I got a chance to tell him congratulations on everything and he said ‘Hey listen, if there’s any chance you want to change your mind and play another year, you can always come here.’But it’s all tongue in cheek man.”
Kobe Bryant on Bob MyersJun 11, 2018USA Today Sports
“I told them before the game, 'I wish I would've had my last game here earlier in the season because you guys just elevate the bar in terms of gifts that I'm receiving.'It was awesome to see them and get a chance to spend some time with [Warriors executive board member Jerry West] and spend some time with [Warriors general manager Bob Myers], who I've known for a long time. Just an absolute class organization.”
Kobe Bryant on Jerry West, Bob MyersJan 15, 2016ESPN.com
