Kyle Kuzma:

“What the front office has done really well is that now we look like a basketball team. The past couple of years, it’s been a little funky. But now, with modern basketball, we’ve got an electric guard in Jordan Poole. We have wings, whether it’s me, Bilal, or Coulibaly, and we have a big in Gafford who can really affect games defensively because he’s so long and his defensive instincts are amazing. So, we’re building a team right now, and in the next couple of years, with a good draft pick, we can be in a really good place.”
Kyle Kuzma on Jordan PooleSep 11, 2024YouTube
