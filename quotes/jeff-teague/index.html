Al Horford:

“We have the 23rd pick and I know the team will draft the best player available. I would like to pick a shooter like Kentucky's Doron Lamb. But it wouldn't bother me if they picked a big who could help me like Festus Ezeli(Vanderbilt). Anyway, I doubt they will pick Jeff Teague's brother, Marquis Teague.”

Patrick Beverley:

“The first team we play against is the Clippers, and if we win, the Minnesota gets into the playoffs after 16 years. Jimmy Butler has been there, Derrick Rose has been there, all these different players have been there, high-level players, all-star, Jeff Teague, all-star caliber type players, and Minnesota has never been there. Andthen you inject Pat Bev in there and we're playing with a number seven seed.”
Patrick Beverley on Derrick Rose, Jeff Teague, Jimmy ButlerSep 9, 2026YouTube
