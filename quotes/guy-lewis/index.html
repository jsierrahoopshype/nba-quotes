Best quotes

“Here's a man who took a little kid out of Rayville, La., put his career and everything on the line, to bring (former UH star) Don Chaney and I in there to give us an opportunity to showcase our talent and our ability, to mold us and grow us and give us a true start in life. Now to have the opportunity to stand before all of the great players and coaches that played this game, to present my coach Guy V. Lewis, words can never express what this means to me. I'm appreciative to have this opportunity to introduce him into the Basketball Hall of Fame.”
Elvin Hayes on Guy Lewis, Don ChaneyAug 7, 2013FOX 26 Sports

Elvin Hayes on passing of Guy Lewis:

“We miss him but I know he's much better off. I can see him running around coaching a great team up there. That kid to the University of Houston in a time of integration & put his career on the line & gave me an opportunity to achieve & accomplish all that I'm accomplishing today. It's all due to Guy Lewis.”
Elvin Hayes on Guy LewisNov 26, 2015Twitter @MarkBermanFox26

Mark Berman:Elvin Hayes:

“All the awards I've received, Hall of Fame, NBA Championships, NBA 50 Greatest, Guy Lewis is a part of all of that.”
Elvin Hayes on Guy LewisNov 26, 2015Twitter @MarkBermanFox26
