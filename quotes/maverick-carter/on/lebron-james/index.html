Best quotes

Maverick Carter:

“We thought about partnering with the NBA and we had a conversation and David Stern, rest in peace, who I actually always really admired. David Stern basically told them, 'F*ck do I need you for? I'm the NBA. I own basketball in the world.'And he said, 'So if I'm not going to do it with the NBA, I'm not. The next best person to partner with me on building a basketball business and league in India is LeBron James.'And that's why I wanted this deal.”
MCMaverick Carter on LeBron James, David SternJul 27, 2026YouTube

Maverick Carter:

“I left Nike in '06 to work to be full-time with LeBron. LeBron had an agent at the time, fired his agent. The same one Kevin (Durant) fired soon after. And we started a company to manage LeBron. And frankly, I didn't know what the f*ck I was doing, the truth. I mean, I knew the only thing I knew is I had to get smart people. I knew the buckets, like we need some legal, we need accounting. I knew those things and I knew how to find people.”
MCMaverick Carter on LeBron James, Kevin DurantJul 27, 2026YouTube
“LeBron's a 29-year-old man with lots of money, got a wife, two kids, one on the way. He makes his own decisions. He doesn't need anyone pushing him any way, and a guy like that, you're not going to push him either way. We'll help him think through things and help him see through things, but he makes his own decision in his heart. Because, ultimately, he has to live with it. I'm not the one who has to show up and play games for any team.”
MCMaverick Carter on LeBron JamesJul 21, 2014ESPN.com
