Tyronn Lue:

“Moving over six inches on the bench—that’s what people don’t understand. It’s tough. Especially when it happens midway through the season. We had just gone to the Finals. Lost Game 6 without Kevin Love and Kyrie. And now I’m being asked to replace Coach Blatt, who had done a hell of a job. If we didn’t make it back to the Finals—or at least to Game 7—I was going to be viewed as a failure. And that pressure? Man, it was real.”

“He's going to be here.(...) He was great for us all season long. (...) Throughout the playoffs, for the first three rounds, he was big. (...) We want him here, he's a big part of what we're doing and what we're trying to do going forward.”
