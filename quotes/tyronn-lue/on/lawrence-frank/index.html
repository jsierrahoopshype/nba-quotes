Ty Lue on the relationship between he and Lawrence Frank, specifically as NBA trade season begins:

“We always work close together. Lawrence has been a great partner. Us trying to be on the same page, understanding what we need, what we wanna do. We’ve always been on the same page… He understands the game, he’s been a coach…We’ve always been on the same page as far as the talent you wanna get, who you wanna try to get, the players you wanna try to get. It doesn’t workout all the time. You can’t get the guys you want every single year… It’s a tough business. We always stay locked in, and we’re hand in hand.”
