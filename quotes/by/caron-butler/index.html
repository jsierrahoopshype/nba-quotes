Caron Butler:

“It was just kind of something we knew. It was like, ‘Go talk to him.’ [Or] ‘I didn’t talk to him, you talk to him.’ It was just one of those things. But I will say this: whenever you talked to a guy like Gilbert, he always listened. He respected our voices, Antawn and myself. He always listened. And the other guys always listened to us, as well. And just on that particular incident that day, it went too far.”
Caron Butler on Gilbert Arenas, Antawn JamisonOct 8, 2015Washington Post
“We all went to a grand jury and statements were taken so it was something that was already documented. When you’re writing a book, you know that the process is a long interview. “I don’t have any malice in my heart and believe it or not, I love Gilbert. I [loved] Javaris. Those are dudes I went to war with. I would never share information in the locker room or anything that would hurt anyone. But because it was documented, the story was brought up. I gave my take.”
Caron Butler on Gilbert ArenasOct 11, 2015Boston Globe
“He was on the [training] table and I talked to him. The whole Lamar situation had broken out [and] when we got the news, he told me that he was leaving to go check on Lamar. I consider him family, somebody I'll be friends with long after the game of basketball is over. He feels the same way, that's my brother for life.”
Caron Butler on Kobe BryantNov 20, 2015Los Angeles Times
