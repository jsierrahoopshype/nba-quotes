What Dean Wade has said about other NBA players and coaches

Best quotes

Dean Wade:

“They are unbelievable. They are so fast and quick. Tyrese Maxey, he gets out in transition and it is unbelievable how fast he moves, how fast he can change direction. I feel like the thing that really surprised me the most is they never make the wrong decision, they are always making the right decision. If that is dashing it out on the baseline, kicking out, scoring, whatever it is, they always make the right decision. That is hard, especially for a young guy. Tyrese Maxey is only in his second year, so seeing him, he is playing like he is a vet. He knows the right plays and he is doing it and he is not forcing anything.”
Dean Wade on Tyrese MaxeyOct 1, 2026YouTube

Dean Wade on the high basketball IQ of new 76ers teammates:

“It is completely different. You know their tendencies playing against them, but when you are on the same team, you can pick their brain. My biggest thing is the way they think the game is the most impressive thing. Tyrese Maxey, Joel Embiid, LeBron James, Jaylen Brown, everyone, the way they think the game is at such a high level. As an NBA player, you think you know the game well, and then you get on a team like this and it is night and day. You see the way they think about plays, think about defenses, the way they think about rotations. It is similar to some things I thought, but they go a little more in-depth and it is super impressive.”
Dean Wade on LeBron James, Joel Embiid, Jaylen Brown, Tyrese MaxeyOct 1, 2026YouTube

Dean Wade on what has stood out about his new teammates:

“My biggest thing is the way they think the game is the most impressive thing. Tyrese, Joel, LeBron, JB, everyone. The way they think the game is such high level. As an NBA player, you think you know the game well and then you get on a team like this....it's night and day being on this side with them instead of against them.”
Dean Wade on LeBron James, Tyrese MaxeySep 30, 2026x.com
