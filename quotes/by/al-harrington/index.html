Al Harrington on Reggie Miller work ethic:

“Reggie Miller is probably the hardest workers that I’ve ever seen. And most superstitious player I’ve ever seen too. When I say he did the same thing the same way every single day. Like, he got to the gym at the same time every single day, he sat down the same way, he fu*king blew his nose I will never forget because I used to watch him he would take three napkins or three pieces of tissue and put it on his thigh like, one, two, three turn it fold it blow his nose throw it away. Put his socks on the same way at the same time on the clock every single day. Bro was amazing just to watch him.”
AHAl Harrington on Reggie MillerAug 20, 2026x.com
