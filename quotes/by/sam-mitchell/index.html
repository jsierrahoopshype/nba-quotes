Sam Mitchell:

“It was Kobe Bryant. I mean we're talking about one of the greatest players to play that had an unbelievable night. If he scored 81 and we win the game, what would you guys in the media have been saying?”
SMSam Mitchell on Kobe BryantDec 18, 2017CBSSports.com
