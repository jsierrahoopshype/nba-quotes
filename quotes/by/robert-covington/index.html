Best quotes

Covington said of Pierce.

“Lloyd Pierce was the best coach when it came to bringing that out of me. He gave me challenges, made me guard, he gave me opportunities to put myself in better positions to understand the different dynamics of defense. I watched elite defenders from previous years. that’s just how I became able to do a lot.”
Robert Covington on Lloyd PierceMay 15, 2020The Athletic

Interestingly, he did so while also criticizing other athletes like LeBron — whom he suggests is all talk but no walk.

“I want to dedicate this fight to all the first responders, all the military out there. This world would not be safe without you guys, you keep us safe, and not these woke athletes. I’m sick of these spineless cowards like LeBron James,”
Robert Covington on LeBron JamesSep 20, 2020Clutch Points
