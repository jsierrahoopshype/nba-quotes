Keaton Wagler on comparison:

“I would say Tyrese Haliburton. I just think based off of our size that we both have and how we have the ball in our hands and can make plays, and knowing when it's our time to score the ball and when it's our time to get others involved and make the right play. That's a big thing for him is that he always makes the right play, and that's something that I always do too, is look to make the right play and play the right way.”
Keaton Wagler on Tyrese HaliburtonJul 1, 2026YouTube

Keaton Wagler on who he watches:

“I watch a lot of, you know, good NBA guards like Haliburton and SGA and a couple others. So I try to just steal little things out of, you know, each of those players, but I think the closest person is probably Haliburton, just off of size and the way we play, being able to have a really good feel for the game and use wall screens to create advantages.”
Keaton Wagler on Tyrese HaliburtonJun 24, 2026x.com

Keaton Wagler:

“When I get there, I can't wait to play against Kevin Durant. He's one of the best scorers I've ever seen and being able to see that in person live and get to go up against him is going to be, you know, it's going to be a crazy experience.”
Keaton Wagler on Kevin DurantJul 1, 2026YouTube
