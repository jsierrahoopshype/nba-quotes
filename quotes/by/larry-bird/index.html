Pacers President of Basketball Operations Larry Bird on Retirement of David Stern:

“I want to thank Commissioner Stern for all the contributions he has made the last 30 years. He took a struggling league to a very high level while also taking the NBA globally instead of just nationally and we have all benefited from it. There are thousands of families that he has helped to lead a comfortable life and gave us the opportunity to do more than expected. I know every player is grateful for that. I would like to wish David and Dianne the best in retirement.”
LBLarry Bird on David SternJan 31, 2014NBA.com

Larry Bird on If he feels at all sorry for the rash of criticism that LeBron has faced:

“He brought a lot of it on himself, but here’s what I feel about LeBron: he’s as good as anyone that’s ever played this game. I think he’s going to win a lot of titles; I think he’s going to continue to get better. To me, he’s an amazing athlete, he’s never been hurt, he’s got the body of a football player and he’s got skill. I just think he’s off the charts good. And I think his time is going to come. I think they did a remarkable job in Miami this year bringing that team together and getting where they got. They’ll get better next year. Their time is going to come if he stays healthy, and he’s going to have a lot of success in this league and win championships.”
LBLarry Bird on LeBron JamesJun 17, 2011Sports Radio Interviews

Larry Bird:

“My family and I send our sincere condolences to David Stern’s family. There are no words that can really describe the far-reaching impact of Commissioner Stern's brilliance, vision, fairness and hard work over so many years.
