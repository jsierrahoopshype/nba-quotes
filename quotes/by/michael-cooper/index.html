Who is your GOAT?Cooper:

“My GOAT is Kareem. He is an unstoppable player. You look at three things for a GOAT. One, can they score at any time at any point in the game? Kareem was unstoppable, even double or triple-teamed. If you triple-teamed Kobe, LeBron or Magic when they come across the floor, they’re going to pass the ball. But with Kareem, you couldn’t double or triple team him. He had the Skyhook. Next, a GOAT makes others around him better. Kareem made others better. No one other than Magic made others around them better when they were double or triple-teamed. For a GOAT, if you foul, will he make his free throws? Kareem shot in the 80s in crunch-time free-throw shooting. So that’s why Kareem is my GOAT.”
MCMichael Cooper on Kareem Abdul-JabbarJul 16, 2026Fade Away World

Michael Cooper on Lonzo Ball & Kyle Kuzma:

“Lonzo Ball has shown that he’s kind of like a Magic Johnson-type of player. He’s the type of player that makes other players around him better. I think Kyle Kuzma has shown that he can shoot the ball with some of the best.”
MCMichael Cooper on Magic Johnson, Lonzo Ball, Kyle KuzmaAug 24, 2018Twitter @RyanWardLA

Michael Cooper:

“You know what? The Forum Club was a different place. It was almost like Studio 54 in New York at the time, and Dr. Buss wanted that. He paraded women that came to the game around, and then he’d take his entourage and go sit up by the flag. That was part of LA.”

“And when I walk into the gym I see Kareem Abdul-Jabbar and Jamaal Wilkes and Norm Nixon. So, we get into practice and stuff. Jerry pulls me to the side and he goes, ‘Coop, I already have scorers. I got 30 points in Kareem. I got 27 to 28 from Jamaal Wilkes. I got 22 to 25 in Norm Nixon. There is not enough balls to go around for you to get shots. So, I need somebody to play defense.’“So, I said, ‘Hey, I already got the fundamentals down. I got the aggressiveness down.’ And now I’ve been told that this was going to be my role.
