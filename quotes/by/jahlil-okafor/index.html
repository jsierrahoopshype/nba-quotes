Mike Mazzeo:Jahlil Okafor on Kobe Bryant:

“It's different. It's just different. I've played against LeBron James twice, you see KD (Kevin Durant) but when you see Kobe it's just a different feel. I was in the weight room stretching and he walks in. It was like you're seeing like a God kind of in a sense. For me that was my Michael Jordan. That was somebody that I always watched. You see him score 81 points, you see him win championships and I was always rooting for him to win, so it was surreal to see him.”
Jahlil Okafor on LeBron James, Kevin Durant, Kobe BryantDec 2, 2015ESPN.com
