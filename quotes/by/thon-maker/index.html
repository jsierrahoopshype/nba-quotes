Thon Maker:

“It helps us as a team. You see Andre (Drummond) doing that, grabbing and going. It’s not grab and go, and necessarily go finish it. If you have the lane, go ahead and finish it, but more to get the offensive moving, get everybody running. You see Andre does it a lot. He’ll come down, get it out to the shooters on the wings, and it’s so dangerous; it’s hard to stop. The offence is flowing, everybody’s spreading, nobody’s waiting and clogging on the lane. They want me to get better in that area.”
Thon Maker on Andre DrummondAug 29, 2019FOXSports.com
