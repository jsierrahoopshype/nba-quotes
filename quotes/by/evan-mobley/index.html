Evan Mobley on James Harden:

“Like you said, a legend. It’s crazy that I get to play with him and be around him every day and learn from him. It’s surreal in a way. But as I get to know him every single day, he’s a great guy, just a normal dude just like all of us. I feel that’s a big factor that a lot of people hold people on pedestals, but he’s just a normal guy just like all of us. I feel that gives everyone else confidence that he has a good personality, natural personality, and gets along with everyone. He’s not trying to big-time anyone or anything like that.”
Evan Mobley on James HardenOct 2, 2026YouTube
