Shawn Kemp:

“At the time of playing basketball in the 90s, it was a very physical sport. It wasn't a weak man's sport. Only the strong survived. You want to compete at those best levels. So I don't think drugs really came into the picture until probably when I started to lose. When you start to lose, your life changes. The one thing that I kind of regret about back then is playing with Gary Payton. We had this great friendship, this great camaraderie where we won so many games, winning 50, 60 games a year.”
Shawn Kemp on Gary PaytonJun 20, 2024YouTube

Shawn Kemp:

“The general manager came to me and he was like, 'Well, if you want to be one of the highest paid, then all you gotta do is go out there and outplay Michael Jordan.'And I was like, okay. A couple of years later, we were playing in the championship, and I look at it as an opportunity for me to shine and also an opportunity for you to make more money. If I can outscore Michael Jordan in this series, and also play well and help our team achieve what we're trying to achieve, then I'm going to put myself in a pretty good position.”
Shawn Kemp on Michael JordanJun 20, 2024YouTube
