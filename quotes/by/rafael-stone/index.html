Best quotes

Rafael Stone on Amen Thompson:

“I think he's a very, very good basketball player. Since about halfway through his rookie year, we've been a materially better team when he's on the court. And he keeps getting better. I thought he had a really good year last year. He just got better and better and better and better. And he helps our team on the court in literally every way you can. We turn the ball over less, we turn the other team over more, we rebound better, we shoot a higher percentage, we shoot free throws better. Everything's better with Amen Thompson. So yeah, we're very, very high on him.”
RSRafael Stone on Free throws, Amen ThompsonOct 9, 2026YouTube

Rafael Stone on the expectations for Kevin Durant in Houston:

“Obviously when you trade for somebody of Kevin Durant's stature, it's very important that you have good conversations coming in about expectations. And he understood coming in that this wasn't going to be a situation where we're trading for him and now we're going to go get 75 veteran players to play with him, which sometimes happens, but it's not like that has a great track record of success. And so, he understood that he was coming in to play with a bunch of younger guys and that the team was probably going to look the way it looks now. And it has. And is best I can tell, you guys can ask him, but he really enjoys them.”
RSRafael Stone on Kevin DurantOct 9, 2026YouTube

Rafael Stone on Jalen Green:

“Jalens's electric. His first step is as good as anybody in basketball. His 2nd and 3rd step is even better. When he jumps, it's really high so good things happen. And on top of that he can handle the ball and shoot. I think Jalen's a bucket.”
RSRafael Stone on Jalen GreenJul 30, 2021Twitter @SalmanAliNBA
