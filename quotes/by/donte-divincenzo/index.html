Asked about Knicks' adjustments if Mitchell Robinson (ankle) misses Game 4, Donte DiVincenzo says,

“First and foremost, I hope we have Mitch. I expect Mitch to play. But if he doesn't, it’s been like that all year. You have Precious Achuiwa, you have Jericho Sims, those guys have been ready all season long. So, you know, it's the next man up mentality and we've earned the two seed with Mitch missing 50 plus games. So I expect him to play. I hope he plays and if he doesn't, we know the next man up.”
Donte DiVincenzo on Jericho SimsApr 27, 2024Twitter @IanBegley

Donte DiVincenzo:

“I think whatever team we have, we’re going to be very good. No matter what happens in the offseason, I think overall everyone’s going to come back and get better. I think with (Knicks head coach Tom Thibodeau) there's always a foundation, it doesn't matter who’s on the court.”
Donte DiVincenzo on Tom ThibodeauJul 3, 2024SportsNet New York
“No matter what happens the rest of the offseason, I think overall everybody is going to come back and get better. And I think every single person in the organization is going to do everything they can to take that next step.”
Donte DiVincenzo on Tom ThibodeauJul 1, 2024New York Post
