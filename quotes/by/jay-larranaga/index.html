Jay Larranaga on Tyronn Lue:

“I think everyone on our staff is so blessed to have Ty as our boss, right? So, that's the first thing. And that appreciation for kind of how he treats everybody on a day-to-day basis, how he treats our staff, how he treats the players... You know, someone said a long time ago, like, he treats them so well that they almost feel guilty not to give an honest effort. And that's kind of what I try and do is like I'm gonna try and treat them really well, I'm going to try and give them everything possible to help them be successful. And then hopefully they take that lead.”
JLJay Larranaga on Tyronn LueJul 6, 2026x.com
