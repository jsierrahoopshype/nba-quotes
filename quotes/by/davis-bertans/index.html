Tom Orsborn:Bertans on Belinelli's off-balance shots:

Davis Bertans on Marco BelinelliJan 18, 2019Twitter @tom_orsborn

Fred Katz:Davis Bertans on Tobias Harris’ 37-point performance:

Davis Bertans on Tobias Harris, Joel EmbiidMay 23, 2021Twitter @FredKatz
