Tomer Azarly:Mo Bamba on his injury management plan and if he can play back-to-backs:

“I am not Joe (Joel Embiid).Nah, I'll die on my shield if I have to, but there's definitely a plan in place. Asked if he had a chip on his shoulder, Bamba told The Athletic:

With just his swagger and his mentality. And he remembers every single guy, every single team that passed up on him in that draft order, and it’s just more motivation and more fuel.”
