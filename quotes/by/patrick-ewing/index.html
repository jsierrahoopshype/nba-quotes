Best quotes

“Going against coach Riley, me going against Alonzo. There was a lot of love and hate in that rivalry. Two teams that mirror each other. Pat Riley did an outstanding job of building the Knicks with Dave Checketts and then he leaves and goes to Miami. I think the respect we had for one another, that led to the physicality and the thrill of victory and the agony of defeat.”
Patrick Ewing on Pat Riley, Dave CheckettsApr 28, 2023Newsday

Ewing on the 1994 Finals:

“We had an opportunity to beat Houston, and I’m not gonna say it was the game where John (Starks) missed those shots. I think that the third game — our first game back at home — Sam Cassell hit a huge 3 to propel them to the victory, but we were right there to get that game, and if we had gotten that game I thought that the series would not have gone back to Houston and we would have won. But it is what it is. Take our hat off to Hakeem [Olajuwon] and those guys, they did a great job.”
Patrick Ewing on Sam CassellMay 6, 2021New York Post
“It’s hard because I lived through it and a lot of the stories they are talking about, I have already known. It’s great . . . Michael Jordan was and is a great player -- one of the best things to happen to the NBA.”
Patrick Ewing on Michael JordanMay 11, 2020Newsday
