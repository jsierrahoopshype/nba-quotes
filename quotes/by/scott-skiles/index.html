Skiles on Udrih's position:

“By anybody's measure, most franchises, most coaches, most GMs would say Beno is a point guard. That's the type of game he plays. But that doesn't mean he can't do other things. The way the game is played now, there are so many opportunities where teams are looking to put one big man in the game and four versatile guys. Beno is a high-level player. There are times where both of our guards (Jennings and Udrih) or three point guards are going to be in the game together.”
Scott Skiles on Beno UdrihJun 28, 2011Milwaukee Journal-Sentinel
