Hassan Whiteside:

“Pat Riley personally came out on the court and worked me out. It was the hardest workout of my life, still to this day. He had on a suit, hair slicked back, collar shirt. I’m in there, breathing like I’m about to black out before I stop this workout. It was not skill; it was just full court dunks. He was like, ‘Lick your hand and touch as high as you can on the backboard.’ I must’ve done at least 120-something dunks. Pat Riley knows talent. He wanted to see if he could break me, see how mentally tough I was.”
Hassan Whiteside on Pat RileyAug 27, 2024YouTube

Hassan Whiteside continued.

“Unfortunately he got injured [in the past]. But I’ve seen his work ethic. He’s going to continue to keep getting better. I think [the trade] shocked a lot of people. Me and Blake are with the same agency. I believe him when he says he was actually shocked about it. It’s good he’s with a team that he feels like he can move on with. Even Avery Bradley and them guys going to the Clippers. The thing about trades is somebody has to want you. [The Pistons and Clippers] felt like it was even.”
Hassan Whiteside on Avery BradleyFeb 2, 2018Miami Herald
