Gordon Hayward:

“When I was in Utah, I had this marketing scheme. I play League of Legends. I put out a tweet or an Instagram about being the best, like I can beat anybody one-on-one in the NBA. I can beat LeBron one-on-one, but I was teasing, saying in League of Legends. We happened to play them like a week afterwards, and I got a text from somebody in Cleveland saying, 'Dude, why'd you do that? LeBron's talking about it. Why would you piss him off out of all people?' We went and played the game, and I went back and forth with him and ended up hitting a game-winner against them at home in Utah. So that was a pretty cool feeling.”
Gordon Hayward on LeBron JamesAug 8, 2024YouTube
“So I said don’t worry about this one at all. We all know the type of player that you are. Some days are diamonds, some days are stones, and I think it’s something where it’s his first game and those things happen. But I have ultimate confidence in LaMelo,I think he’s going to be a great player in this (league) for many, many years.”
Gordon Hayward on LaMelo BallDec 24, 2020The Athletic

Gordon Hayward on Brad Stevens, his former college coach:

“Brad’s whole career has basically taken situations where it’s like, ‘He’s not going to do that well because of this or that,’ and then he somehow finds a way to do way better than people expected. This is just another one of those scenarios. He’s just a master at putting guys in the right positions. Like, if somebody’s not good at coming off pick-and-rolls, don’t have them come off pick-and-rolls. That sounds simple, but he’s really good at it. If we have somebody that’s a really good cutter, put him in a play where they cut to the rim, and they’re not going to be able to guard that.”
Gordon Hayward on Brad StevensDec 12, 2017Boston Globe
