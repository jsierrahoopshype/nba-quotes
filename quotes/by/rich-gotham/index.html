Best quotes

Celtics Team President Rich Gotham on Jayson Tatum’s recovery process:

“He's gone from walking around in a boot and just kind of wiggling his toes as a rehab to, as you see him today, he's walking around a little more freely. That's obviously very encouraging to all of us at the Celtics. He’s such a special guy, such a special player, but also a special person. So, we’re just all in his court, rooting for him to get back, come back. And seeing him progress, it’s fun. It’s funny, when you go out to our practice facility, like, ‘Oh, he’s walking today. He doesn’t have a boot on today.’ Little milestones in what’s a pretty grueling process for him, but it’s great to see him out at something like this and give him back to the community.”
Rich Gotham on Jayson TatumAug 8, 2025x.com
