Reinsdorf:

“If I interview someone and they're not sold on Billy (Donovan), they're not sold on a Hall of Fame coach, they're not sold on a person who's won championship in college, who's gone deep in the playoffs with Oklahoma City, who I believe, with the Chicago Bulls, every year, given the team that he was given, I think he achieved really good results. Not the results we wanted, but that's not because of Billy. So if someone's not interested in Billy as our coach and if Billy wants to be our coach, and someone's not interested in that, then they're probably not the right candidate for us.”
