What Jim Boeheim has said about other NBA players and coaches

6 quotes from Jim Boeheim about 4 other people around the NBA, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

6 quotes·2012 to 2014·Latest quote Oct 9, 2014

Best quotes

Said Boeheim on The Herd with Colin Cowherd:

“I’ve always thought Michael Jordan was the best player that I’ve ever seen. I always have and and I didn’t think it was close. I’m not so sure any more. And I love Michael Jordan. I’m not so sure anymore. This guy is 6-foot-9 and 260 pounds, and he’s getting better. He works on his game. His shooting is getting better. He’s a phenomenal, phenomenal basketball player. I love this game, I love the history of this game. I know we’ve had great, great players through the years. He’s like Magic Johnson with Michael Jordan-type skills as well.”
Jim Boeheim on Michael JordanAug 15, 2012San Antonio Express-News
“He did what he can do. He played very well the final game. Everybody's killing him but Tyson Chandler just didn't try to catch the ball. He threw him the ball and Tyson Chandler went like this (Boeheim dodged in a chair in his office in the Carmelo K. Anthony Center). He was wide open. He should have been looking for the ball right here. Kenyon Martin should have been looking for the ball. They both went like this (Boeheim dodged again). Carmelo gets turnovers and the announcers aren't smart enough to even think, 'Well, the guy should try to catch the ball.'”
Jim Boeheim on Carmelo Anthony, Tyson ChandlerMay 20, 2013The Post-Standard
“I think Carmelo wants to play in New York. He wanted to come there. I think he likes playing in New York. I think he would stay in New York if he's got a chance to win.”
Jim Boeheim on Carmelo AnthonyFeb 5, 2014Newsday
