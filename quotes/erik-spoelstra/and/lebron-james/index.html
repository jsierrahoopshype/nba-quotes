Asked whether getting James going in late-game situations will be a priority for Game 6, coach Erik Spoelstra said,

“We’ll see. There’s obviously going to be some priorities in terms of closing out games, which we’ve been very good at the last 2 1/2 months and particularly during our playoff run. We haven’t been able to do it consistently enough in this series. And that’s something we’ll address. That’s been a part of our fabric all season long, being able to work and improve things. LeBron was much more aggressive tonight. Obviously, he had a triple-double and he had an impact on the game.”
Erik Spoelstra on LeBron JamesJun 10, 2011Sporting News

Spoelstra said the Heat is starting to learn itself, and learn how opponents are countering.

“And we have three of the best attackers in the game, in Dwyane, LeBron and Chris, but that initial attack is often so crowded, regardless of even personnel,”

Spoelstra said he has since learned that James wants to be coached and held accountable.

Erik Spoelstra on LeBron JamesMay 29, 2011Yahoo! Sports
