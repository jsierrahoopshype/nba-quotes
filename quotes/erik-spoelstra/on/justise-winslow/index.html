1 quote

Spoelstra said after Miami’s win in Phoenix.

“To work our rotation a little bit best in their strength zones is something I want to look at. Nothing is in cement right now, but I thought Justise gave us some really rock-solid minutes on both ends of the court. Defensively he was guarding one though four, literally, at different points in the game. And offensively, he showed a real nice poise with the ball in his hands, but also making some plays off the ball, when other guys were making plays. This is definitely something I hope we can build on with him.”
Erik Spoelstra on Justise WinslowNov 9, 2017Palm Beach Post
