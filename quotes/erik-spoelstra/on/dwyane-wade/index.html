Best quotes

Spoelstra said:

“It is actually a good comparison because if you get in those pressure moments and the moments of truth, if you’re on the other side, would you ever, like, want to just give Dwyane Wade an open 3? You would not because he’s a killer. Like, he’s gonna, like, seize that moment. Jimmy has a lot of those same qualities.”
Erik Spoelstra on Dwyane WadeApr 20, 2022The Athletic

Spoelstra said of Toby Keith's As Good as I Once Was.

“And I truly believe that about Dwyane. He might not be able to play the role that he did for us in 2008-09, playing 40 minutes and then I would ask him to play the best player and defend on the other end, as well. But for 20 to 25 minutes, I think he can be every bit of who he used to be, just in compact minutes.”
Erik Spoelstra on Dwyane WadeMar 7, 2018South Florida Sun-Sentinel

Erik Spoelstra said of the Timberwolves star.

“It’s hard not to look at Anthony Edwards and not see Dwyane Wade in him. And I’m not a guy for comparisons. But the way he moves, the way he competes, the way he electrifies a crowd, that reminds me of No. 3. I have told Anthony that, and I feel like I’m going back in time.”
Erik Spoelstra on Dwyane Wade, Anthony EdwardsAug 23, 2023EuroHoops.net
