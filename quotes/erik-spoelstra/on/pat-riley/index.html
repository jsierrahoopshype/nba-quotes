Spoelstra told The Vertical.

“Look, I’m not naïve. I know that if it didn’t get better, [team president] Pat [Riley] might have had to do his job in a different way. I’m very aware of that. But there was never a discussion about not playing this guy, or games are not meaningful. That’s just not us.”
Erik Spoelstra on Pat RileyMar 28, 2017Yahoo! Sports

Spo, on not making a trade before the deadline:

“This is an organizational philosophy since Pat [Riley] got here. You see it through. Each team has a life span to it. We haven’t seen it through [with this group]. We feel we have a great opportunity for these 27 games & playoffs”
Erik Spoelstra on Pat RileyFeb 10, 2023Twitter @flasportsbuzz
