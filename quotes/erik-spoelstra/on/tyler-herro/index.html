Erik Spoelstra on Tyler Herro's decision to take a three instead of taking the transition layup in the final seconds:

“Here’s what I’ll say is Tyler has a fearlessness that you can’t take for granted. He’s hit so many big shots. He wants to take on anything to help you win and he’s had a lot of big plays offensively, especially in fourth quarters in his time with us. And it’s my job now to also coach him in situational basketball.”
