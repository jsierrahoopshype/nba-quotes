Best quotes

Steve Mills:

“We believe it was the smart move. But primarily because over the summer, Scott and I sat down with KP's brother (Janis Porzingis, Kristaps' agent) and talked about the different options that we have in front of us as it related to his extension. We were clear that the best option to make the Knicks a better team long term and have a better group of guys that KP can grow with long term was to not do the extension at this point.”
SMSteve Mills on Kristaps Porzingis, Janis PorzingisOct 17, 2018ESPN
“When we were going through this coaching search, every coaching candidate that we interviewed, they wanted to have an opportunity to coach Kristaps and that was a big thing about why this job was one of the more attractive jobs on the market. So as coaches see the opportunity and what he can become as a player, I believe players start to see the opportunity of who he is and who he would be like to play with.”
