Mark Jackson:

“If he gets the opportunity to coach a legitimate team, you're going to win some games. Coaching a team that isn't good is more challenging. If given a strong team, he'll win games. It's about the opportunity you get. Steve Kerr turns down the Knicks job to coach the Warriors;he's a brilliant basketball mind and has done an incredible job. If he had taken the Knicks job, his career as a coach wouldn't be where it is today. He's been nothing but great to me, and I respect him. He'd tell you it's about the job that he took propelled him to greatness.”
MJMark Jackson on Steve KerrMay 12, 2024YouTube
