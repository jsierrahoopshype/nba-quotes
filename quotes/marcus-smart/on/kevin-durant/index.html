Oh No He Didn't: Marcus Smart on playing with Kevin Durant:

“I get to be on the other side of him taking the shots and not taking the shots over me...his ability to make shots it doesn't matter who's there. And it also doesn't help that he's close to seven foot, and I'm close to six foot. And he just shoots right on top of me, so to be able to pass him the ball and go, okay now I get to actually watch the shot and not have to worry about trying to make a miss... I think that's gonna be the fascinating part to be able to watch him do what he does best”
Marcus Smart on Kevin DurantSep 22, 2026x.com
