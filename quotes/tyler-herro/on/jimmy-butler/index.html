Tyler Herro, on what he will remember about his time with Jimmy Butler:

“I think it was a great five or six years to be able to play with a guy like that. I came in as a rookie. He took me under his wing. “I took some things from him and reinvented my own way to help lead this organization in my own way. I thought it was a great five, six years of what we were able to accomplish, obviously not being able to win a championship with him. It’s a new chapter and we feel like we are headed in that direction again.”
Tyler Herro on Jimmy ButlerFeb 7, 2025Miami Herald

Herro told me in an exclusive interview.

“It may look a little different, but we have young guys that we hope will continue to get better. Bam Adebayo and I are young guys who are starting to become full-fledged veterans in this league. Terry Rozier is healthy now and ready to compete. Then you have Jimmy Butler, who is extra motivated after not being able to play in the playoffs last season.”
Tyler Herro on Jimmy Butler, Terry Rozier, Bam AdebayoAug 20, 2024Forbes.com
