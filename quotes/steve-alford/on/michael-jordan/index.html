And Alford, a man without a lot of regrets, says that one of his few take-backs in life would have been to end his Olympic experience with a suitcase full of Jordan stuff.

“Had I known he was going to go from ‘M.J.’ to ‘Air Jordan,’ I would have had him sign everything that I got in the Olympics,”
Steve Alford on Michael JordanMay 16, 2020USA Today Sports
“I was 19 years old and a complete gym rat. The pick-up games (were competitive). Just practicing against Alvin Robertson, Vern Fleming, obviously Jordan; it was just an amazing group of guys that I got to practice against, from a guard standpoint, every single day. “I told Alvin years later, it's the only time that I’ve ever done any kind of hiding in practice, (if) I knew coach (Indiana and national team coach Bob Knight) was going into a drill where you were going to get matched up against somebody.”
Steve Alford on Michael Jordan, Bob Knight, Alvin Robertson, Vern FlemingMay 16, 2020USA Today Sports
