What NBA players and coaches have said about Colin Kaepernick

7 quotes about Colin Kaepernick from 6 players, coaches and executives, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

7 quotes, 6 of them from players·2016 to 2020·Latest quote Jun 9, 2020

Best quotes

Stephen Curry:

“I respect everybody’s voice, everybody’s platform, and their opportunity and right to protest what they feel in their heart is something they want changed. I’ve said that plenty of times about Colin (Kaepernick). I respect what he’s doing. I respect the message that he’s fighting for, and I hope all the spotlight is on that particular message and the things we can do to make changes that are blatantly obvious we need change, so I hope going forward it’s not about who’s raising their fist, who’s kneeling, who’s standing, who’s doing this or that. It’s about what Colin and other guys – what the message is, and what we don’t want to stand for any more.”
Stephen Curry on Colin KaepernickSep 27, 2016USA Today Sports
“Colin Kaepernick is standing up for what he believes in. That’s what makes our country so great, right? You have the luxury to do so. He was unapologetic about it and, in his defense, I don’t think he was trying to disrespect anyone. I think he was trying to get his point across. I’m all for anyone who wants to do that. As athletes, we have this huge platform. A lot of people are watching at all times. Sometimes it may not be what you do, they like. But if you feel like it’s gonna be impactful, that’s on you. I feel like everyone should stand up for what they believe.”
Kevin Durant on Colin KaepernickSep 9, 2016San Jose Mercury-News

Joe Freeman:Damian Lillard:

“I think what Colin Kaepernick is doing, it takes a strong person. He’s standing up for what he believes in, and rightfully so.”
Damian Lillard on Colin KaepernickSep 26, 2016Twitter @BlazerFreeman
