What Jerry Reinsdorf has said about Jerry Krause

Every quote from Jerry Reinsdorf about Jerry Krause in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

6 quotes·2017 to 2022·Latest quote Mar 19, 2022

Best quotes

“If he was scouting a game, he didn’t arrive at game time. He would arrive during the warmups. He wanted to see what players did before the game if it was basketball. If it was baseball, he wanted to be there for batting practice. He also never let his opinions be colored by other scouts. “It’s fairly common in both sports for scouts to be buddy-buddy. They hang out together. They talk about what they’ve seen. But Krause didn’t want anything to do with the other scouts for two reasons: one, he didn’t want to tell them anything, and he didn’t want anything they believed to color his own opinion. It was very important to him that he formulate his own opinions, not be colored by somebody else.”
JRJerry Reinsdorf on Jerry KrauseMay 20, 2020The Athletic
“We had won five championships at that point, and Krause came to me and said: ‘Look, I'm not sure I can guarantee — I don't know how strong I feel about winning a sixth championship. ButI have a chance to trade Scottie Pippen to the Boston Celtics for two very high picks (No. 3 and 6 in the 1997 draft),’”
JRJerry Reinsdorf on Scottie Pippen, Jerry KrauseMar 19, 2022NBC Sports
“And Krause, if we had done that deal, he was going to take Tracy McGrady and he was gonna take Ron Mercer. “I thought long and hard about it, but my decision came down to this: What would our fans want us to do?’And I concluded that our fans would want us to win a sixth championship, even if it meant we weren't going to be that good afterwards. And that's why I vetoed the trade. I never heard from Michael Jordan about it. If Krause heard anything from Michael Jordan, he would've had to have told me about it.”
JRJerry Reinsdorf on Michael Jordan, Tracy McGrady, Jerry KrauseMar 19, 2022NBC Sports
