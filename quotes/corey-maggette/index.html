Best quotes

Cuttino Mobley:

“There were times, man, where I'd score like 15 first quarter, 12 first quarter, and then I’d feel that—and I have to give the ball up. You know what I’m saying? Like, I always wanted to be part of the group, never lead the group—until I started getting older. Then I was fine with leading, but letting everybody else shine. That was my role with the Clippers—with Elton Brand, Corey Maggette, Shaun Livingston, and Sam Cassell.”
Cuttino Mobley on Corey Maggette, Elton Brand, Shaun Livingston, Sam CassellOct 5, 2025YouTube

Cuttino Mobley:

“And people don’t understand, if you look at my numbers for that season with the Clippers, I could have been an All-Star that year. But again, I don’t care. I don’t care because Elton Brand was an All-Star. Corey Maggette averaged 20. We made the playoffs. We beat the Denver Nuggets in five. It was the first time the Clippers had ever been out of the first round. That was my All-Star.”
Cuttino Mobley on Corey Maggette, Elton BrandOct 5, 2025YouTube
