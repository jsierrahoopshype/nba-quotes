Rivers said before practice on Wednesday.

“I remember them saying it two years ago when [former Clippers guard] Chris [Paul] went out. … They said it was going on before I got here. And then when I got here, I would just hear little jokes about it. Obviously, it’s still jokes. You can go down to any team and they have injuries. It’s part of sports. It is what it is. The only place where it really applies to me is the past three years in a row with the playoffs. I went out last year. Blake was out. Chris went out. It’s not funny. But it’s like, ‘Jesus, what is going on?’”
Austin Rivers on Chris PaulNov 30, 2017The Undefeated
