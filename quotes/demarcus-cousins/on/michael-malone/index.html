Best quotes

Jason Jones:DeMarcus Cousins on Michael Malone hiring in Denver:

“I was extremely happy. I think he’s one of the better coaches in the league. He’s a great man. I learned a lot from him,and I’m just happy he’s getting an opportunity to do what he loves. Like I said, you can’t keep a good man down.”
DeMarcus Cousins on Michael MaloneJun 18, 2015Twitter @mr_jasonjones

Cousins on Michael Malone:

“[He's] extremely aggressive, I’d say that. He has a mind for the game and I like that. He knows what he wants. He knows what he wants from his team. Defensive minded – something that hasn’t been around here a lot. I think he’s the right coach for us.”
DeMarcus Cousins on Michael MaloneSep 30, 2013Cowbell Kingdom

Cousins said of losing Malone.

“It’s kinda hard. I think it’s a lot more emotional on the team this year, because so many guys believe in Malone and his system and this is so unexpected.”
DeMarcus Cousins on Michael MaloneDec 16, 2014Cowbell Kingdom
