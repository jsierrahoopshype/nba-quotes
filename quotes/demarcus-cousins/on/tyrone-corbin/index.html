Marc Stein:DeMarcus Cousins tells ESPN:

“I don't fire coaches or hire them. Everyone knows I liked and respected Coach Malone. I didn't want that to happen. I'm not involved in any coaching decisions. I've heard that George Karl is a great coach. If that is the direction the organization chooses, I'll support it. Out of respect for Coach Corbin, I hadn't planned on making any comments about what is out there. But at this point I felt some things needed to be clarified”
DeMarcus Cousins on George Karl, Tyrone CorbinFeb 10, 2015Twitter @ESPNSteinLine

Cousins said in a statement released by his agents.

“I just hope they make a decision soon and stick with it. George Karl is an experienced, proven coach and if that is who they chose to coach this team, I will support it. I do not like all these discussions in the media while we have a coach in place. It is a distraction and not fair to Coach Corbin and this team.”
DeMarcus Cousins on George Karl, Tyrone CorbinFeb 10, 2015Yahoo! Sports

Jason Jones:DeMarcus Cousins on Corbin's status:

“I didn’t know. I didn’t know officially until last night either. That’s when a lot of us found out.”
DeMarcus Cousins on Tyrone CorbinDec 30, 2014Twitter @mr_jasonjones
