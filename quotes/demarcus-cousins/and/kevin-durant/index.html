When Nichols answered with the Dallas Cowboys and the New York Yankees, Cousins responded by saying, with a laugh,

“Well, put us up there. I would say the Warriors are for sure. You know they hate Kevin [Durant]. They hate me. I think the only guy they really like is Stephen [Curry]. Actually, they may hate him as well. They hate Draymond Green. But I mean, it is what it is. I can't worry about that.”
DeMarcus Cousins on Kevin Durant, Stephen Curry, Draymond GreenJan 17, 2019ESPN

Except … upon more reflection, Durant realized he actually picked the wrong team.

“Obviously, the best team I played on was the Warriors. I want to say the 2019 team was crazy, but we got hurt. With Cousins at the five, that’s probably the best team I’ve been a part of on paper.”
Kevin Durant on DeMarcus CousinsAug 27, 2026Yahoo! Sports

Kevin Durant on if GS having more fun w/Cousins and 9 straight wins:

“I mean, we always have fun. Just because we lose a few games don’t mean s--- is over for us. Just like any team, you go through stretches. But you add another All-Star to the equation, things are gonna change.”
Kevin Durant on DeMarcus CousinsJan 26, 2019Twitter @SteveBHoop
