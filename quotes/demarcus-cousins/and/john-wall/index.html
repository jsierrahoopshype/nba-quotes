Wall said.

John Wall on DeMarcus CousinsNov 29, 2016Washington Post
“You can tell him that, he won’t get mad. And I think now he’s trying to change his ways but when you do it. He’s trying to put that behind him but, it’s kind of tough. John Wall on DeMarcus CousinsFeb 20, 2017FoxSports.com
