Asked what reports the Magic have received about Fultz’s ability to shoot without pain, Weltman responded,

“We have to get him in here, and we have to get our arms around him. We have to see for ourselves. I really think that anything that you don’t experience yourself is just speculation. We have to get our own people working with him and understanding what’s going on. I have full confidence in our coaches, in our performance staff, in our support staff to give Markelle everything he needs to succeed here.”
JWJeff Weltman on Markelle FultzFeb 8, 2019The Athletic

Magic president of basketball operationsJeff Weltman said tonight:

“Markelle Fultz was the number-one pick in the draft two years ago, and it wasn't a fluke. I think the whole league had him there. So we believe that, at 20 years old, the sky's the limit for him.”
