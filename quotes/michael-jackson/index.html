Thaddeus Young:

“His first day, I remember his locker was right next to mine in the practice facility. I come in, all his sh*t is all over my locker. I look at him, he looks at me, he's like, 'What, punk b*tch [expletive]?’ I'm like, 'You know what? I'll sit over here. This your locker room, champ. You got it.' There's nothing I can do or say. Then another day he came in, he hit this old ass iPod. At this point, iPods had been touchscreen. Now, he got the one with the little spin on. Only thing he has on his iPod is all Michael Jackson songs. That's all he listens to is Michael Jackson songs. At that point I knew I was like, he can't do no wrong.”
Thaddeus Young on Michael JacksonSep 3, 2026YouTube
“He would teach me what he did: How to make a ‘Thriller’ album, a ‘Bad’ album, all the details that went into it. It was all the validation that I needed – to know that I had to focus on my craft and never waver. Because what he did – and how he did it – was psychotic. He helped me get to a level where I was able to win three titles playing with Shaq because of my preparation, my study. And it’s only all grown. “That’s the mentality that I have – it’s not an athletic one. It’s not from [Michael] Jordan. It’s not from other athletes. “It’s from Michael Jackson.”
Kobe Bryant on Michael JacksonNov 23, 2010Yahoo! Sports
