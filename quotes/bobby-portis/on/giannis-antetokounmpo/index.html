Best quotes

Bobby Portis on getting traded with Giannis to the Miami Heat:

“It’s fun being able to still play with Giannis. We have a great relationship on and off the court. It’s kind of cool going to a new situation with somebody that you’re super familiar with. I think that’ll make my transition real smooth. — I rock with Gianni. Gianni a good dude. He always showed love to me since day 1. — I remember after we won the chip the first thing he told our GM and owner ‘We gotta sign Bobby back! We gotta sign Bobby back!’”
Bobby Portis on Giannis AntetokounmpoJul 28, 2026x.com

Bobby Portis:

“Last time I got on here, I said it was relieving that he’s talking in a way that shows he doesn’t want to leave, and that we can get a breath of fresh air in general. I think there was so much pressure on our team this year,with so much outside media about Giannis leaving the Bucks—him requesting a trade—or people pushing that narrative, and it gets to the guys on the team. Guys feel that pressure, especially playing with the Bucks, playing with an all-time great. There are always narratives about winning—whether the guys around him are the right guys, and whether the organization is still the organization for him. So much goes into it.”
Bobby Portis on Giannis AntetokounmpoFeb 24, 2026YouTube

Bobby Portis on being a Heat fan his whole life, and conversations he had with Giannis about coming to Miami:

“I’ve been a Heatle. I’ve been rocking with the Heat since the Mavericks and the Spurs in the finals. Ray Allen in the corner, Chris Bosh offensive rebound, pass out to the corner, bow! Everybody thought it was over with the ropes out. The conversations I had with Giannis has just always been about winning. Where can we go to win? How can we get another championship? Once you guys talk to him at 3 you’ll understand all he cares about is winning.”
Bobby Portis on Ray Allen, Chris Bosh, Giannis AntetokounmpoJul 16, 2026x.com
