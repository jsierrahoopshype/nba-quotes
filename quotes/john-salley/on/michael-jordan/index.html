John Salley:

“MJ came out in 1984 and David Stern became the commissioner, and he said that, ‘Michael, you and I are going to change the world.’ So part of the reason he’s the messiah and they don’t talk about anybody else is because that’s part of it. He is the greatest I played against, but there were some bad people that came before — but they will never get that shine.”
JSJohn Salley on Michael Jordan, David SternApr 15, 2025YouTube

1 quote

John Salley on why he thinks that Isiah Thomas is so disrespected by so many people in the league:

“Because it was a false narrative. Isiah took the fall when they said that he didn’t want to give the ball to Michael [Jordan],which when you’re watching that game he gave the ball to Michael the whole time. Michael and I had the same agent and at times he wanted me not to like Isiah. That was his personal thing. But there’s no reason for it to be like that. I just don’t understand because he is really a good guy.”
JSJohn Salley on Michael Jordan, Isiah ThomasAug 29, 2023YouTube
