Ime Udoka on Alperen Sengun potentially improving this season as a free throw shooter and defender:

“I've never really taken Alperen (out of a game) due to free throws. Defensively, that's a different thing, obviously. At times, you want to be able to switch and have favorable matchups on that end. [Improvement] will keep him on the floor, as it does a lot of guys, but we're very specific with who we sub at the end of games specifically for offense and defense. The better he is, the more he will be out there.”
IUIme Udoka on Free throws, Alperen SengunOct 15, 2024Twitter @clutchfans

Mitty: Ime Udoka on why Alperen Sengun being out is not the reason for Jalen Green’s resurgence:

“We wanted to really increase the pace and get the threes up and all those things don’t have anything to do with Alpi- When Jalen was struggling earlier in the year he had the same quality looks and Jalen started to read the game better - I think they can complement each other very well”
