Wade:

“I guess the kind of team you’re on. I guess whether you’re playing good basketball or not. I think it’s easy for Tim Duncan to come in every year because they have a very good team. He knows his role could be limited and he wants to be around what they’re doing. Team is important. Health is important — if you’re not hurting every time you get on the floor. And family would be the third thing. It depends how your family’s feeling. You give a lot of time away from your family, for sure. So are you being too selfish by continuing to keep going or do they still want you to go? Those would be the things.”
Dwyane Wade on Tim DuncanMar 22, 2016Palm Beach Post
