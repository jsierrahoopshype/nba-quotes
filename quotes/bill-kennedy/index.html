Best quotes

Statement from Rajon Rondo:

“Yesterday, I said that my words toward Bill Kennedy were unacceptable and did not reflect my feelings toward the LGBT community. Some have interpreted my comments as a non-apology. I want to be clear, from the bottom of my heart that I am truly sorry for what I said to Bill. There is no place on or off the court for language that disrespects anyone's sexual orientation. That is not who I am or what I believe and I will strive every day to be a better person.”
Rajon Rondo on Bill KennedyDec 15, 2015NBA.com

Silver told Yahoo’s Adrian Wojnarowski.

“But, while Bill Kennedy was known to be gay by many people in the league, Bill Kennedy had never made that very, very personal decision to publicly come out and announce to the world that he was a gay NBA referee. So, I have to say, in the back of my mind, I was concerned about that. “It did not seem appropriate to me that I should, by virtue of a bad act by Rajon Rondo, out Bill Kennedy.”
ASAdam Silver on Rajon Rondo, Bill KennedyDec 19, 2015Larry Brown Sports

Kings Majority Owner Vivek Ranadive on Rajon Rondo's comments:

“Rajon's comment is not reflective of the culture of the Sacramento Kings organization or the world we want to live in. He's apologized, and has been suspended by the NBA. On behalf of the team, I apologize to Bill Kennedy for any harm this may have caused.”
VRVivek Ranadive on Bill Kennedy, Rajon RondoDec 14, 2015Twitter @SeanCunningham
