Josh Lewenberg:Dwane Casey on the passing of Rasual Butler, who he coached with the Raptors in 2011-12:

“I just saw him this summer in LA. We talked at length about him getting into coaching. He was a veteran leader for us. That is why he stayed in the league so long. He left us way too soon.”
