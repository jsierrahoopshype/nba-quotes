Dwane Casey on Andre Drummond’s (likely) upcoming free agency:

“Andre understands and knows who he is, what he means to us, how important he is to our program, where he is in his career, where he stands in the league. I try to stay out of the contract part of it. I’m there for him. My thing is, I want every one of our players to be rewarded, but the only way you’re rewarded in this league is by winning. I look back to Toronto and see Kyle Lowry with $100 million or whatever it was. That’s the same conversation I had with (Lowry). I hope that (Drummond) gets every penny that is coming to him, but the only way we accomplish that together is through winning. … That’s the basics of contract talks. I don’t get into that other stuff.”
Dwane Casey on Andre DrummondSep 26, 2019The Athletic

Dwane Casey on the Andre Drummond trade:

“It was as much of a business decision as anything else. The direction we were going in, the direction he needed to go in from an individual standpoint. I know Tom (Gores) loved Andre, our whole organization loved Andre.”
Dwane Casey on Tom Gores, Andre DrummondDec 26, 2020Twitter @omarisankofa

Dwane Casey on Blake Griffin, Ed Stefanski, Reggie Jackson, Andre DrummondJun 10, 2018Booth Newspapers
