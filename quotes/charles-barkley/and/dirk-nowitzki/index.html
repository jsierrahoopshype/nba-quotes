Eddie Sefko:Charles Barkley on Dirk Nowitzki at an exhibition in Germany:

“I’m like, who is this guy here? So I say, who the heck are you? And he told me he’s Dirk Nowitzki and he said: I’m going in the army. I said, right, you’re 7-feet tall. You’re not going into the army.”
Charles Barkley on Dirk NowitzkiApr 9, 2019Twitter @ESefko
