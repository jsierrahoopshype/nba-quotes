Karl-Anthony Towns:

“I would say the person that's toughest is probably going to be like DeMarcus Cousins or Steven Adams. I've probably in my life ever had to tag the roller. You try to slow down the roller so it makes it easier to deal with the pick and roll, the player, two offensive players and the two defensive players. It makes it easier and gives them more time to figure out the situation with the PNR.”
