Rudy Gobert on Nikola Jokic tonight, specifically in the 3rd quarter:

“He was in the zone. I mean, a couple shots I think I actually blocked and the ball went in. It was just incredible in that quarter. He put the team on his back and he was making everything. Drawing fouls, getting to the line. So, definitely one of those stretches that we’re going to watch the film and I think there are things we should have done better, but there’s also plays he made that I think are tough to overcome.”
Rudy Gobert on Nikola JokicMay 15, 2024Twitter @DaneMooreNBA

Nikola Jokic on the Timberwolves:

“When Tim Connelly made that trade (for Rudy Gobert) everybody was laughing at him and what he was doing. But he made a great team. I think he deserves credit for doing that.”
Nikola Jokic on Rudy Gobert, Tim ConnellyMay 3, 2024Twitter @HarrisonWind

Eric Walden:Rudy Gobert, on Nikola Jokic's game:

“He got it going early. I should have done a much better job. He scored way too easily, especially in the first half. … I take full responsibility for that. The team relies on me, and I’ve got to do a better job.”
Rudy Gobert on Nikola JokicJan 31, 2021Twitter @tribjazz
