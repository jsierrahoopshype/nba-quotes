Trae Young on John Collins being traded to Utah:

“It's definitely tough seeing him go but at the same time like you know this is the business. JC knows that too like we've had conversations over the years and we're so close that we noticed in the business if we don't win things can change in a heartbeat. Me as a teammate I loved playing with JC I've throwing a lot of lobs to him he's made me look really good a lot. I've always appreciated JC and I wish him nothing but the best.”
