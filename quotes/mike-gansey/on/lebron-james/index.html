Best quotes

Gansey on LeBron before today's BxW scrimmage:

“With LeBron, I just think there's no superlatives or words you can say what he does He set the tone from day one, really, when he got here, and he's the first one in the building, last one to leave. I mean, the guy is the biggest professional I've ever seen. Like just his routine, his habits, and I think some of our guys are seeing that, obviously. It's like, well, if LeBron's the first one in here today, you know, what am I doing? I'm a young guy coming in at 10 o'clock, where he's in here at 7:30. So I think it's rubbing off on a lot of the guys, and they see it, and hopefully, it just continues that way.”
MGMike Gansey on LeBron JamesOct 3, 2026x.com

president of basketball operations Mike Gansey told The Athletic.

“LeBron James and his camp have kept quiet, and there has been speculation for different teams. But we’d obviously love to have LeBron. I respect the heck out of him. In my opinion, he’s the best player of all time. I think his decision is dragging out because he has a lot of good options and he wants to figure it out. He would obviously add a lot to our team. But at the end of the day, I know he’s going to make the right decision for him and his family.”
MGMike Gansey on LeBron JamesJul 14, 2026New York Times

Oh No He Didn't:Sixers president Mike Gansey on signing LeBron James:

“Without getting Jaylen, I don't think we get LeBron. We just said, hey, let's try to put a full court press: everyone reach out, sell him on Philly. I think if he comes he could be the missing piece and we could be really good”
