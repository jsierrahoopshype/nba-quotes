Best quotes

Pau Gasol:

“I don't think that Howard re-signing with the Lakers puts me out ot the team for sure, but I guess the chances would grow. I know the Lakers are pushing hard trying to keep Howard with a spectacular campaign in Los Angeles, putting banners in key spots... I hope they do the same for me next season when I become a free agent... I doubt it.”
Pau Gasol on Dwight HowardJul 2, 2013Catalunya Radio
“That wasn’t anything that was on my mind. Like I told you guys in the beginning, this is a great time for me to learn from a guy like Kobe, a guy like Steve Nash and Pau Gasol, and that’s what I intended on doing. I’ve learned so much from the beginning of the season until now that it’s going to help me moving forward in my career. So, this has been great. Everything that Kobe is doing, everything that Pau and Steve and those guys are doing will help me in the long run as far as taking a team over the hump.”
Dwight Howard on Pau Gasol, Steve NashMar 18, 2013ESPN.com
“I think they will try to get Dwight and extend him. That's why they brought him here. That's the team's base for the future. I don't think everything is set in stone in the sense that if he stays, I'm definitely gone or if he goes, I'm definitely here. Ownership has to answer that.”
Pau Gasol on Dwight HowardApr 28, 2013Sulia
