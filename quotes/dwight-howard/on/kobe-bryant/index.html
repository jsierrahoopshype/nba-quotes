Dwight Howard on his relationship with Kobe Bryant:

“We’re not best friends but I would say that we understand where we’re at. We’re pretty much on the same page as far as what it’s going to take for us to get to the next level and for us to win. I have to bring that energy and effort every night and because I’m in better shape, I’m able to do that for longer periods.”
Dwight Howard on Kobe BryantMar 23, 2013Sports Radio Interviews

Dwight Howard:

“Who said we [him and Kobe Bryant] fought? We never ever fought. So for the people to say that... It's just ridiculous.”
Dwight Howard on Kobe BryantMar 4, 2013CBS Los Angeles
“I told [Bryant] as soon as I got here, 'Hey, I want to be one of the greatest to ever play. I want you to push me every day,'”
Dwight Howard on Kobe BryantOct 9, 2012SI.com
