Dwight Howard on Shaquille O'Neal:

“It’s got to be like a one-sided beef because I ain’t never had no beef with this man. Maybe because when we were playing against each other, I was probably joking and laughing while doing some stuff, but not like that. I don’t know, maybe he wore the Superman logo better, that’s why he’s mad. But everybody can be whatever doggone character they want to be. I wasn’t trying to be Superman because of him or copycat something he was doing. People called me Superman because I jumped over my teammate in practice and was doing real-life Superman stuff. It wasn’t because I wanted to call myself Superman like him.”
Dwight Howard on Shaquille O'NealSep 28, 2024YouTube

Josh Robbins:Dwight:

“He's mad about 'Superman.' I didn't know he made it up. I didn't know Superman came from Shaquille O'Neal.”
Dwight Howard on Shaquille O'NealJan 20, 2012Twitter

Dwight Howard on Shaquille O'Neal:

“Why do I have to ask Shaq to wear a cape for a dunk contest? It’s not like Warner Brothers told me I couldn’t do it. He doesn’t own the damn cape. I’m just wearing it for the crowd, for the fans. That’s what I was doing. He’s not mad at me for wearing a Superman suit, right? Because I don’t need his permission. Imagine if I had to ask Michael Jordan for permission to wear his socks while playing a pickup game with my kids at home. That’s ridiculous.”
Dwight Howard on Shaquille O'NealSep 28, 2024YouTube
