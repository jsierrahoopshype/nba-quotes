“It’s what makes this issue so tricky, is that great players — Steph (Curry) and Tim Duncan, Wemby — they’re not only team-changing, but they’re franchise-changing, for even beyond the scope of those guys’ careers. “And so there’s only a handful of players that can do that, that are that valuable. And so teams are all clamoring for them. Sometimes, you don’t know who they are. Steph was the seventh pick. Giannis (Antetokounmpo) was the (15th) pick. So it’s not always the first couple guys, but more often than not, the first pick has an opportunity to be that guy, and that’s what creates this issue.”
Steve Kerr on Stephen Curry, Tim DuncanFeb 10, 2026New York Times
