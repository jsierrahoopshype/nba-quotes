Wes Goldberg:Steve Kerr on the Brad Wanamaker addition, who played last season for the Celtics, who they play tomorrow night:

“I love Brad. He's a guy who I just trust whenever I put him out there. I just trust him. And it was that way from day one.”
Steve Kerr on Brad WanamakerFeb 1, 2021Twitter @wcgoldberg

Drew Shiller:Steve Kerr on Brad Wanamaker going 4-for-26 from deep the last 15 games:

“I know Brad is frustrated. I feel his pain. Really hard role to play, but it's one you sort of have to learn how to play. Brad is way too good of a shooter to not eventually get going.”
Steve Kerr on Brad WanamakerFeb 9, 2021Twitter @DrewShiller

Drew Shiller:Steve Kerr on Brad Wanamaker:

“I watched him quite a bit while he was in the bubble. I really, really liked his game -- admired his toughness and his stability ... I could see him even playing with Steph. I like his demeanor. He's just a real pro.”
Steve Kerr on Brad WanamakerDec 9, 2020Twitter @DrewShiller
