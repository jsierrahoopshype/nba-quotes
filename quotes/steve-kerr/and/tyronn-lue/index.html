Best quotes

Mirjam Swanson:Ty Lue tells @TomerAzarly:

“I like Steve Kerr. I do, I really do. He's always checking up on me ... very positive, motivating guy. don't cheer for too many guys, but I do cheer for Steve...a genuine good person. He is one of my friends in coaching, which is pretty rare, for me.”
TLTyronn Lue on Steve KerrNov 28, 2021Twitter @MirjamSwanson

With two off days before Cleveland finishes this Western trip Saturday in Utah, coach Tyronn Lue arranged for his team a tour of Napa Valley, Calif.

“Steve Kerr came up to me before the game (on Christmas) and was like, 'man, I heard you guys are going to Napa. That's really great,'”
TLTyronn Lue on Steve KerrDec 27, 2017Cleveland Plain Dealer
