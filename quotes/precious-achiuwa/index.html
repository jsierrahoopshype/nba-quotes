Wade said on NBA TV’s draft broadcast.

“Bam’s taken a beating down there. So, to have somebody that can come in and kind of play the same way . . . he’s a young Bam. Coming in, Bam couldn’t shoot early on, even though he was real athletic, great going to the rim, a great shot blocker. With the Heat’s ability to put the work in with these players and teach them the NBA game, (Precious) can develop into what Bam has now developed into. So I love this pick for us because it’s something that we needed, and something Bam needs to continue to stay at that All-Star level.”
Dwyane Wade on Precious AchiuwaNov 19, 2020The Athletic
