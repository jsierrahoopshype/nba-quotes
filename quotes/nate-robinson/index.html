What NBA players and coaches have said about Nate Robinson

5 quotes about Nate Robinson from 5 players, coaches and executives, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

Tony Allen:

“It was no greater feeling—I ain’t even gonna lie— than to beat Doc. Like I say, man, I told a lot of websites this, a lot of podcasts this. You know, I was in Boston before I came to Memphis. Like I said, I asked Doc and Danny Ainge, 'Hey, man, give me a third year.' They wanted to re-sign me to the same deal, but they put a lot of belief in Nate Robinson—shoutout Nate Robinson. But after winning the championship and going to the finals, you know, I thought I was embedded in the team. I thought I was family to the Celtics, you know what I’m saying?”
Tony Allen on Danny Ainge, Nate RobinsonNov 24, 2024YouTube

Kevin Garnett:

“The team with Shaq, Bib Baby, Kendrick Perkins, Jermaine O'Neal, Nate Robinson... That was probably the most fun I've had since I've been in the league... That team was a character. [...] There was never a dull moment in that locker room. I wish we got Shaq earlier in his career... That was a cool team.”
Kevin Garnett on Kendrick Perkins, Nate Robinson, Jermaine O'NealSep 10, 2012YouTube
“I watched it. I'm not Nate Robinson. I think I have a little more experience than Nate. I've been training stand-up and boxing for 8-10 years,”
Deron Williams on Nate RobinsonNov 17, 2021ESPN
