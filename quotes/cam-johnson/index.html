Jacque Vaughn on the possibility of Cam Johnson seeing expanded opportunities as a ball handler:

“I think it’s going to be a balance with him. We do need his elite shooting. I think if [there’s] one thing we lean on, we need that. We need the space that he provides for us. Literally, if he can shoot 10 threes a game, I would love that. I’d take him out to dinner every single night. He’s a 40% career 3-point shooter... But at the end of the day, I think he’s growing as a player, and you saw that at the end of the year: His ability to play pick-and-roll, he’s big, he can get downhill, he can play fast. So a lot of good things ahead for him.”
JVJacque Vaughn on Cam JohnsonOct 4, 2023Twitter @erikslater_

Sean Marks on Cam Johnson's free agency:

“I don’t have a crystal ball. I don’t know. I think it’s one of those things that maybe we should expect the unexpected. Things happen all the time in this. We’ll be prepared for that. Cam knows how we feel about him. we hope he's a Net.”
Sean Marks on Cam JohnsonJun 23, 2023Twitter @erikslater_
