Steph Castle on Carter Bryant:

“For him to be a rookie and come in and there's no dropoff defensively. That alone says a lot. For him to come in and give us a breather and defensive rebound for us and try and make minimal mistakes is big because we need those minutes. In the playoffs, every possession matters. So, when he's doing that and making it tough on (SGA) and tiring him out, in the long run, it definitely helps.”
Stephon Castle on Carter BryantMay 28, 2026x.com
