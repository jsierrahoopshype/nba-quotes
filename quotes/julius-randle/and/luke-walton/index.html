Best quotes

Luke Walton:

“A lot of that's not going to be answered until they get here and we see them play and what they can do and who they can work well with. And even that takes time. Maybe Isaiah and Julius [Randle] develop a great two-way game or [Kyle] Kuzma–- you just never know. So I'm not going to spend my time too focused on that right now as opposed to getting ready for OKC.”
Luke Walton on Isaiah Thomas, Julius Randle, Kyle KuzmaFeb 8, 2018ESPN
“And I believe in Julius and I continue to challenge him and some nights when he doesn't play big minutes, it's because other guys are rolling and some nights it's because I don't believe he is playing up to the standard that he set for himself. I want him to feel and realize the difference in those nights as part of his growth as a player... For me a lot of times we want him running, we want him playing fast, we want him running into screens. He is such a dynamic roller when he does it that he collapses a defense with how he can move around the court.”
Luke Walton on Julius RandleDec 29, 2017ESPN
