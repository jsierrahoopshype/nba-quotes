Giannis Antetokounmpo on Erik Spoelstra:

“I think he is a great coach. The little interactions I have had with him, he is an amazing human being. I am excited to play for him. I had a very different perception of him while I was being on the other team. I felt like he was very militant and very tough and maybe bordering like an asshole and pushes his players. But now I feel like he is very supportive, caring, honest. They say that he lets you go, he lets you be yourself and play and do what you really do. He fills you up with confidence.”
Giannis Antetokounmpo on Erik SpoelstraOct 17, 2026YouTube
