Best quotes

Giannis on Khris Middleton, the closer, in part:

“I want to be a winner. I have the whole game to be ‘the guy.’ I don't care about being the guy in the fourth quarter. I trust Khris to death. If Khris asks for the ball, better give him the ball.”
Giannis Antetokounmpo on Khris MiddletonJun 28, 2021Twitter @malika_andrews

Giannis Antetokounmpo on Bucks’ All-Star presence:

“I’m really proud of Khris [Middleton], Pat Connaughton, @BucksBango. We did a great job of representing Milwaukee. A few years ago we won 15 games and now we’re one of the best teams in the league.”
Giannis Antetokounmpo on Khris Middleton, Pat ConnaughtonFeb 17, 2020Twitter @BenGolliver

Giannis Antetokounmpo on a convo with Khris Middleton:

“I told him the day you retire is going to be the toughest day in my career because I’ve been with you the whole time, and it’s been an unbelievable journey. He’s a great team guy, from where we started and where we are.”
Giannis Antetokounmpo on Khris MiddletonJun 28, 2021Twitter @MikeAScotto
