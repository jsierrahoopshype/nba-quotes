Thanasis Antetokounmpo:

“When we first started, me and Giannis used to make like 300 euros combined,but this was third division. We are a small country, but we have a big, big, big tradition when it comes to sports. You know, those years we played against you guys—that '04-'05 team—actually shaped the way we look at basketball. I don't think me and Giannis play basketball if it's not for seeing all these guys play against you, seeing Papaloukas, Baby Shaq playing.”
