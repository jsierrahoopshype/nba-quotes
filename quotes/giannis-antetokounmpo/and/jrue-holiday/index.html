What Giannis Antetokounmpo and Jrue Holiday have said about each other

Best quotes

Jrue Holiday:

“I feel like the way that I play doesn’t necessarily appeal to fans outside of Milwaukee fans or fans outside of and that know me, you know what I’m saying? I don’t high fly, dunk over people, dunk on people like Giannis. And then people don’t really find defense that pretty or notable.”
Jrue Holiday on Giannis AntetokounmpoJan 29, 2022Milwaukee Journal-Sentinel

Jrue Holiday on Giannis Antetokounmpo:

“I’ve never seen him take a game off. I’ve never seen him take a practice off. I’m telling you, there were times—matter of fact, remember when Giannis got hurt in that Atlanta series, during the Eastern Conference Finals? He was out for about a week or whatever. Once we got to the Finals, the practice before the Finals was the first day he actually practiced with us. I’m talking about, he’s going 150 miles an hour—taking off, dunking, going all out. We’re like, 'If you don’t slow your ass down, we don’t need you for today. We need you tomorrow!' But that’s just the type of mentality and hunger that Giannis has every single game, literally in everything he does on the court.”
Jrue Holiday on Giannis AntetokounmpoDec 3, 2024YouTube

an NBA scout told HoopsHype.

“Milwaukee’s perimeter defense got worse, but what Dame will give them offensively will be much better. I think Dame’s been overburdened with ball-handling in Portland, and so was Giannis in Milwaukee during critical playoff moments. Now, you have two guys who can handle the ball and create for themselves at pivotal times to win big games. Dame will get shots off the ball he hasn’t gotten before when he's playing off Giannis.”
Jrue Holiday on Giannis AntetokounmpoSep 28, 2023HoopsHype
