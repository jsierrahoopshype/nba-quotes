Marcus Morris on Jaylen Brown:

“We are talking about one of the guys that carried this team, that has been the longest tenured Celtic on the team. I had the privilege to play with him and Jason Tatum and watch these guys grow up. This is a mature young fellow in this league, he is in his prime right now. I don't know if you can actually go out on the market and find a better player that plays defense and offense at the highest level, being a great two-way player. He is a leader, he is a motivator, he is the definition of a Celtic.”
