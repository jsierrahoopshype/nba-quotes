What Marcus Morris has said about LeBron James

Every quote from Marcus Morris about LeBron James in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

4 quotes·2018 to 2019·Latest quote Jul 22, 2019

Best quotes

“Me just leaning on LeBron for 15 seconds that possession, I think that helps. It’s me being physical, anybody being physical, I think that helps toward the entire game. You’ll look back at the first quarter and, hey, he scored 21 or whatever it was. But then in the fourth quarter you look and, hey, he worked for every basket he got. You know what I mean? And that’s what we’re trying to do. Just limit the easy work. And then, shit, 40-something minutes a game — everybody’s human.”
Marcus Morris on LeBron JamesMay 18, 2018The Athletic

Morris has seen the speculation surrounding him turning down the Clippers’ three-year offer, but makes it clear that this was his decision and that he wanted to go through his process.

“All this stuff that (Paul) didn’t want me to go to the Clippers and didn’t want me to go against LeBron, that’s not true,”
Marcus Morris on LeBron James, Rich PaulJul 22, 2019The Athletic

Marcus Morris on the chance to defend LeBron James in playoffs after getting a taste a couple years ago in Detroit:

“I’m a little older, a little more experienced. Personally, I think I’m probably the best guy defending him in the league, outside of Kawhi.”
Marcus Morris on LeBron JamesMay 12, 2018Twitter @ESPNForsberg
