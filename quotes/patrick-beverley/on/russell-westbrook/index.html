Best quotes

Patrick Beverley:

“I signed a three-year $40 million contract with the Clippers. I turned down a three-year $50 million with Sacramentojust to stay with the Clippers. For me to be there, all the great guards they've had, I was part of that starting five that helped the Clippers go to the first time ever Western Conference Finals. They've had great guards. They've had James Harden, they had Russell Westbrook, they have Chris Paul, they've had Rajon Rondo. They had a plethora of point guards that just can never help them get over the hump, but I was part of that starting five that helped them finally, first time ever, and they haven't been back since, get to the Western Conference Finals.”
Patrick Beverley on Chris Paul, Rajon Rondo, James Harden, Russell WestbrookSep 9, 2026YouTube
