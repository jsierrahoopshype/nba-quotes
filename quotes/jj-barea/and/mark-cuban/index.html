Mark Cuban on J.J. Barea:

“If we can just get back Jet (Jason Terry) we’ll be set. J.J. became available and they got a hold of us and said, ‘Look, we think we’re going to get waived. Would you guys have an interest in him coming back?’I talked to coach, talked to Donny, and it wasn’t an easy decision because we really like Gal. He improved dramatically and we think he’s got a real spot in this league but coach was like, ‘Look, being able to go small with J.J., with Raymond out initially, it’ll be a good compliment.’ So ‘who let the baby on the court’ is back.”
