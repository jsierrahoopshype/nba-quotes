What NBA players and coaches have said about Andy Elisburg

4 quotes about Andy Elisburg from 4 players, coaches and executives, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

Coach Spo says Andy Elisburg was in his ‘bag’ this offseason and speaks on acquiring Giannis:

“Pat had his vision, but Andy Elisburg went to work this summer. That was him being in his bag. — I still think about it. Giannis chose us. It’s surreal seeing Antetokounmpo in a Miami Heat Jersey but I’m sure it’s surreal for him also. — He really wanted to trust a place that would be dead ass serious about trying to win just like he is.”
Giannis Antetokounmpo on Andy ElisburgSep 30, 2026x.com

Lowry told Sixers Wire of the Heat.

“I have admiration for these coaches, the players, the ownership group, Pat (Riley), Andy Elisburg, like I have admiration and love for those guys. Those guys gave me an opportunity to go to East Conference finals, go to a finals, get a house here, have my family settled. Like I said, I cheer for them except for when I’m playing against them.”
Kyle Lowry on Pat Riley, Andy ElisburgApr 4, 2024Sixers Wire

Neff said of player development.

“They’re the gold standard. I don’t see how you can turn down Miami considering the pipeline, the amount of attention — starting at the top with Pat Riley, [general manager] Andy Elisburg, and most of all [vice president/basketball operations] Adam Simon — that they have shown with this kind of player. All things being equal, I pick Miami, because they will be the best at it, them and Toronto. “When Miami shows interest, you listen and they were the first one to show interest. It gets your attention. Great system. They train their coaches. I had Briante Weber there. If I could pick any place for a G-League guy to go, it would be Miami.”
BNBill Neff on Pat Riley, Adam Simon, Andy Elisburg, Briante WeberJan 10, 2020Miami Herald
