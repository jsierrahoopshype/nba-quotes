Tristan Thompson:

“I got to give a big shout out to Kevin Love. Kevin Love put it together. If you had to look at your bingo card and guess who would put it together back then, you would have said Kevin Love. Kevin Love has been in Miami. He has been taking that leadership role and he has done the group chat. So I am like, shout out to Kevin Love for doing it. He put his mind to it, got his team together, and we all showed up. It was amazing.”
Tristan Thompson on Kevin LoveJul 6, 2026YouTube

Love said at Wednesday’s shootaround.

“I would run through a wall for him. He’s the best. Just the type of guy he is, he’s a lifelong friend of mine, so it would be tough to see him go. Obviously I would like to have him around selfishly, but if there’s a better situation out there for him, going to make him happier, have a chance to win a championship, then so be it. I only know Tristan Thompson as a Cavalier and I like it that way.”
Kevin Love on Tristan ThompsonFeb 6, 2020The Athletic
