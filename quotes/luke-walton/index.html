Larry Nance Jr. on the job Luke Walton is doing and whether there’s anything to the suggestion that he has lost the locker room, as LaVar said:

“I think Luke is great. What you hear about a lot of NBA coaches is that they’re bad at communicating and stuff and he’s the _total_ opposite. He’s always pulling guys aside [to help us], talking to us and getting out in front of any potential problems that may arise. I love him as a head coach. I think he’s great, and I think he’s going to be great. I stand firm in my belief in him.”
Larry Nance Jr on Luke WaltonJan 27, 2018audioBoom

Larry Nance Jr. on LaVar Ball’s negative comments about Luke Walton and whether they discuss it as a team:

“I mean, every now and again [we talk about it], but it’s more so just in passing. It’s not like we’re ever sitting around like, ‘What are we going to do about this?!’ No. He’s a grown man and he can say what he wants. Luke is a grown man and he can deal with it. We’re all grown men, individually. As players, it’s not necessarily our job to deal with it, worry about it or even think about it. We pretty much leave it alone. Of course we all see it, but he’s got the right to an opinion just like everybody else does.”
Larry Nance Jr on Luke Walton, LaVar BallJan 27, 2018audioBoom

Lakers general manager Mitch Kupchak told USA TODAY Sports.

“There’s no doubt (Walton is) in command. He’s in charge. I wouldn’t say he’s easygoing. He knows what he wants, and he’ll enforce whatever it takes to get what he wants to get on the court. “He’s got a little bit more of an edge than I thought. I knew, as a competitor and as a player, he had the edge. But he does have a little bit more of an edge as a coach than I thought he would’ve.”
MKMitch Kupchak on Luke WaltonNov 8, 2016USA Today Sports
