Best quotes

During Simmons’ podcast, Oakley continued:

“[Jordan] always praised Scottie, always praised Scottie. He never talked down about him, but I don’t know what happened since ‘The Last Dance.’ It seems like things just — he probably said,‘OK, Scottie said this stuff about me. OK, I’m not gonna say nothing.’ He’s not gonna say nothing back in the press, none of that, try to go back to Scottie. He’s just gonna keep doing what he’s doing — play golf, fish, relax and smoke cigars.”
Charles Oakley on Michael Jordan, Scottie PippenJan 28, 2022New York Post

Charles Oakley:

“Everybody got to see a different side of Michael Jordan, if you didn't see the Hall of Fame speech. In the speech he pointed out a lot of people who were waving their finger at him, so he got the last finger point. The Last Dance is the same thing, he got the last say. If you mention Michael Jordan, you can’t have the first say, you’d better have the last say.”
Charles Oakley on Michael JordanDec 1, 2021Betway.com
“Most games went down to two, three possessions. Y’all made shots. We didn’t. The best player won. Michael was a better player than Patrick hands down.”
Charles Oakley on Michael Jordan, Patrick EwingMay 4, 2020New York Post
