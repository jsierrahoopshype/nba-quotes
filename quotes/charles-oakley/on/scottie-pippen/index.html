What Charles Oakley has said about Scottie Pippen

Every quote from Charles Oakley about Scottie Pippen in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

During Simmons’ podcast, Oakley continued:

“[Jordan] always praised Scottie, always praised Scottie. He never talked down about him, but I don’t know what happened since ‘The Last Dance.’ It seems like things just — he probably said,‘OK, Scottie said this stuff about me. OK, I’m not gonna say nothing.’ He’s not gonna say nothing back in the press, none of that, try to go back to Scottie. He’s just gonna keep doing what he’s doing — play golf, fish, relax and smoke cigars.”
Charles Oakley on Michael Jordan, Scottie PippenJan 28, 2022New York Post

Oakley explained to Betway.

“We don't know, but one day we will find out. I don't think Scottie would just turn [to] all this from ‘The Last Dance.’ Some stuff happened 10-20 years ago, but now he’s got a platform to talk about it. But he did say he wants to have his last say about ‘The Last Dance,’ so who knows what's going on?”
Charles Oakley on Scottie PippenDec 2, 2021basketballnews.com

Charles Oakley:

“Scottie felt like he was mistreated, and he didn't look good. Scottie did some things that we talked about. I’m friends with Scottie and Mike, but I don't talk about Mike to Scottie, or Scottie to Mike.”
Charles Oakley on Scottie PippenDec 1, 2021Betway.com
