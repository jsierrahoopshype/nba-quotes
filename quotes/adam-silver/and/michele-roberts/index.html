Adam Silver on CBA deal talks with Michele Roberts:

“We continue to talk all the time. I think Michele Roberts and I both have the same goal which is to avoid any sort of work stoppage. And we know one of the ways to avoid a work stoppage is to talk early and often. And we're doing that.”
