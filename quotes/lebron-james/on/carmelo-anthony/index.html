Best quotes

“I can only give my advice and words of encouragement (to Carmelo). It’s like if you’re trying to comment on somebody else’s marriage. You’re not with them every day. You don’t know what they’re going through. You don’t where the communication broke down, so you can’t comment on that. That’s what (journalists) do. I just want the best for him. And I want him to be happy playing the game he loves. If it’s here great. If it’s not, then great as here as well.”
LeBron James on Carmelo AnthonyFeb 4, 2017New York Daily News
“Melo is top 3 in our league as far as scoring. You look at KD, you look at Steph as well. Russ, those guys, James Harden. Carmelo is right up there with them. I just try to make it tough. Those guys, you just try to make it tough. You contest they shots and you got to live with the result. They’re going to make some and hopefully they miss a few as well.”
LeBron James on Carmelo AnthonyNov 14, 2015Akron Beacon Journal
“But I know what I see from the outside looking in. We all had to work through out setbacks. I know that's what [Anthony] and those guys are trying to do, just like we had to do.”
LeBron James on Carmelo AnthonyMar 25, 2011ESPN.com
