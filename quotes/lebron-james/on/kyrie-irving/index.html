What LeBron James has said about Kyrie Irving

Every quote from LeBron James about Kyrie Irving in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

“I’ve been speaking to Kyrie Irving for the last three years, just giving him the ins and outs about how to approach the game. I think he’s a great talent, and if the Cavs pick him he’s going to be a great pick for them. He’s solid — solid point guard, true point guard and also can score. In this day and age in the N.B.A, everyone’s looking at it like a point guard’s league. You got these young great point guards — D-Rose, Westbrook and all these young, athletic point guards that do so many great things. I think Kyrie Irving is really good.”
LeBron James on Kyrie IrvingMay 19, 2011New York Times
“The last couple years, Kyrie Irving obviously being as great as he was in the fourth quarter, we kind of picked our games. There were games half the time that he had it going and, ‘Hey, go get [it].’ There were games half the time where I had it going and I’d go get it. “But right now my teammates look at me and they’re like, ‘OK, like this is your quarter. You’ve done this your whole career. Let’s try to make some things happen.’ It’s very important that I try to come through for them.”
LeBron James on Kyrie IrvingNov 24, 2017Akron Beacon Journal
“About how it’s time to bring him back in the league. “Maybe he just said it because he believes and there’s nothing else to it. I can tell you around the league and even within the Lakers, some corners of it, those comments were read as LeBron opening the door to…at least opening the door to, ‘Hey, if you all are comfortable with it and all the blowback it would be, and the Nets are willing to just get out of the business now of Kyrie Irving so we can get him for much cheaper, not both picks, maybe one pick.'”
LeBron James on Kyrie IrvingNov 11, 2022Lakers Daily
