Extended thoughts from LeBron on JJ Redick:

“I think whatever happens, JJ will continue to grow. Thought he had a hell of a rookie campaign ... it’s a hell of a lot harder being a rookie head coach coaching the Lakers. A whole other ballgame. That’s one thing for sure - we have a great, up and coming coach that will be great for many years.”
