LeBron on Anthony Edwards:

“That was the best thing about our team. You know, we had the vets—myself, Steph, KD, AD, Jrue Holiday—and then you had the younger guys, Tyrese Haliburton, Anthony Edwards, Devin Booker. Just to see the excitement that Ant-Man played with, showed off the floor, he wanted to compete against everybody. You probably saw him want to compete versus the Team USA ping pong team. He said he could beat people in swimming, you know. He wanted to compete versus everybody. And just to see that electric and that energy from the young guy, and for us as the older guys remembering at one point having all that energy, it was definitely a blessing for sure to be around.”
LeBron James on Jrue Holiday, Devin Booker, Anthony Edwards, Tyrese HaliburtonSep 21, 2024YouTube
