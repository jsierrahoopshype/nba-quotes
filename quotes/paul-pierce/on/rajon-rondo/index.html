What Paul Pierce has said about Rajon Rondo

Every quote from Paul Pierce about Rajon Rondo in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

“That’s why the whole thing evolved like it did with us not talking to him. Ray didn’t really have the best relationship with Rondo anyway. That was nothing. [Rondo], me and Kevin, he didn’t have any talk with us [before his Miami decision]. I tried to call him and I didn’t get any return calls before he signed with Miami. That was our rival. We were brothers. We came in together. We just wanted a heads-up or a ‘what’s on your mind?’ or something like. Then, all of a sudden, he left. That was the biggest disappointment on my end. Not even getting a callback at that moment.”
Paul Pierce on Rajon RondoNov 2, 2016The Undefeated

Paul Pierce told the Herald.

“I mean, I think it just kind of spiraled out of control. “I didn’t see that coming. When they made the trade, I looked at Dallas as a real, real contender. Now, they had a guard who could run the show, defensively match up with the other guards of the West. “I’m not on the inside. I can’t tell you what happened, how it all came to that. I just want Rondo to be in a place where he can show people the player that he is, that he truly is.”
Paul Pierce on Rajon RondoSep 27, 2015Boston Herald
