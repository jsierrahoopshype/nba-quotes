What NBA players and coaches have said about Anthony Mason

7 quotes about Anthony Mason from 6 players, coaches and executives, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

“(Mason) was in the hospital. I think he was having some discomfort, some kind of chest pain. One of his guys said, ‘You have to have yourself looked at.’ He goes into the hospital and the whole event took place there. I spoke to family members, and had he been in the (hospital) lobby as opposed to the third floor, where he was, he would have died. Fortunately, he was close enough to the emergency facilities that were brought to bear and saved his life. It was touch-and-go, literally minute to minute, then hour to hour, andnow it looks like it's more day-to-day. He survived. That's the first thing,”
DCDon Cronson on Anthony MasonFeb 13, 2015New York Daily News
“You know why? I came in with Anthony Mason, Derrick Coleman, Eddie Jones. [They would tell rookies] 'You put the bags up. You better have the condoms. You better meet us.' That's what I came into.”
RDRicky Davis on Anthony Mason, Eddie Jones, Derrick ColemanApr 12, 2013Grantland

Jordan:

“I’m deeply saddened to learn about the passing of Anthony Mason. Mase was one of the toughest competitors of his era. While on the Hornets, he was beloved by fans throughout the Carolinas for his hard-nosed play and skill on the court. The Hornets organization extends its condolences to his entire family.”
Michael Jordan on Anthony MasonFeb 28, 2015Charlotte Observer
