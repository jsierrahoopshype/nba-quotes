What NBA players and coaches have said about Kareem Abdul-Jabbar

Best quotes

Richard Jefferson:

“There is no GOAT in basketball because there's multiple levels. Kareem Abdul-Jabbar I think had the greatest career, Bill Russell won the most championships, Michael Jordan is the icon, and I think Kobe Bryant and what he did mentally for the game and really for athletes all around the world. ButI would just say if you were to build a basketball player, you could not build a better basketball player than LeBron James. There's no skill set he doesn't have, there's no physical ability. So for me, if I was building a basketball player, mentally and physically, he would be the person that I would build.”
Richard Jefferson on LeBron James, Kobe Bryant, Michael Jordan, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Bill RussellSep 12, 2026YouTube

Who is your GOAT?Cooper:

“My GOAT is Kareem. He is an unstoppable player. You look at three things for a GOAT. One, can they score at any time at any point in the game? Kareem was unstoppable, even double or triple-teamed. If you triple-teamed Kobe, LeBron or Magic when they come across the floor, they’re going to pass the ball. But with Kareem, you couldn’t double or triple team him. He had the Skyhook. Next, a GOAT makes others around him better. Kareem made others better. No one other than Magic made others around them better when they were double or triple-teamed. For a GOAT, if you foul, will he make his free throws? Kareem shot in the 80s in crunch-time free-throw shooting. So that’s why Kareem is my GOAT.”
MCMichael Cooper on Kareem Abdul-JabbarJul 16, 2026Fade Away World

Jerry Stackhouse:

“I mean, from some skill standpoints, he’s probably better. He’s a better ball handler than Michael Jordan was. I think he’s a better facilitator than Michael Jordan was. Was he a better scorer than Michael Jordan? No. Was he a better clutch performer? No. Michael Jordan went to the Finals six times, won six championships, six Finals MVPs. So, to me, he’s always going to be the GOAT in my book. No matter what people might think, what might have happened in Washington, or where we didn’t see eye to eye, Michael Jordan is still the greatest player to ever put on a pair of basketball shoes in my mind. And I’m probably going to put Kareem after that.”
JSJerry Stackhouse on Michael Jordan, Kareem Abdul-JabbarMay 12, 2026YouTube
