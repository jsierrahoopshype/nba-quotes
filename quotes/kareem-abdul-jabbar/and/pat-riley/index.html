What Kareem Abdul-Jabbar and Pat Riley have said about each other

Kareem Abdul-Jabbar on Pat Riley, and Pat Riley on Kareem Abdul-Jabbar: every quote in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

5 quotes·2017 to 2026·Latest quote Feb 24, 2026

Best quotes

Abdul-Jabbar:

“I was genuinely pleased. It felt long overdue, and I couldn’t think of a more fitting tribute. Coach Riley played a pivotal role in our championship success—his leadership, vision, and unwavering commitment were instrumental to everything we achieved. We had great players on our team, but all with different personalities. And the hardest part of coaching is getting players to make a consistent effort. So I have great admiration for Pat’s ability, which led our team to five championships.”
Kareem Abdul-Jabbar on Pat RileyFeb 24, 2026Essentially Sports
“He’s a lot like me. The first time I got my Coach of the Year award I was embarrassed to go and get it because I got it with a team behind me of Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, Magic Johnson, James Worthy, etc.That residual reward will come to [Spoelstra]. The ones that he has that are more important to him are the ones that he’s wearing on his fingers.”
Pat Riley on Erik Spoelstra, Magic Johnson, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, James WorthyMar 9, 2017Palm Beach Post
“Training, travel, personal chefs, personal trainers, all that stuff has come into play since Kareem. I hope people realize Kareem’s story as well and how different it was. He went to college for four years; LeBron came out of high school. But they both dominated from Day 1. They both turned potential into greatness from Day 1.”
Pat Riley on LeBron James, Kareem Abdul-JabbarFeb 1, 2023Associated Press
