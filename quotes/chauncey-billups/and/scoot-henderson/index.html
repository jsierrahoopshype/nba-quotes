“Taking me out of situations where I’ve messed up a couple times. That’s his way of letting me know, ‘Take a deep breath and when you get back out there, turn it up a notch.’I appreciate it a lot, having a coach like that and a mentor like that. Seeing other players’ development, you don’t see that a lot. Having Chauncey on my side as a head coach is great to have.”

Chauncey Billups on Scoot Henderson:

“I didn't realize how good of a playmaker he was. I knew he was explosive and tough and competitive, but he has a feel about his game, he has a way of bringing you with him. For a young guy, that's not easy. He can get to his spots pretty easily. He can really guard. He'll learn a lot of tough lessons by the way he's so competitive. It's gonna be some tough nights that I'll have to let him go through, because that's how you learn. But he's been a dream, to teach him and watch film with him. It's been a lot of fun.”
Chauncey Billups on Scoot HendersonOct 2, 2023Twitter @highkin

Sean Highkin:Chauncey Billups on Scoot Henderson going into year 2:

Chauncey Billups on Scoot HendersonOct 3, 2024x.com
