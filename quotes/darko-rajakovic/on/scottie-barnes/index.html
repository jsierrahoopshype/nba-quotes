Darko Rajakovic on Scottie Barnes’ role:

“That’s going to be definitely work in progress. We cannot take away from his identity and what he brings to our team, which is hustle plays, rebounding, playing in the dunkers. He just needs to do a good job of recognizing when he needs to go and break the dunker and when he needs to be more in a pick and roll up high. I wish that he played a little bit with more force on offensive end to be honest with you. I’m used to seeing him playing more forceful, going to the rim, finishing above the rim. So that’s something that we got to continue working on. I like that he got nine rebounds and that he was active on the glass tonight.”
