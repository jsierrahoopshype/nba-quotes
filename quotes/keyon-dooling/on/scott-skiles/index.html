Keyon Dooling on Scott Skiles:

“During my time in Milwaukee, he had an average relationship with the players. I don't think it was great, I don't think it was bad. His work ethic, X's and O's and game-planning were his strengths. Where he fell short is maybe with people.”

Keyon Dooling:
