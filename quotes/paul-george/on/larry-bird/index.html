Paul George:

“Obviously, Anthony Edwards is super talented and is a huge talent going forward, but just how much the game has grown and how far you got to pay dues to those dudes that did it before us, and I think that's just where the translation between that era and his era…They don't really know too much about Magic Johnson and Larry Bird. Obviously, MJ is moving how guys move in this era today, so I think that was kind of like where he was going with it, as far as the movement and the explosiveness, the athleticism, and being able to contort your body.”
Paul George on Magic Johnson, Larry Bird, Anthony EdwardsSep 4, 2024BasketNews
