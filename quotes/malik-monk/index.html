Best quotes

Steve Clifford:

“With Malik Monk, then it became, ‘Do we play him over Jeremy?’ when (Lamb) is playing so well? For Malik to be an every-night starter, which he has the talent and potential to be, he’s going to have to play both spots. To be an every-night starter you have to play at both ends of the floor unless you can (score) 26 a night. You have to be able to guard your position. He is undersized, so to say he’s going to be a (shooting guard) every night against every player (is a stretch).I do think he can play both spots, but I don’t think it was in his best interest to use him the way that I did.”
SCSteve Clifford on Malik MonkFeb 19, 2018Charlotte Observer
