“What Leon did with the Knicks is impressive…He came in…ship was sinking…sunk…He wiped all that s**t out––front office, players…cleaned house. Imma bring in a cultural guy:[Tom Thibodeau]…hold you accountable…ain't gonna get no sleep…Now you get to mold your team the way that you want…build the pieces…draft picks…players Thibs really trusts…”
