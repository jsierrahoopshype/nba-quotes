Carmelo Anthony on Pablo Torre:

“You got to take the stand and tell us which owners. Say the owner of the XYZ teams are asking about LeBron’s transportation. F** all that journalism sh**. Like I respect the journalism, but if we're going to keep it a buck, keep it a buck. Tell us who the owners are. Because if we were saying, if a player was saying something about the owners, that owner would find out who the f*** was saying that sh**, and we would be in a situation where we could be f***ed up… It's investigating too much, but I've said this previously that you got what you got, and I don't want you to stop being a journalist, because you do great journalism and I respect you as a journalist.”
Carmelo Anthony on LeBron James, Pablo TorreOct 15, 2026Youtube

Anthony said.
