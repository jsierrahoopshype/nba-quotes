Best quotes

Carmelo Anthony on Pablo Torre:

“You got to take the stand and tell us which owners. Say the owner of the XYZ teams are asking about LeBron’s transportation. F** all that journalism sh**. Like I respect the journalism, but if we're going to keep it a buck, keep it a buck. Tell us who the owners are. Because if we were saying, if a player was saying something about the owners, that owner would find out who the f*** was saying that sh**, and we would be in a situation where we could be f***ed up… It's investigating too much, but I've said this previously that you got what you got, and I don't want you to stop being a journalist, because you do great journalism and I respect you as a journalist.”
Carmelo Anthony on LeBron James, Pablo TorreOct 15, 2026Youtube

Anthony said.

“Why can't we say, 'M.J. was very great, LeBron is very great, Kobe is very great.' We're not allowed to say those things today, because it's always this or that, and that's just our society -- you have to choose one.”
Carmelo Anthony on LeBron JamesMay 28, 2020CBSSports.com
“I can only give my advice and words of encouragement (to Carmelo). It’s like if you’re trying to comment on somebody else’s marriage. You’re not with them every day. You don’t know what they’re going through. You don’t where the communication broke down, so you can’t comment on that. That’s what (journalists) do. I just want the best for him. And I want him to be happy playing the game he loves. If it’s here great. If it’s not, then great as here as well.”
LeBron James on Carmelo AnthonyFeb 4, 2017New York Daily News
