Best quotes

Carmelo Anthony:

“I think that Chris Paul got to let it go. Meaning, don't even sweat it. Let them sweat on it. Let them decide that. Let them figure that out. Continue doing what you're doing. Yeah, anybody who have an opportunity to have their jersey hung up in the rafter is going to want their jersey in the rafter. I think we make it seem like it's just so easy to get your jersey in the rafter. No, it's not easy to get your jersey in the rafter. It's about what you leave in this organization, the impact, the effect, not just the wins and losses. How you made people feel around here in the organization, the community, the city. It goes deeper than just banners.”
Carmelo Anthony on Chris PaulOct 8, 2026YouTube

Chris Paul on what excites him most to play with Carmelo:

“A lot of things, Melo like a lot of the guys on the team always play like we have something to prove. And obviously the friendship, brotherhood that we share. It’s always nice to play with someone you’re close with.”
Chris Paul on Carmelo AnthonyAug 10, 2018Twitter @Rockets_Insider

Anthony told The Undefeated.

“I’m sure he thought it was going to be more. I think he thought there would be more — Rondo, Ingram, all of them involved in it. I’m actually surprised [Paul] got two.”
Carmelo Anthony on Chris Paul, Rajon Rondo, Brandon IngramOct 22, 2018The Undefeated
