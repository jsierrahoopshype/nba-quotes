Best quotes

“You enjoy the process. But ultimately, you make a choice that you think is best for you and your family. We obviously firmly believe that here is that place for Carmelo. And we’re going to do our best not to convince him or change his mind from maybe a decision that he’s already made but give him some confidence that with the personalities involved and our commitment to working hard to achieve success, that this is place where he can have what he wants. He wants to be on a great team and around great people that want to achieve greatness. He’s great. And he deserves that. And so that’s what we want to work to create with him. And hopefully we can do it.”
Derek Fisher on Carmelo AnthonyJun 10, 2014Chicago Tribune

Fisher said Tuesday after summer league practice.
