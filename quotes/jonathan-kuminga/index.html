Draymond Green:

“Before the first Pelicans game, it was announced that Kuminga was benched, and it was a complete overreaction. If not, they were just going to zone us, clog the paint, and make it hard for everyone. That’s not a knock on JK, if anything, it’s probably a knock on me and Trace. But the media tried to blow it up, like they always do. JK approached it with the right mindset, came out hard, and the lineup change actually sparked him. He had his best two games of the season so far because of it.”
