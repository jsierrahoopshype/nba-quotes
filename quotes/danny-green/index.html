Best quotes

Kyle Kuzma:

“I think what propelled us during that championship run in 2020 was just our friendship. Everybody got along, one through seventeen. That’s what really matters—the teams that get along the best are usually the best teams in the NBA. We all trusted each other, and we had a lot of smart, high-IQ vets, whether it was Danny Green, KCP, Dwight, or JaVale. We also had hungry guys like me and Caruso, who were young and ready to prove something. We had a squad that was built on trust and hunger.”
Kyle Kuzma on Danny GreenSep 11, 2024YouTube
“First and foremost, I want to thank Danny Green for everything he gave the Sixers organization over the last two seasons. He was a veteran leader, consummate professional on and off the court, and made us all better. I wish him continued success in his recovery and look forward to seeing him back on the court. Heading into the draft, we had a goal of strengthening our rotation and believe we accomplished that by acquiring De’Anthony Melton. He is a young, tough and talented two-way player who can impact the game on multiple levels. We think he will thrive here and we’re excited to add him to our roster,”
Daryl Morey on Danny Green, De'Anthony MeltonJun 24, 2022NBA.com

Curry told ESPN’s Nick Friedell.

“He wasn’t around. So I don’t know what [Green] meant by that. ‘Wasn’t cordial,’ I don’t know what that means, but the relationship was fine. He just wasn’t around so we didn’t see him a lot or we didn’t talk a lot. “But as far as now, he’s on my [team]. I went to battle with him last year, we did a lot of good stuff and I’m going to go to war with who I’m going to go to war with and who’s on my team, so I’m looking forward to getting back out there with him and doing good stuff.”
Seth Curry on Danny GreenFeb 24, 2022NetsDaily

I’m betting my money on Philly… Ws mean confidence. It’s not cool to be overconfident, but it’s very cool to be confident.
