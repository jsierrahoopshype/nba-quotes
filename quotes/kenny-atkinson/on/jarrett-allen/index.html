Best quotes

“I’ve known that since training camp that he’s going to contribute right away. I thought in the beginning [he’d be in] G-League. That was before we discovered who he is. I like his basketball IQ. I like his toughness. Love went at him twice and scored, and then [Allen] came back and blocked his shot. He didn’t back down. Yes, I think if he stays healthy, he’s going to be a contributor to this team.”
Kenny Atkinson on Jarrett AllenNov 24, 2017New York Post
“You always look back on it like, ‘Man, should we have (given a player more rest)?’ This is more of a contact thing (with Allen). The soft tissue is one of the ones where you’re like, ‘That’s overuse.’”
Kenny Atkinson on Jarrett AllenMar 4, 2026New York Times

The way Allen has played since returning to action — and the way the Nets have struggled for a solution at center — it’s apparent he’s going to have to start playing extended minutes, sooner rather than later.
