What Dennis Rodman has said about Michael Jordan

Every quote from Dennis Rodman about Michael Jordan in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

4 quotes·2013 to 2020·Latest quote Apr 20, 2020

Best quotes

OK, back to Rodman:

“It’s very easy to do what they define greatness [as now] -- Michael Jordan did it when it was tough, really tough. And what’d he do? Lead the league in scoring 10 years in a row. Ten years in a row, he led the league in scoring. Ten years in a row, back then, that was hard. Averaged like 32 points a game. That was hard back then. Now it’s easy. All these [expletive] triple-doubles … back then when he was getting his ass whooped, I mean beat down every game, and then when he played against us [the Detroit Pistons], he said -- guess what -- ‘I gotta go back in the gym.’ And he got tough. He got tough and he got great. And greater. So, that’s it.”
Dennis Rodman on Michael JordanApr 5, 2017CBSSports.com
“You know what, LeBron’s doing one thing that I always said that Michael Jordan never did,”
Dennis Rodman on LeBron James, Michael JordanApr 5, 2017CBSSports.com
“That's the only thing we had, smarter players. ...I would take Bosh out of his damn game easy. That's not even a problem. And how would Dwyane Wade match up with Scottie Pippen? Really? And Scottie Pippen would guard LeBron easy. And how are you going to cover those two guys (Jordan and Pippen)?”
Dennis Rodman on LeBron James, Chris Bosh, Dwyane Wade, Michael Jordan, Scottie PippenJun 7, 2013ESPN.com
