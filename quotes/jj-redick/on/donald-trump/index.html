What JJ Redick has said about Donald Trump

Every quote from JJ Redick about Donald Trump in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

6 quotes·2017 to 2020·Latest quote Jul 31, 2020

Best quotes

JJ Redick on Pardon My Take:

“I don’t think there was one moment. Doc used to always talk about how when one group was together for a long period of time,instead of getting closer together you end up pointing fingers at each other. It was weird because separately everybody was really cool with each other, off the court everybody sort of got along. And then, there was just so much pettiness, it was just pettiness. It’s weird to think what we had the potential to accomplish and what ultimately derailed that was pettiness. Like, Donald Trump-level pettiness.”
JJ Redick on Donald TrumpAug 24, 2018podcastone.com

Redick said in a later interview.

“I’m a voracious reader … and I’ve become sort of obsessed with Trump in the last six months. I don’t really speak about it, not because it’s not my place or I don’t have a voice — I do. But I would say this: There’s been a lot of jokes and side comments from people in the league about [White House Press Secretary] Sean Spicer and alternative facts and all that stuff, but I don’t think any of it is funny.”
JJ Redick on Donald TrumpFeb 3, 2017Los Angeles Times
