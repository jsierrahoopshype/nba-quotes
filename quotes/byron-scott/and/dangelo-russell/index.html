Lakers rookie point guard D'Angelo Russell said after Friday’s game.

“Coach [Byron Scott] was demanding we run it. It was kind of tough because we didn’t really get to run it against defense. We just talked about it, threw it in and then played with it. I feel like it’s a great offense, but it was kind of thrown at us.”
D'Angelo Russell on Byron ScottFeb 27, 2016ESPN.com
“At 19, he was a little bit more businesslike at practice and games. D’Angelo still has a playfulness about him. Sometimes in practice he’s joking around and losing a little bit of focus. But he’s 19. I understand that. Chris Paul was probably like 23 years old by the time he came into the league in his mental capacity. But like I said, each point guard, each guy I have, is different.”
