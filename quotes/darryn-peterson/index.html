Will Hardy:

“My job is to give Darryn (Peterson) a lot of feedback. I need to pick the moments appropriately and I need to prioritize what he needs to hear now versus what he may need to hear later (...) I have preached to our young players for years now that there is a difference between playing poorly and shooting poorly. I thought that Darryn a couple of times made really good choices and just missed. If those shots go in, maybe he feels differently. Darryn has been a great learner and he is willing to receive feedback. My responsibility, not just with him but with our whole team, is prioritizing what is important for them to hear tomorrow versus what can wait.”

Keyonte George on what impresses him about Darryn Peterson's game:

“I think just how fast he picks up on things. Obviously, in this league, it is split second. You can make that mistake and it is over. I think for him, we talk about different things whether it is picking roll, or iso spacing, or even little things with our jump shots. He picks up on it right away and he is always willing to learn. So I think that is the main thing, coming in as a high pick and still humble enough to still want to learn.”
Keyonte George on Darryn PetersonSep 21, 2026YouTube

3 quotes

Jazz owner Ryan Smith:

“We met with Darryn at the combine. Darryn was awesome in the interview. Darryn wanted to go number one. Everyone wants to go number one. But by the way, we had three different top guys come in. I don't even call it a workout. I call it dinner. I don't even go. I'm like, okay, I'll go to dinner with you because there's no sweat, there's no nothing. They're sitting down with Will watching film. They put on their jersey to check out the size and everything. They take a couple of pictures, they call it a workout, but no one's doing like the three-minute run to see how fast they can go up and down the court.”
RSRyan Smith on Darryn PetersonJun 25, 2026YouTube

DeMarcus Cousins on Darryn Peterson:

“Obviously, he deserves a chance to get drafted, and I don't think teams are going to hold everything that's happening for this young kid against him. But it does bring in some concern. It will make a team wonder or multiple teams wonder about personality, character, and things of that nature. Everything I've ever heard about this kid is he's a gym rat, super hard worker, good kid, come from a good family. But when there's a lot of smoke around your name, it's easy to get lost in the sauce and narratives can become true. I just want this kid keep his name clean, keep his nose clean, just put your head down to do the work.”
DeMarcus Cousins on Darryn PetersonJun 17, 2026YouTube

Bill Self on Darryn Peterson checking himself out:
