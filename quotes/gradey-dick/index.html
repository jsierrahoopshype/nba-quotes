Best quotes

He kept an eye on Dick early in the year when he was struggling, but realized soon enough the kid was going to be all right.

“You’re always concerned about rookies, in general, because they’re going through so many changes. But with Gradey, his attitude never changed, his joy never changed, and his mindset never changed, in terms of being a happy-go-lucky 19, 20-year-old kid. It was never like he was sulking or quiet, there was none of that,”
Garrett Temple on Gradey DickApr 11, 2024SportsNet

Darko Rajakovic on Gradey Dick:

“Overall, he’s getting more comfortable playing NBA minutes, going against NBA personnel. He’s finding his spots on the court where he can score. Obviously when he’s on the court together with Kelly [Olynyk] with the second unit, the ball just finds him. So, he’s able to do a good job offensively there. Defensively, it’s still a work in progress. There are good moments, there are good stuff there, but there is also recognition of the personnel and who’s on the court and how he needs to guard. And there is no other way to learn those things, but to go through experience and to get to know.”
DRDarko Rajakovic on Gradey DickFeb 24, 2024TalkBasket

Josh Hart:
