Jimmy Butler on his former teammate Bam Adebayo scoring 83 points:

“Good for him. 83 is a lot of points, and he is a crazy, talented player. He can do everything incredibly well. But it’s always a team effort. Nobody just gets scored 83 on. It’s a team thing, you know, you’re sending double teams. (And a ref thing?) Could be. Double teams, triple teams, whatever. The whole, whatever you call it, shebang, the whole team, the whole book, whatever. And he made shots, a lot of them. So good for him. 83 is historic.”
Jimmy Butler on Bam AdebayoOct 16, 2026x.com
