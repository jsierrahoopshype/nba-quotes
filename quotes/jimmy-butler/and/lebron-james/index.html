Best quotes

LeBron on playing against Jimmy Butler:

“Love it. Love it. One of the best competitors we have in our game. ... I don't know how many more opportunities I'm going to have so to be able to go against a fierce competitor like that.”
LeBron James on Jimmy ButlerOct 4, 2020Twitter @taniaganguli

Heat’s Jimmy Butler on Finals:

“It’s been like this for a very long time:If you want to win, you’ll have to go through a LeBron James-led team... That’s what it normally comes down to... You’re going to get the same test over & over until you pass it. That test is LeBron James.”
Jimmy Butler on LeBron JamesSep 28, 2020Twitter @BenGolliver

Butler, on if LeBron James remains his toughest assignment:

“You're not gonna pull me into that. Because if I say he is, then the next time I play Kevin Durant, he's going to try to score 60.Or Devin Booker gonna try to give me 70. So no, you're not gonna get me. Not today.”
Jimmy Butler on Kevin Durant, Devin Booker, LeBron JamesMar 28, 2017Chicago Tribune
