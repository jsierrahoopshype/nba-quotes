What Jimmy Butler and Tom Thibodeau have said about each other

Jimmy Butler on Tom Thibodeau, and Tom Thibodeau on Jimmy Butler: every quote in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

10 quotes·2014 to 2018·Latest quote Nov 9, 2018

Best quotes

Butler said after that Oct. 10 practice.

“I love this. Damn. I’m so fortunate and happy that I get to play basketball every day. And I don’t know what else to do, like it’s crazy. I’m up there with (Thibodeau).He just don’t leave the office. I leave the office, but I go home and work. I’ve got weights at the house, we got a gym right there. I’m working. I’m on you, Thibs, I’m on your tail.”
Jimmy Butler on Tom ThibodeauOct 18, 2017St. Paul Pioneer Press

Thibodeau says of last year.

“I think a veteran understands that the fourth quarter is a lot different than the first three quarters. And Jimmy [Butler] has been one of the great finishers in our league.”
Tom Thibodeau on Jimmy ButlerOct 18, 2017ESPN

Thibs on where things stand between T'Wolves and Butler for potential extension:

“We know how important Jimmy is to the team. But we don't talk about contract negotiations or anything like that so just get ready for the season.”
Tom Thibodeau on Jimmy ButlerJul 10, 2018Twitter @NickFriedell

Christopher Hine:Tom Thibodeau on managing Jimmy Butler's minutes:

“We have some parameters. If he’s had rest in between he’ll play a little bit more. There’s some games he’s going to play less. But we have an idea. He's averaging about 35 minutes per game and that's about what we talked about.”
Tom Thibodeau on Jimmy ButlerNov 9, 2018Twitter @ChristopherHine

Tim MacMahon:Thibs on decision for Jimmy Butler to skip this trip:

“I knew going in, looking at the schedule, where we were and the amount of time he’s practiced,I knew we would talk after the game. It’s what I wanted, too. Obviously, I wanted his input, but I wanted to be smart about it.”
Tom Thibodeau on Jimmy ButlerOct 20, 2018Twitter @espn_macmahon

Thibodeau on Jimmy Butler doing interview right after practice:

“It is what it is. In today's world, it's a different world. There's all this social media. It's the accessibility. I have no problems with a guy doing interviews. It's what happens. I want the team to be first.”
Tom Thibodeau on Jimmy ButlerOct 12, 2018Twitter @eric_nehm

Tom Thibodeau on the media's role in this Jimmy Butler situation:

“You guys, your job is the drama. Our job is to get ready to play. So I understand you have a job to do. We have a job to do... We're not going to get into daily behavior.”
Tom Thibodeau on Jimmy ButlerOct 12, 2018Twitter @DaneMooreNBA
