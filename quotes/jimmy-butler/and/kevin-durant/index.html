What Jimmy Butler and Kevin Durant have said about each other

Jimmy Butler on Kevin Durant, and Kevin Durant on Jimmy Butler: every quote in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

Kevin Durant on the new-look Timberwolves:

“Jimmy Butler was hurt. I could tell he was hurt he got traded. Ain't not loyalty, man. Do your thing, Jimmy. That's a lot of talent. Let's see how that works. Let’s go down the line with that: I’m just saying somebody will have to give up something in their games in order for it to work, and I believe that they will. But [Karl-Anthony] Towns needs to be the guy that they get the ball to, I think, because he’s so good. Jimmy needs to be facilitating. Wiggins is going to be the guy if you need a basket, he’s going to be the finisher.”
Kevin Durant on Jimmy Butler, Andrew Wiggins, Karl-Anthony TownsAug 28, 2017CSNBayArea.com

Butler, on if LeBron James remains his toughest assignment:

“You're not gonna pull me into that. Because if I say he is, then the next time I play Kevin Durant, he's going to try to score 60.Or Devin Booker gonna try to give me 70. So no, you're not gonna get me. Not today.”
Jimmy Butler on Kevin Durant, Devin Booker, LeBron JamesMar 28, 2017Chicago Tribune

KC Johnson:Kevin Durant:

“I know what type of player and worker Jimmy (Butler) is. That’s the one you need to lead your franchise. He’s grown into that.”
Kevin Durant on Jimmy ButlerJul 28, 2016Twitter @KCJHoop
