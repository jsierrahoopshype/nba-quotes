Griffin told ESPN this week.

“We were concerned what would it look like for him because Kyrie had been really active with us in recruiting everybody that was going to come if LeBron didn't come. And was very successful as a recruiter. [Gordon] Hayward wanted to come, Chandler Parsons would have come, Channing Frye would have come, Trevor Ariza would have come. Like, we had an opportunity to get everybody, and he had done a great job for us. So there was a little bit of trepidation on our part of, 'Is he going to feel like we've asked him to go backwards?' And from the very beginning, it was like, 'Griff, all I want to do is win.' So he's been very receptive to it.”
DGDavid Griffin on LeBron James, Channing Frye, Trevor Ariza, Chandler ParsonsJun 1, 2017ESPN

Kevin Love:

“I don't want to make it seem like I was this great guy that came in and sacrificed right away. It was tough for me initially to do that because I didn't have a great veteran until the triangle came along, which was you and Channing, that helped change my life in that way.”
Kevin Love on Channing FryeJun 8, 2026YouTube

Richard Jefferson on thhe group chat dynamics of the 2016 Cavs:

“When we talk about the group chat from the team, there's like six of us, and it's been the same six. There was the full team group chat, then there was the guys that were J.R., Swish, Tristan, Kevin, Channing, and Bron. So that's the group of us. And then we start doing, hey guys, we should get together, do some golf and go do this, and then a conversation kind of got started is, and like, hey, it would be wrong if we didn't invite some of the other guys because it's going to look like, we're calling it this championship. No, it was the group chat from the championship team, not the championship team group chat.”
Richard Jefferson on Channing Frye, Tristan ThompsonJun 26, 2026YouTube
