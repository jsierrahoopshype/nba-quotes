Draymond Green on Joe Johnson comments about Amen Thompson getting paid $200 million with no jump shot:

“I do think number one, you get paid for what you’ve done. I think there’s always this idea of you get paid for what you’re going to become. No, no. In this league you get paid for what you you’ve done. Amen Thompson has put together a body of work that says if you want to keep me, this is going to be the going rate. Now, yes, he needs to improve. But on the flip side, I do get a little frustrated sometimes when people come out like, “Yo, this guy can just gain a jump shot.”
Draymond Green on Amen Thompson, Joe JohnsonSep 9, 2026x.com

Mike D'Antoni:

“And then Raja Bell got hurt (left calf in 2006 conference finals). What really ticks me off is that, and I don't know if we would’ve won or not, and I don’t know if we get to the finals or not, nobody knows, but the first year we tried, Joe Johnson breaks his orbital bone in his face (against the Mavs in the 2005 conference semifinals).And then the next year, Raja Bell gets hurt during the Dallas series. So, he's hobbled and hardly played or didn't play or wasn’t up to his usual self in the Western Conference Finals. And then the third year, we had the suspension.”
Mike D'Antoni on Raja Bell, Joe JohnsonAug 15, 2026Arizona Republic
“But I think they're still at the top of their games. I think Paul is still one of those guys who can go off for big nights and still have big scoring nights. Kevin is a culture change. He won't play but 20 to 25 minutes a night and there'll probably be nights when he doesn't play but his presence there alone will absolutely change the culture of Brooklyn. There's no doubt about it. I think for some of the young guys, even some of the veteran stars, Joe Johnson and Deron Williams, will learn and understand what a winner is and looks like and professionalism and being prepared.”
Doc Rivers on Joe Johnson, Deron WilliamsJul 28, 2013Boston Globe
