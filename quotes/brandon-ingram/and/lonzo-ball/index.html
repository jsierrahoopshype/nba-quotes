“I know it’s part of the business, but it was the first time I felt like somebody didn’t really want me in a way, I guess. But it was Anthony Davis, so there was no shame in that. He’s one of the best players to ever play. “So for myself, BI (Brandon Ingram), and Josh Hart as well, we were happy to get to a new situation and have a start-over moment. I think it helped my career personally. In the long run, it made me a better person and a better player.”
Lonzo Ball on Anthony Davis, Brandon Ingram, Josh HartNov 15, 2021NBC Sports
“LeBron and Rondo are arguably two of the best leaders ever. Having both of them out, that's two of our main guys who are talking on the court. It's new for me. It's new for BI [Brandon Ingram]. Kuz [Kyle Kuzma] probably talks the most out of all of us.”
Lonzo Ball on Brandon Ingram, Kyle KuzmaJan 14, 2019ESPN
