Kings coach Luke Walton said.

“You can tell he’s got those defensive instincts. He might not always been in good position, but he recovers out of it because he has length and he understands angles. He’s a good young player. I see why Philadelphia likes him so much. I still don’t think anyone can keep De’Aaron in front of them in those situations, but he does do a nice job on the defensive end.”
Luke Walton on De'Aaron FoxMar 21, 2021Sacramento Bee
