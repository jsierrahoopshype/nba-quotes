Olden Polynice on how he would react if he was Joel Embiid and LeBron James walks into the locker room and starts setting standards:

“I’ll be like, you ain’t my daddy. You ain’t my daddy you my teammate, you know. You have to respect. Again, the man doesn’t walk on water. I will give you the respect, but that’s about it. You’re still not going to come in and just like dictate stuff. You know, first of all look, the man plays for the 76ers and he’s jumping on helicopters every day going to New York. It’s like how are you spending time with your teammates getting to know them outside of practice, which is something you should be trying to do.”

Olden Polynice on Rich Paul saying Kobe Bryant would have supported LeBron James’ decision to join Philly:

“This goes out to Rich Paul. I have nothing but respect for you as far as how what you’ve accomplished and how you got there. You worked your butt off. But you need to shut the fu*k up with that statement because that is the most asinine thing. Number one, we don’t know. You shouldn’t bring up Kobe in any way, shape, or form that he would know what you know, LeBron, you know, the decision LeBron made. That’s some bullsh*t, Okay. This is clickbait all over again. That’s like me walking up and slapping somebody and going, Malcolm X would have understood it. I don’t know what the hell Malcolm X would understand. And so to me, don’t do that. That’s so disrespectful.”
