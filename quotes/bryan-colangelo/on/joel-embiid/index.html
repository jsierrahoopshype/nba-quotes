What Bryan Colangelo has said about Joel Embiid

Every quote from Bryan Colangelo about Joel Embiid in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

5 quotes·2016 to 2017·Latest quote Sep 20, 2017

Best quotes

Keith Pompey:Colangelo on if Embiid had a setback:

“No.He’s a 7-foot, 280-pound specimen that had knee surgery in the offseason. You know, cut his season short. He had five-and-half, six months to his return to play. It looks like if you walked into the gym. He could be playing five-on-five basketball. But once again, we are going to take our cues from people that now best.”
BCBryan Colangelo on Joel EmbiidSep 20, 2017Twitter @PompeyOnSixers

Bryan Colangelo provided additional comments on Embiid’s status, saying,

“[Embiid] has been on the court, working out slowly and methodically getting back to the point where he’s going to be ready for some five-on-five type competition. At what point, I’m not sure yet, but he looks terrific in his drill work. He’s coming along. That’s not to get over-hyped about it. It’s just to say he’s progressing well, and that’s encouraging for all of us.”
BCBryan Colangelo on Joel EmbiidMay 9, 2016NBA.com

Derek Bodner:Colangelo:

“We hope that they're both (Embiid and Simmons) going to be able to play together this year. That's the goal.”
BCBryan Colangelo on Joel Embiid, Ben SimmonsFeb 9, 2017Twitter @DerekBodnerNBA
