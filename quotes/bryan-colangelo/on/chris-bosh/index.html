Toronto Raptors GMBryan Colangelo spoke out Thursday, a day after losing Chris Bosh to the Miami Heat, and said the star player's move was simply the result of a number of factors all meshing together.

“It was unfortunately a perfect storm with respect to the way the season ended, and it was even a more perfect storm with respect to the options that were out there for Chris Bosh,”
BCBryan Colangelo on Chris BoshJul 8, 2010TSN
“We're losing Chris Bosh not because of the city, and not because of this organization, and not because of where we find ourselves, but we're losing Chris Bosh because he's got an opportunity to go play in completely unknown, unforeseen and unheard of circumstances. The possibility of three guys joining forces - this is unique and it's somewhat of a revolution for our league, and he's part of that revolution.”
