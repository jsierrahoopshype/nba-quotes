Ben Bolch:Doc Rivers:

“To my dad’s dying days, if I brought up Michael Jordan over Bill Russell he almost wanted to hit me.”
Doc Rivers on Michael Jordan, Bill RussellFeb 27, 2015Twitter @latbbolch
“I’ve seen Shaq [Shaquille O’Neal] win titles, Bill Russell win titles, Dennis Rodman win titles. So there’s a lot of guys that have missed free throws and won a lot of NBA titles, so that’s not a concern for me at all. What D.J. does for our team is far more valuable than those missed free throws. “I can’t remember the last time he didn’t [have energy for us]. He plays with great spirit and plays hard pretty much every night and he does everything right for us. I agree with Jeff Van Gundy, he should just go walk out on the All-Star Game and go play. Just start playing. Nobody’s going to tell him to get off [the court]. So I think he should just go do it.”
Doc Rivers on Shaquille O'Neal, Dennis Rodman, Bill RussellFeb 8, 2015Boston Globe
