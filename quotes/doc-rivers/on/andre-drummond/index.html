Omari Sanfoka II:Doc Rivers on Andre Drummond:

“He's been great. He's been one of those guys that guys love. He's been a great light. I'm pushing him, I told him 'I'm not going to just let you be you.' That's part of what I have to do and he's been great at it. He's allowed it.”
Doc Rivers on Andre DrummondOct 15, 2021Twitter @omarisankofa

Doc on Andre Drummond in an in-game interview with NBATV:

“I think it's a great place for him. Obviously, I'm biased, but having him and Joel, just think of that. Joel goes off the floor, Drummond comes on the floor. We’re gonna be protective of Joel so in the games Joel misses, we’re gonna have Andre Drummond starting in those games. That’s gonna make us all feel good all the time.”
Doc Rivers on Andre DrummondAug 9, 2021Twitter @Ky_Carlin
