Doc Rivers on Dirk Nowitzki:

“He's his own bird, is what I love about him. There's not been a Dirk. Ever. There's been a lot of great players in our league, but the great, great players are the ones where you say well there's never been one of those. To me that's Dirk. I don't know, who would you say was a Dirk before Dirk? No one.”
Doc Rivers on Dirk NowitzkiMar 23, 2017ESPN
