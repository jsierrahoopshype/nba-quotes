Doc Rivers on Shai Gilgeous-Alexander and Jerome Robinson:

“We believe that No. 12 and 13 now is a great number to be drafted at. But five years from now, when everyone does the redraft, they’re going to say, ‘Wow, these guys should have been No. 2 and 3 [or] No. 1 and 2.’”
Doc Rivers on Jerome Robinson, Shai Gilgeous-AlexanderJun 25, 2018Twitter @jovanbuha

Michael Grange:Doc Rivers on Canadian PG Shai Gilgeous-Alexander:

“He can be great, he has all the tools, he just needs to work at it and he will. If he doesn’t become great it will be his fault and mine, we’ll share the responsibility”
Doc Rivers on Shai Gilgeous-AlexanderFeb 3, 2019Twitter @michaelgrange

Doc Rivers on the Shai Gilgeous-Alexander/Paul George trade:

“I even told Kawhi when he was saying ‘I need another guy’… I kept saying, ‘Kawhi, Shai’s gonna be a star.’”
Doc Rivers on Paul George, Shai Gilgeous-AlexanderDec 13, 2023Twitter @BillSimmons
