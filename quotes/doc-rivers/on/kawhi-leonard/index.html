What Doc Rivers has said about Kawhi Leonard

Every quote from Doc Rivers about Kawhi Leonard in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

“There's a lot of great players. LeBron is phenomenal, KD is phenomenal. Not that [Leonard] is Jordan or anything like that, but he's the most like him. Big hands, post game, can finish. Great leaper, great defender, in-between game. If you beat him to the spot he bumps you off, and then you add his three-point shooting.”
Doc Rivers on Kawhi LeonardMay 29, 2019Sports Illustrated

Ohm Youngmisuk:Doc Rivers on Kawhi Leonard's Game 7 experience:

“You can't guarantee that he will play well or not because he is human and all players are. But you know the moment won't be too big. That is the one thing, with him, you just know that. So that's comforting.”
Doc Rivers on Kawhi LeonardSep 14, 2020Twitter @NotoriousOHM

Mark Medina:Doc Rivers on Matt Barnes guarding Kawhi Leonard:
