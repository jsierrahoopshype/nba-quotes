Best quotes

Chris Forsberg:Doc Rivers on whether he thinks Paul Pierce will retire:

“Depends on the day I talk to him. Paul has had the summer, he's gone back and forth. I think he has a right to do that. I really do. And so, you know, Paul didn't have the best year last year. I don't think he wants to go out that way, so I think that's why he's working to try to come back. But he still may change his mind next week. So we just have to wait. I told him if I see him at training camp, I'm assuming he's playing.”
Doc Rivers on Paul PierceSep 6, 2016ESPN

Brian Robb:Doc on Paul Pierce's legacy:

“I would say this: In this day and time, he stayed. You think about it, no one sticks.... Wewere pretty bad for a stretch and even before, yet he stayed and then it worked out for him. I just think about his loyalty to this organization is amazing.”
Doc Rivers on Paul PierceFeb 11, 2018Twitter @BrianTRobb

Jared Weiss:Doc on Pierce:

“We bumped heads a couple of times. But at the end of the day, I'm sitting in my office, then Paul walks in & he says, 'We're good. We're gonna do this together.'I was grateful because we live in a time now, with superstar players, as a coach, that means a lot.”
Doc Rivers on Paul PierceFeb 11, 2018Twitter @JaredWeissNBA
