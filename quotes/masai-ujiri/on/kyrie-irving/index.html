Masai Ujiri:

“Kyrie Irving is one of the finest basketball players to ever walk around the world. I remember Kevin Durant saying there is only one Kyrie walking around the world. I am blessed to have had a relationship with him. Now we find ourselves on the same team. He is coming off injury, but he has had an incredible summer. He is right back at it. His spirit, you can tell he is a very passionate dude. He thinks differently, but inside, Kyrie is a great dude, a great person. All the young players look up to him. He is such a kind person around employees, around everybody.”
