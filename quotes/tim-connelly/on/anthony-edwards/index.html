What Tim Connelly has said about Anthony Edwards

Every quote from Tim Connelly about Anthony Edwards in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

5 quotes·2022 to 2026·Latest quote Sep 30, 2026

Best quotes

Tim Connelly:

“Anthony's best chance to be the best player in the world is to be the best two-way player in the world. His competition is so good and so talented to be in that mix of the top four or five guys to be an MVP candidate. I think he'd be the first to tell you at times last year, and some of it was our fault, we put him in positions where he had to do so much offensively. We think a lot of the ball-handling stuff will be out of his hands now with more traditional point guards next to him. But he's got to be a two-way player. He's got to be a lead on both ends.”
TCTim Connelly on Anthony EdwardsSep 30, 2026YouTube

Tim Connelly on Anthony Edwards:

“The guy can be as good as anybody in the world. That type of talent, he's certainly got the winning DNA that we're all looking for. So, I just don't want him to ever lose that joy. It's pretty special to watch a person who's that comfortable in his own skin. We can't skip steps to be an elite team. He can't skip steps to being an elite player. Butthere is no limit on what he can do. He can be the best player in the world.”
TCTim Connelly on Anthony EdwardsJun 20, 2022Minneapolis Star-Tribune

Tim Connelly:

“We continue to challenge Anthony because we think he can be one of the best players ever. And we don’t say that lightly.”
TCTim Connelly on Anthony EdwardsSep 30, 2024x.com
