Kevin Garnett:

“I think Boston's going to be fine. That team has always been proven, they always succeed, always push the envelope when it comes to winning, and they have good pieces up there-- Jeff Green, Rajon (Rondo), Brandon Bass, guys up there that's really hard-working guys, that will come in and compete every night, are going to put forth a valid effort. So they're not going to be shabby, not at all.”
Kevin Garnett on Rajon Rondo, Brandon Bass, Jeff GreenSep 10, 2013Booth Newspapers
“What I like about Brandon, he just … he’s never satisfied with his role. He goes about his job the same, every day. He’s a rare guy in that he’s getting better. I think he’s better right now than he’s ever been in his career. And I think his upside is still ahead of him.”
Danny Ainge on Brandon BassFeb 23, 2015CSNNE.com

Rajon Rondo:

“It happens so often in this league. It starts in training camp when you have to say goodbye to the guys that get cut. You are attached to them for a few weeks and they get cut. I’ve been here for a long time. I’ve been friends with Jeff, Brandon and Avery for five years now. We still keep in touch. No one has died. We still have the same phone numbers and we keep in touch.”
Rajon Rondo on Brandon BassJan 2, 2015Boston Globe
