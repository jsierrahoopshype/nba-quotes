What John Wall and LeBron James have said about each other

John Wall on LeBron James, and LeBron James on John Wall: every quote in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

John Wall:

“Sad thing, injuries happen; it’s part of the game. But I felt like I was the second-best player in the East that year after LeBron James. We were hooping, we were balling. You solidify yourself. When you get to that second max, even third max, you feel like you made it. I’m one of those guys people talk about on the regular.”
John Wall on LeBron JamesJul 18, 2024YouTube

John Wall:

“At that time I was in a dark space trying to find happiness. For me was going out partying, having fun, just trying to find something, yeah, to get me some peace and it wasn't the right way. And when I had all that go through, and I had like, me... you got to think,2017 I signed a max contract, I'm one of the top 10 best players in the league. Yeah, second best player after LeBron. Have Achilles injury after that. And now I see, I go from top bottom to the bottom.”
John Wall on LeBron JamesMar 13, 2024YouTube
“LeBron (James). However you say the guy from Milwaukee's name, I don't know how to say his name. I don't want to say it wrong.... And probably Kevin Love. It's great to see him playing back at a high level since he got traded from Minnesota. You understand what he was when he was on their team, so to get back to averaging double-figures — double-doubles — is good for him.”
John Wall on LeBron James, Kevin LoveJan 13, 2017USA Today Sports
