Jordan Ott on Luke Kennard:

“There are very few movement shooters in the NBA. It feels like they are going away and he can do it. And I think he has more playmaking ability. We saw it there at the end of the last season, the playmaking ability of him. He is another guy defensively we are going to ask to probably do something he has not done, try to stretch him there to take the ball, like we talk about a lot. Just knowing him and how he approaches it, he is one of those routine, consistent, and the shooting piece is as real as I have ever seen.”
