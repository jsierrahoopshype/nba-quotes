Bradley Beal:

“It’s a totally different dynamic (for him), right? A hundred percent. We’re all used to having a certain system built around us. And granted, this is more or less still (Booker’s) system that we still have;a lot of the offense that he was a big part of years ago. But nonetheless, it’s a big sacrifice. And I think the beauty about us is that we’re all three unselfish guys, humble guys, and you know that, hell, any of us can get it going on any night and it’s just a matter of us picking our spots. Where does each other like the ball? What plays do you like? Do you like post-ups more or handoffs more? It’s just getting a feel for each other a little bit more.”
