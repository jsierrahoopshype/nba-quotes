“Your dad, [Doc Rivers], was an NBA coach, you've been able to work out with Paul Pierce and [Kevin Garnett] and Ray Allen and all that. We felt like you had the upperhand. He had the NBA socks when we were playing. We were thinking about all of this stuff. You had access to all this stuff and we didn't. We were kind of envious of it. His game was like super far advanced from everybody else; his handle, his floater, everything. In a way, we were like 'man, this dude.' We were kind of jealous of him for a little bit.”
Bradley Beal on Paul Pierce, Ray Allen, Doc Rivers, Kevin GarnettSep 28, 2018NBC Sports
