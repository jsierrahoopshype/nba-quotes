Best quotes

Durant said when asked about the difference between a great season and a player having an MVP-type season.

“There's a lot of guys that play extremely well for years and years and just starting to get MVP consideration. But I think that consistent play sometimes gets a little boring to people but if you look at a guy like James Harden, he's been doing it for three years, same things and people start to act like it's new. Steph Curry has been doing the same thing for three years and I guess the narrative (is) around their team but what he's been doing is new to everybody,”
Kevin Durant on James Harden, Stephen CurryFeb 13, 2015ESPN.com

Kevin Durant:

“I do see myself signing a contract extension. Ican't tell you exactly when that'll happen, but I do see it happening. Seeing the quick progression of this franchise from where it was right after the James Harden and Chris Paul era… Seeing when Ime [Udoka] got here and how he turned it around so fast—it felt organic and natural. Coming into the gym, getting to be a Houston Rocket for the first time... I’ve always had respect for this coaching staff, this fanbase, this state, this city. So it feels right.”
Kevin Durant on Chris Paul, James HardenSep 29, 2025YouTube
“We are grown men now. We know what we want. We really know the game of basketball now. We are not those young guys that want to run around and just shoot and dunk all day. And then for me, I sit back and I know what player Kevin Durant is. He's one of the best players to ever touch a basketball.”
James Harden on Kevin DurantJan 19, 2021ESPN
