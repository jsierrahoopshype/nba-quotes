James Harden on Kevin Durant (Thunder 2009 to 2012; Team USA, 2012; Nets, 2020 to 2022):

“His change of pace, change of direction ... he'll get you leaning one way and once he crosses back over, he's so tall that if you're not on his hip, he's shooting right over you or he's getting to the basket. That right there automatically is just a different type of a handle for a 7-footer. You really don't see that. I don't see [another] guy at 7 feet that can shoot it fluidly like him and handle the rock and do the things he do.”
James Harden on Kevin DurantJan 31, 2025ESPN
