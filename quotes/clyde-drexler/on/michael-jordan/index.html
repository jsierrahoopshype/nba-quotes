“You have to take it in perspective. We were truly competitive. I haven’t seen (the documentary). So I don’t know how to respond to your question. But I have a lot of respect for Michael and Magic. I wish them nothing but well.”
Clyde Drexler on Michael JordanMay 31, 2020USA Today Sports

Drexler also offered up suggestions on a handful of other NBA greats.

“And then you've got guys like Dr. J [Julius Erving], Larry Bird, George Gervin, Elgin Baylor, Oscar Robertson, Jerry West. All those guys are in the conversation,and so for people to bring this up today, to me it's just unbelievable. And I love Michael and LeBron. But still, let's not take something away from those other guys who played.”
Clyde Drexler on LeBron James, Michael Jordan, Jerry West, Larry Bird, Oscar Robertson, Elgin Baylor, Julius Erving, George GervinMay 29, 2020NBC Sports

Drexler said:

“That’s Michael’s documentary so obviously it’s going to be from his perspective. Everybody is entitled to their own opinion. A lot of times guys didn’t like each other from other teams, but as you get older, you’ve got to get beyond all of that and show some love and some respect for the people you played with and against.“This is a team game, it’s not one guy,”
Clyde Drexler on Michael JordanMay 29, 2020deseret.com
