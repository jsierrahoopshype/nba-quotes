Mat Ishbia:

“When I traded for Kevin Durant, it was a no-brainer. A thousand times out of a thousand, you make that trade. You get Kevin Durant on your team with three and a half years left on his contract from the time I traded for him, and you put him next to Devin Booker, and you go play. AndI feel great about the trade then. I feel better about it even now. It's been a great thing for our organization, and I can’t wait to see it come to fruition the way we all envision it.”
Mat Ishbia on Kevin Durant, Devin BookerNov 20, 2024YouTube
