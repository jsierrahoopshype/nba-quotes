Kevin Durant:

“To say I was frustrated about that — that’s just a basketball thing that we always (feel). We trying to figure it out, you know what I mean? Style of play is not something that I’m pissed off at the team about. It’s just like, ‘Yo, we need to figure it out to get better.’ I think [new head coach Mike Budenholzer] has been watching film of what we did last year to try to incorporate some of those things that worked for us and try to finetune some of the things that didn’t.”
Kevin Durant on Mike BudenholzerJul 10, 2024Arizona's Sports Page

Mike Budenholzer on how Kevin Durant has processed trade rumors:

“How people process and think about things and how long it takes, there's not a script. So I think Kevin's in a good place with his teammates, with coaches, today in practice.... He's just been a great leader for us”
Mike Budenholzer on Kevin DurantFeb 11, 2025Twitter @GeraldBourguet

Kevin Durant on if he wished Mike Budenholzer kept in the game to start the 2nd quarter after scoring 16 points in the 1st quarter of Suns 30-point loss to Boston:

“I prefer to play 48 minutes every night, but I know how realistic that is. I feel like I'm always simmering hot regardless. I might be 0-for-10 and you've seen games this year I start 1-of-9 and I end up finishing the game shooting well because I feel like I'm always hot. Maybe that's a little overconfidence in myself or delusion, but that's how I feel when I'm out there.”
Kevin Durant on Mike BudenholzerMar 27, 2025x.com
