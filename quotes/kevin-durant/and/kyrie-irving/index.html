Kyrie Irving:

“Kevin Durant has a presence about him that's really unassuming but he dominates games. [...] And I think that in and of itself was like, 'OK, now I got the championship, now I just wanna work on my craft.' Andnow, of course, this platform we're on as athletes becomes like, everyone starts attacking like, 'NO, YOU'RE NOT ENOUGH. You're not this, you're not that.' And it's just like, 'Bro, give it a break. Seriously. Let it go.' Yes, he's on an incredible team but he's also the best player on that team.”
