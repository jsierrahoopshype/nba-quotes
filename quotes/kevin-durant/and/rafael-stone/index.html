Rafael Stone on the expectations for Kevin Durant in Houston:

“Obviously when you trade for somebody of Kevin Durant's stature, it's very important that you have good conversations coming in about expectations. And he understood coming in that this wasn't going to be a situation where we're trading for him and now we're going to go get 75 veteran players to play with him, which sometimes happens, but it's not like that has a great track record of success. And so, he understood that he was coming in to play with a bunch of younger guys and that the team was probably going to look the way it looks now. And it has. And is best I can tell, you guys can ask him, but he really enjoys them.”
RSRafael Stone on Kevin DurantOct 9, 2026YouTube

Kevin Durant:

“To walk in here every day, you see the attention to detail from everybody — not just the leadership of Rafael and Ime and Tilman but everybody who works in here — the level of detail that it takes to become a successful team and franchise. So happy I’m a part of that. I want to keep working to build and show my worth here.”
Kevin Durant on Tilman Fertitta, Rafael StoneOct 20, 2025Houston Chronicle

Speaking at practice on Monday just before the team flew to Oklahoma City, Kevin Durant said he was willing to sacrifice money to maximize the Rockets’ team-building opportunities.

“Just talking to Rafael (Stone) and Ime (Udoka) and how they wanted to build the team, and how I seen myself in this organization, I just thought it was a perfect contract for it,”
