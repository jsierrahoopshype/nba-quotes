What Kevin Durant and Steve Nash have said about each other

Kevin Durant on Steve Nash, and Steve Nash on Kevin Durant: every quote in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

Here’s Durant on his relationship with Nash:

“Our relationship started from him playing and me admiring him as a player. And then playing against him and competing against him and then finally getting a chance to work out with Steve – 7 or 8 years ago. (I) understood how his mind works for the game and every time I was in the gym with him I was like a sponge. I’m looking forward to this, man. I always feel like I’m a student of the game and (Nash, as) somebody who has experienced so much and played in different eras, I’m looking forward to him teaching me some more things about it as well.”
Kevin Durant on Steve NashSep 15, 2020Yahoo! Sports

Davide Chinellato:Nash:

“My relationship with Kevin Durant goes back to when I was still playing. We're friends. It's a privelege to work to one of the best players to ever play the game. He's always developing as human being, always trying to grow and to learn”
Steve Nash on Kevin DurantSep 9, 2020Twitter @dchinellato

Durant on Nash's presence:

“I think having Steve there and collaborating with our floor general every day is only gonna yield good results for our scorers like myself and Taurean, Caris and Spencer. Them having that relationship I think is gonna be key for us going forward.”
Kevin Durant on Spencer Dinwiddie, Taurean Prince, Steve NashOct 1, 2020Twitter
