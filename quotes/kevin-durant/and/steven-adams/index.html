What Kevin Durant and Steven Adams have said about each other

Kevin Durant on Steven Adams, and Steven Adams on Kevin Durant: every quote in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

Kevin Durant:

“It's growing every day. I feel like we can be even closer as a coach-player. Somebody that I always can lean on throughout a game and vice versa. We need to continue to keep building that. Also having Fred (VanVleet) and Steven (Adams) back is going to be good for all of us, being in these leadership positions and having younger guys on the team that look up to us. It's a group effort in everything we do, leadership, scoring, defending, everything is a group effort.”
Kevin Durant on Steven Adams, Fred VanVleetSep 24, 2026YouTube

Durant told Newshub on leaving Adams and the Thunder.

“Steven is going to be great, man. He works hard, he loves the game, and he just had that pure fun and joy.”
Kevin Durant on Steven AdamsAug 6, 2016Stuff.co.nz

Steven Adams on how his role might change with Kevin Durant gone:

“It isn't up to me whether I'm going to say ‘yo, I'm going to do this’. It's the coach's job to put us in the best position to succeed, not only individually but as a team. So I'm just waiting for his word … [I might] shoot some threes.”
Steven Adams on Kevin DurantAug 24, 2016The Wireless
