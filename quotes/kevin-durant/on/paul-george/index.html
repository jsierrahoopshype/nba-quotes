Kevin Durant on GOAT conversations:

“I almost say that stuff to challenge people's thinking on why do you even have a GOAT list for one and what does it actually mean in the grand scheme of things? And to also highlight that it is such a subjective thing that I can ask 20 people that you may not ever know or talk to who's their GOAT.And they may say me, they may say Tracy McGrady or Paul George, but what does it matter at the end of the day?”
