Kevin Durant on the new-look Timberwolves:

“Jimmy Butler was hurt. I could tell he was hurt he got traded. Ain't not loyalty, man. Do your thing, Jimmy. That's a lot of talent. Let's see how that works. Let’s go down the line with that: I’m just saying somebody will have to give up something in their games in order for it to work, and I believe that they will. But [Karl-Anthony] Towns needs to be the guy that they get the ball to, I think, because he’s so good. Jimmy needs to be facilitating. Wiggins is going to be the guy if you need a basket, he’s going to be the finisher.”
Kevin Durant on Jimmy Butler, Andrew Wiggins, Karl-Anthony TownsAug 28, 2017CSNBayArea.com
