Sam Presti on Josh Giddey:

“It was an up and down year. I think he would acknowledge that. He's 21. 21 year old's typically have up and down years... He got started quickly and as the team got better, he's seen some resistance with that.. The thing about Giddey that I respect is that he is tough and he is clutch. He's been asked to change some things... and he hasn't flinched one time.”
