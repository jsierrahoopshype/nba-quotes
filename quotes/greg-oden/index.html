David West:

“Greg Oden. The one year that dude was healthy, we played them like three times in the first month-and-a-half of the season or something. I remember bumping up against him and being like, 'Yo, this dude is going to be a beast.' We couldn't do anything with him -- me and Tyson [Chandler] together -- he was just dunking everything around the basket. He was 285-to-290 [pounds], but not like a bulky fat. If Greg Oden had stayed around, I don't think small-ball evolves. If he's healthy, I don't think small-ball evolves the way it did. No way.”
David West on Greg OdenOct 14, 2020basketballnews.com
“The best I ever was around – one of the best. Go back to his junior year, we’re playing Ohio State and I put Noah on Greg Oden. And he’s out there guarding guards. We played Brad’s team, I think Brad was an assistant, we played Butler to go to the Elite 8. [They’re] playing with four guards and I’ve got him guarding a guard.”
BDBilly Donovan on Greg OdenMar 20, 2018Celtics Wire
“We will track, I think, Greg Oden. From my understanding is that he's working out, he's working out on the court and that he wants to be ready to play in training camp. But this isn't the time to have that conversation with him. We do have interest in him and we will track him.”
Pat Riley on Greg OdenJul 15, 2013South Florida Sun-Sentinel
