Best quotes

Toumani Camara on Scoot Henderson:

“To me, he put it together. Scoot always been super athletic, super fast, great finisher, great driver, great shooter, and I feel like now mentally he just put it all together. How can he affect the team defensively, how scrappy he could get, and offensively make the right play every time. I think there’s no one time this summer where I’m like, ‘You should have done this, Scoot.’ Every time he drive and making a decision, it’s the right one and it’s the one that we need for the team.”
Toumani Camara on Scoot HendersonOct 10, 2026YouTube

Chauncey Billups on Scoot Henderson:

“I didn't realize how good of a playmaker he was. I knew he was explosive and tough and competitive, but he has a feel about his game, he has a way of bringing you with him. For a young guy, that's not easy. He can get to his spots pretty easily. He can really guard. He'll learn a lot of tough lessons by the way he's so competitive. It's gonna be some tough nights that I'll have to let him go through, because that's how you learn. But he's been a dream, to teach him and watch film with him. It's been a lot of fun.”
Chauncey Billups on Scoot HendersonOct 2, 2023Twitter @highkin

Sean Highkin:Jrue Holiday on Scoot Henderson:

“Scoot is aggressive. How he gets to the basket is amazing. Me being able to space the floor and shoot the three is something that will help him out. Being able to play off him and knowing that Scoot is the next one up and [helping him] showcase what he can do.”
Jrue Holiday on Scoot HendersonJul 29, 2025Bluesky
