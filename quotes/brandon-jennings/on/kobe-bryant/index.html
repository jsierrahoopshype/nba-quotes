Brandon Jennings:

“I don't have MJ in my Top 5 because when I was seven or eight years old, that was the last time he won a championship. I didn’t really understand basketball like that, so I didn’t really start loving the game until I saw AI and Kobe Bryant in the early 2000s. That’s why he can’t be in my Top 5.I respect him, I can watch his highlight tape, but what I really saw and felt were guys like Steph Curry, LeBron James, Kobe, Shaq, and KD.”
Brandon Jennings on LeBron James, Kobe BryantOct 22, 2024YouTube
