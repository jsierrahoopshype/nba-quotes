Best quotes

Delonte West:

“We were playing Cleveland, out in Seattle, the first month of the season, and I was cooking them. I remember we were sitting at the foul line, and LeBron James, he was like, man, I followed you in college. You remember that time I came to Philly at the Palestra to watch you play? And I was like, yeah, I remember. He was like, man, you want to come play with Cleveland? I thought he was just doing a veteran trick. (...)I thought he was just trying to get me out of my game by getting familiarity. But he was serious. He asked me to come play with Cleveland.”
Delonte West on LeBron JamesSep 6, 2026YouTube
