Earl K. Sneed:Dirk Nowitzki:

“It's just an incredibly sad story. I think (Lamar Odom) was a great guy, even though stuff didn't work out for him here. I've got nothing but positive and nice things to say about him. He was always cool to us -- his teammates. Sometimes, he was just a little frustrated because it wasn't going his way, but (Lamar Odom) was a fun guy to be around. It's just a sad story, and we all obviously wish (Lamar Odom) well. We're thinking about him. Hopefully he'll pull through.”
