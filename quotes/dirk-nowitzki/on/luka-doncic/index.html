Dirk Nowitzki on Luka Doncic, continued:

“Obviously, I don't want to put too much pressure on him, either. But coming over from a different country, I went through the same thing 20 years ago. It's tough. You have to adjust to a lot of things. For me, that took a whole year. That year was really really tough. We're going to do everything we can to help this kid develop, have fun, and learn, so we can see the best Luka here hopefully in a year to a couple more years. He's an incredible talent. His court vision and passing for his size at his age is something I've never seen in my 20 years.”
Dirk Nowitzki on Luka DoncicSep 11, 20181053thefan.radio.com

When Dwyane Wade asked who the greatest European basketball player of all time is, Dirk Nowitzki shared his admiration for what today’s superstars achieve.

“To be honest, I take more pride in watching them. We know records are meant to be broken,and there’s always someone who comes along who is better, does better things, is more athletic. That’s life. I really take pride in watching these kids. I see Luka Doncic and what he does,”
Dirk Nowitzki on Luka DoncicJan 21, 2024EuroHoops.net

Dirk Nowitzki on taking Luka Doncic under his wing:

“Nobody really takes him under his wing. He’s a confident young kid. He’s a millennial obviously. Their swag is a little different than when we got in the league. He felt from Day 1 he belongs here and he’s been showing that.”
Dirk Nowitzki on Luka DoncicJan 4, 2019Twitter @ESefko
