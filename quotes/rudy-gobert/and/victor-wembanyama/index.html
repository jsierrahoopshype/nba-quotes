Best quotes

Victor Wembanyama:

“There are active players I admire. As I said, some of the guys, you know, just kind of... you know, some of the guys I've been watching growing up, I'm not disappointed, but I'm like, like that's it? But some of them, talking like KD, you know? Of course, I get to know Rudy Gobert even more with the national team. I know their work ethic, I know how they view the game, and it's really inspiring, and I want to do the same as them.”
Victor Wembanyama on Rudy GobertOct 16, 2024YouTube

Victor Wembanyama on facing Rudy Gobert in the playoffs:

“It's great. We're already lucky to face him regularly during the regular season, and it's tough to play against him, it's demanding. He's played a huge role in my journey, has been a role model, has inspired me in so many ways that should actually inspire more people, especially when it comes to the care given to the body, which is an example for all big men.”
Victor Wembanyama on Rudy GobertMay 1, 2026x.com

Gobert:

“Victor is passionate about health, recovery, and the mental aspect of the game. He’s very young. But at the same time, he already understands how to improve things for his daily lifestyle and his daily recovery. A few days ago, he asked me what kind of filter I have in my house for water. Those are things that not a lot of 22-year-olds are asking. It shows how smart he is. But it also shows how dedicated he is to every aspect of greatness. That’s just an example.”
Rudy Gobert on Victor WembanyamaMar 14, 2026Responsible Gaming
