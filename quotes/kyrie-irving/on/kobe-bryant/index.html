Kyrie Irving on Kobe Bryant:

“I'm not letting people revise history. I'm not letting people come on and talk about Kobe like Kobe wasn't one of the best—not only that played, but that came in as a 17-year-old against grown men. And by the time this man was 22 years old, he had a few championships, and he was busting the best of the best's ass.”
Kyrie Irving on Kobe BryantJul 23, 2025YouTube

At every turn, he saw Bryant follow the same guiding principle: the one person who was going to control his career was Bryant himself.

“Despite what was going on from the outside influences and what everyone else felt was best for him, [Bryant] always did what was best for his career for himself,”
