Kyrie Irving on Luka Doncic:

“I’m amazed. I don’t use that word lightly either… I think he’s answered a lot of calls. When Luka first came into the league,there were a lot of questions on what he was going to turn out to be. I know a few people are eating their words. Watching him ascend into becoming an all-time great is amazing and I don’t take it for granted because I played with one of the greatest of all time, arguably the greatest of all time… I want to be supportive and continue to be an all-time great next to him, continue to show him the ropes, and let him continue to fly.”
Kyrie Irving on Luka DoncicMay 25, 2024Twitter @MikeAScotto
