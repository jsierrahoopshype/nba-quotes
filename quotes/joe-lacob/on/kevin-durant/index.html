What Joe Lacob has said about Kevin Durant

Every quote from Joe Lacob about Kevin Durant in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

“I said that with Kevin Durant. I’m still friends with Kevin Durant and I still really like him. And I will always love him for what he did for this franchise. By the way, he took less money when he was here. He really helped us. We were able to get a couple of other players. … Players don’t usually do that and he did that. So I have great respect and admiration for that for Kevin. And I would for Steph no matter what he does, too. He’s done a lot for us, we’ve done a lot for him. I would hope that we provide the environment for him that he wants to be at for the rest of his. … “I’m certainly not worried about the guy you mentioned recruiting him. I don’t think that’s going to happen.”
JLJoe Lacob on Kevin DurantJun 23, 2021lebronwire.usatoday.com

When asked about Iguodala’s No. 9, Lacob echoed a similar sentiment.

“We always wanna be gracious. I’ve said all along, he and any player like Kevin Durant, Andre Iguodala, whoever it is, a free agent, it's their choice. They earned it,”
JLJoe Lacob on Andre Iguodala, Kevin DurantAug 28, 2019NBC Sports

Warriors owner Joe Lacob -- who doesn't like to discuss the past very much -- probably never will understand why the four-time scoring champion chose a different path.

“I can't get mad when Kevin Durant -- who I felt pretty close with -- decides he's going to leave. Which to me, made no sense,”
JLJoe Lacob on Kevin DurantFeb 24, 2020NBC Sports
