Channing Frye:

“Nick Nurse, one thing that's not taking into consideration is Nick Nurse is a beast. And so when you add all these personalities to a team, Joel Embiid, Tyrese Maxey, you know, Bron, VJ Edgecombe, you know, Jaylen Brown, you need a coach that can handle all that that's going to put them in the right situation. Gansey on V.J. Edgecombe's leadership:

“V.J. has no fear, that’s what I love about him. Like, he’s a super competitor, but he’ll also tell you what’s on his mind and he’s obviously a high-IQ basketball player. So when he talks about something, you’re listening because he knows what he’s talking about. But the talent level is obviously elite. I mean, he does, like, you know, he’s had plays in practice where it’s game point and he blocks someone at the rim for the game. He gets a loose ball for…that’s flying out of bounds. He gets a rebound that wins a game. He just does all the little things and intangibles and he’s just a winning player. And the thing I love about him, too, like I said, no fear and he just competes at a high level.”
MGMike Gansey on VJ EdgecombeOct 3, 2026x.com
