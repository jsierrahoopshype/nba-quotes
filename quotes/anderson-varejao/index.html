Best quotes

Matthew Dellavedova:

“In my second column,I talked about how Andy Varejao (pictured) took Sergey Karasev and I to a Brazilian restaurant. The other people were joking that we would get sick of it because he likes to go there so much. We thought they were joking about that. I think we have eaten there about three or four more times since then,but Sergey and I still aren’t sick of it, it’s great.”
Matthew Dellavedova on Anderson Varejao, Sergey KarasevDec 12, 2013Bendigo Advertiser

Quote from Cavaliers General ManagerChris Grant:

“Clearly our players' health is our top concern and we are disappointed for Anderson, and his family. We know what a competitor he is and how much he wants to be on the court helping his teammates. In the meantime, he has our full support and we look forward to welcoming him back as he recovers.”
CGChris Grant on Anderson VarejaoJan 21, 2013NBA.com
