Justin Martinez:Kenrich Williams on Aday Mara:

“I really like Big Fella, Big Spain is what I call him. I like Aday. I feel like he has the potential to be a first team or second team All-Defensive type of guy.”
Kenrich Williams on Aday MaraOct 1, 2026Twitter

NBA Courtside:Jay Willams says OKC and the Spurs won the draft:

“They continue to put their chokehold on the NBA.What did we talk about a lot with OKC? How did Chet Holmgren play in the NBA finals? What’s going on with Isaiah Hartenstein when he had go against Wemby? That got a guy named Aday Mara who has a 7’4 wingspan, super talented can give you depth off the bench and give you an extra body to go against Wemby. How about for San Antonio for Wembanyama we talked a lot about who’s the enforcer when Kornet played in that series, now you bring a guy that brings a lot of physicality played for Dan Hurley at UConn a absolute beast on the glass.”
JWJay Williams on Dan Hurley, Isaiah Hartenstein, Chet Holmgren, Aday MaraJun 24, 2026x.com

Justin Martinez:Otega Oweh on what it's like playing alongside Aday Mara:

“It's on both sides of the floor. On defense, you can be a little more handsy and a little more aggressive, knowing he'll be in the paint to clean it up. And then on offense, you've just got to be on your Ps and Qs. He can be posting up and trying to score. And if you cut, he's bigger than everybody, so he can just easily see that you're wide open.”
Otega Oweh on Aday MaraJul 5, 2026Twitter
