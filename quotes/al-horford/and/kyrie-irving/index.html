What Al Horford and Kyrie Irving have said about each other

Al Horford on Kyrie Irving, and Kyrie Irving on Al Horford: every quote in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

“I really don’t understand it. I think we’re all kind of looking for those answers right now. And I just think time will tell. Obviously, Anthony Davis is probably having one of the better seasons that he’s had in many years, and they have their guy in Kyrie (Irving) that can kind of run the show for them. So it will be interesting to see obviously how it plays out from both sides.”
Al Horford on Kyrie Irving, Anthony DavisFeb 2, 2025New York Times

Horford says, describing one mind-blowing sequence.

“There was a defender that was coming. [Irving] was running full speed, stopped, put the ball [behind] his back. There was a guy right here, so I don't know how he got the ball across it, but the ball split the two guys, he took a step, the other defender—the third defender—came up, he took another step, he grabbed it, whipped it behind his back and laid it up. But it was like a fading floater with the left hand. It's stuff that you can't make up.”
Al Horford on Kyrie IrvingOct 16, 2018Bleacher Report

When asked how he'll be different in Boston, Irving replied,

“Actually playing point guard, which is very, very much exciting. Getting that thing on the break and allowing Gordon (Hayward) to have the space around him as well, and allowing Al Horford to make plays as a big, and having an offense that's not necessarily dictated just on me isolating. So I'm excited about that.”
Kyrie Irving on Gordon Hayward, Al HorfordSep 18, 2017Booth Newspapers
