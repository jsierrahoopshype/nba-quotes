Best quotes

Al Horford:

“It was Game 7, about four minutes to go in the fourth quarter. It was still a very close game and we're getting out of the timeout and I'm looking at Marcus Morris, I'm looking at Jayson Tatum, Jaylen, myself. We're gassed. It's Game 7, we're giving everything we have. I'm in great shape and I'm still like just very tired from the game... andI look at the other side and he's sitting kind of like in the scores table just leaning very casual. He's played the whole every single minute up to that point and he looked unfazed, he looked unbothered and and to me that was disturbing the fitness level the conditioning that he's in.”
Al Horford on Marcus Morris, Jayson TatumMar 30, 2023YouTube

Al Horford:

“Jayson was in such a zone it didn’t matter who was out there.… For him to play like that, that’s what special players do.”
Al Horford on Jayson TatumMay 14, 2023Twitter @SouichiTerada

teammate Al Horford said.

“I was very proud of them. A lot of people look at the offensive stuff but, defensively, their activity being in the right positions every time, scrambling on defense -- Tatum did that time and time again and so did Jaylen. So it was nice to see them have success on offense, but defensively that's where I'm focused with them and they've been doing a really good job.”
Al Horford on Jaylen Brown, Jayson TatumOct 25, 2017ESPN
