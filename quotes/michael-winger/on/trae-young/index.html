Michael Winger on Trae Young’s defense:

“He’ll be the first to tell you he’s not a humongous athlete. He gives up size on the defensive end. But he’ll play with effort,he’ll play with intelligence…But he’s not a shot-blocker and guys could potentially bully him…but that’s okay because that puts more pressure on Alex and we don’t mind the pressure on Alex. That puts more pressure on Bilal, that’s fine, we want that pressure on those guys.”
MWMichael Winger on Trae YoungMay 14, 2026x.com

MoreMichael Winger on why they traded for Trae Young:

“He’s an absolute genius. He’s a savant with the basketball in his hands..up until that point, other players on the floor have had to create for themselves and others and we don’t have a pure natural playmaker. We struggle offensively to create because we don’t have someone as naturally gifted as he is at creating something. He’s an offensive engine, we lacked an offensive engine. We have great play finishers, we have great athletes but we didn’t have an offensive engine.”
