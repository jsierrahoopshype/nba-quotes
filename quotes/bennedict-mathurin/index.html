Indiana Pacers coach Rick Carlisle on Bennedict Mathurin:

“Mathurin has a veteran-type toughness and consistency and belief. The reason why he believes so strongly in his game is because he works so hard on his game. He’s getting better and better. The way he’s playing and the positions that he’s putting himself and his teammates in are showing great growth.”

Darius Garland on Bennedict Mathurin:

“He has no fear. I didn’t know he was that creative with the ball in his hands…In Indiana he was aggressive, but over here T-Lue and everybody is letting him rock out. I really like it… Ben is gonna be Ben. We really need him to go be Ben. I love the aggression. I didn’t see it in Indiana but I love it over here.”
Darius Garland on Bennedict MathurinMar 10, 2026x.com

Buddy Hield on Pacers teammate Bennedict Mathurin:

“He’s a confident guy. He works hard. … He’s one of those guys that has a whole chip on his shoulder, and it comes from a good place, you know? He’s always hard on himself. He puts a ton of work in. Every time I go to the gym, he’s in there putting extra work in, watching film and talking to coaches. He’s one of those guys that are going to continue to get better in this league, and he’s just a rookie. The numbers that he's putting up and the things he’s doing are very impressive. He has a long way to go, a lot to learn about the game, but where he is at right now? I’m speechless.”
Buddy Hield on Bennedict MathurinJun 28, 2023Twitter
