Best quotes

Bradley Beal:

“Nobody knows this, I’ve only told a handful of people this. I committed to Florida my sophomore year. The only people that knew were me and Billy Donovan. My parents didn’t know, my brothers didn’t know, my friends didn’t know, my high school coach didn’t know, my AAU coaches didn’t know. Only me and Billy Donovan. They had just come off two championships. He said, 'I’m not guaranteeing you’ll start. I’m not guaranteeing you play time, but you’ll come here, work hard every day to get better, and become a man by the time you leave.' That’s very important versus other coaches who’ll give you this, that, or even offer a house for your parents. I loved that. I’m going to play for him.”
Bradley Beal on Billy DonovanOct 8, 2024YouTube

For Bradley Beal, his one-season career at Florida was more than just a stepping stone to the NBA; it was a learning experience that aided his transition into becoming a man, on and off the court.

“It was amazing for me and very well needed. I always say it was short-lived because I love Billy Donovan,and I wanted to win him a national championship, and I always told him that even when he [is] coaching with the Bulls now,”
Bradley Beal on Billy DonovanJul 12, 2021Sports Illustrated
“What was tough for me, what drew me to Florida was my relationship with Billy Donovan,”
Bradley Beal on Billy DonovanNov 2, 2020247Sports
