What Isaiah Thomas has said about Kobe Bryant

Every quote from Isaiah Thomas about Kobe Bryant in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

Thomas said during his Lakers Voices chat on Tuesday.

“He would tell me what he sees, what I can do and what I can do better to be able to get my team a win. That was throughout the whole playoffs. “That was special to me, because to be able to have Kobe Bryant talk to you and help you out is amazing, especially for me. That was my favorite player growing up.”
Isaiah Thomas on Kobe BryantMar 7, 2018NBA.com

Isaiah Thomas on Kobe’s camp:

“Kobe Bryant is arguably the best basketball player to ever play. ... To see him in that setting and such great players be in that setting and be able to compete and be able to learn from Kobe Bryant, that was one of the best two days of my life.”
Isaiah Thomas on Kobe BryantSep 30, 2019Twitter @FredKatz

Isaiah Thomas on being a Laker:

“It hasn't really hit me yet. I'm a lifelong Laker fan. I mean, my family is from Inglewood, California. I was brainwashed in to being a Laker fan as a young boy and Kobe [Bryant] has been my favorite player.”
Isaiah Thomas on Kobe BryantFeb 12, 2018Twitter @RyanWardLA
“That's just what I was going to do, no matter what. The people that helped me were my family -- my parents, my close friends, the Celtics organization, Brad Stevens, coaching staff, my teammates and so many guys reached out. Floyd Mayweather, Tom Brady, Kobe Bryant. I remember Kobe texting me and calling me and telling me nobody can tell you what to do, but if you do play then play to be the killer that you are and leave it all out there on the floor. I took that to heart.”
Isaiah Thomas on Kobe Bryant, Floyd Mayweather, Brad Stevens, Tom BradyNov 16, 2017Cleveland Plain Dealer

2011-12

1 quote

Thomas said with a laugh.

“In the first game of the season, we were playing the Lakers. Now, Kobe Bryant is my favorite player. When I checked into the game, Paul Westphal said, ‘Okay, Isaiah, you got Kobe.’ I kind of looked behind me. I didn’t think he was talking to me. I asked, ‘I got Kobe?!’ It didn’t seem real. So I start guarding Kobe. As he was backing me down, I was smiling. That’s my favorite player and I was guarding him. It was crazy. He scored a couple of times, but I stopped him a couple of times. I was happy with that.”
Isaiah Thomas on Kobe BryantMay 12, 2012HoopsWorld
