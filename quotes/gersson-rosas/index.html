Chris Finch on the Wolves offseason:

“I think we made great strides in addressing the needs. I think the front office, Gersson and his crew, did a really good job. We had limited bullets this year. We weren't in the draft, but we had priorities and we nailed every single one.”

Chauncey Billups:

“I liked the Minnesota situation. They have some good people. I love (owner Glen Taylor) so I was attracted to the situation. Interviewed well, but obviously they went with Gersson Rosas, who was a very good candidate and I’m sure will do a terrific job. But even with that situation, they reached out to me.”
Chauncey Billups on Glen Taylor, Gersson RosasAug 3, 2019Detroit Free Press
“I'd love to be with the Rockets for life. This obviously solidifies us for a little while. I'm just really thankful to Tilman Fertitta for having the faith in our team. And really, it is about us having a team of people that makes this all work; Coach (Mike) D'Antoni, (vice president) Keith Jones, (vice president) Gersson Rosas, (trainer) Jason Biles, (assistant general manager) Monte McNair, (assistant general manager) Eli Witus, (assistant general manager, Vipers GM) Jimmy Paulis.”
Daryl Morey on Gersson Rosas, Tilman FertittaMar 13, 2019Houston Chronicle
