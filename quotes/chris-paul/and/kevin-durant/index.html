Kevin Durant:

“I do see myself signing a contract extension. Ican't tell you exactly when that'll happen, but I do see it happening. Seeing the quick progression of this franchise from where it was right after the James Harden and Chris Paul era… Seeing when Ime [Udoka] got here and how he turned it around so fast—it felt organic and natural. Coming into the gym, getting to be a Houston Rocket for the first time... I’ve always had respect for this coaching staff, this fanbase, this state, this city. So it feels right.”
Kevin Durant on Chris Paul, James HardenSep 29, 2025YouTube
“He's come back and he's controlling his team. I look at his box score almost every night that he plays and I see that he's at eight or ten points, but has sixteen assists, four rebounds, and just two turnovers. That's dominating a basketball game. He's been doing that this whole year and they've been winning games so he's back to his normal self. When he wants to take the game over, he will. I'm glad he's back. As a fan of the game, I'm glad to see Chris Paul back on the floor. He does whatever he needs to do to win and that's the kind of player I like.”
Kevin Durant on Chris PaulDec 11, 2010HoopsWorld
