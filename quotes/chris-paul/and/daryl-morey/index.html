Best quotes

Kevin Durant the Rockets trading for Chris Paul:

“I'm not surprised by anything, but it was kind of like 'Hmm. Damn, that's a smart move. I wouldn't think anybody would be that smart to pull that off.' I think it was a great move. You put two guys together that are crafty with the ball, they're going to get everybody shots and they going to get each other shots. It was a ballsy move to pull off by [Rockets GM Daryl] Morey. I don't think anybody would have thought to do that.”
Chris Paul on Daryl MoreyAug 28, 2017CSNBayArea.com

C.J. Paul told Yahoo Sports.

“Daryl Morey said he wasn’t going to trade Chris. Think about that. And now he’s going to the Finals on a team that three and four years ago he never would have played at. We thought Houston was going to be the last stop, and now it’s been two more stops. We’re just enjoying this, and I’m just happy for Chris.”
Chris Paul on Daryl MoreyJul 1, 2021Yahoo! Sports
“I loved working together on how to get his incredible Hall of Fame teammates Dwight Howard, Chris Paul, and Russell Westbrook to the Rockets. I will be rooting for James to win a championship in Houston. It's how this story should end.”
Daryl Morey on Chris Paul, Dwight Howard, Russell WestbrookOct 18, 2020ESPN
