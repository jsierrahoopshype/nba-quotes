Damian Lillard:

“I’m thankful to the Trail Blazers’ organization and Coach Stotts for giving me the opportunity and the freedom to go out there and accomplish something like this. Just looking at it being a list of Hall of Famers and LeBron (James) being the only active player to accomplish something like that, obviously it’s great company.”
Damian Lillard on LeBron James, Terry StottsJan 30, 2017NBA.com

Damian Lillard told CSNWW.com.

Damian Lillard on Terry StottsMar 28, 2014CSNNW.com
