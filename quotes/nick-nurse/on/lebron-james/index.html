Nick Nurse on LeBron's daily routine at today's practice:

“I think that we saw a little bit of it in the summer. He’s a very early guy, so he's in here very early, and he's got his set, straight to the weight room when he gets in, and then he's into his skill work and all that kind of stuff. So, listen, I think he's obviously in great condition. Played a long time. He knows what he's doing with that stuff. And I mean, to me it's always great to see. I always again, I'm an early person too. Fired up to get up and start my day and get working, and I like that. I think that'll transcend to some players.”
Nick Nurse on LeBron JamesOct 2, 2026x.com

Ausin Krell:Nick Nurse:

“[Embiid] and LeBron have both been a part of everything we've done. I will say they're both pretty smart about when we're scrimmaging and run 7 or 8 plays, they're getting themselves in and out. I think they're not overdoing it. But, yes, they want to get in shape, get the chemistry, feel.”
