Josh Lewenberg:Nurse on OG Anunoby:

“He's super hungry, he's super motivated to be a really good player at both ends of the floor. He's working, he's studying, his conditioning is amazing, his care for his body is amazing. He wants it really badly.”
Nick Nurse on OG AnunobyApr 17, 2021Twitter @JLew1050
