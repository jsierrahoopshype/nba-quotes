Jarrett Jack:

“Carl Landry is instant offense off the bench. We tried to get him to dig in a little more on defense, but he’ll figure it out. He sat next to me in the locker room, and he’s a great dude. I think he’d fit in well with the group.… We’d have to keep the leash attached, but Kenyon (Martin) is my guy.”
Jarrett Jack on Kenyon Martin, Carl LandryJul 13, 2012San Francisco Chronicle
