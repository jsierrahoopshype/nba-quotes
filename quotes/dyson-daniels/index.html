What NBA players and coaches have said about Dyson Daniels

5 quotes about Dyson Daniels from 5 players, coaches and executives, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

5 quotes, 2 of them from players·2022 to 2026·Latest quote Apr 23, 2026

Best quotes

Draymond Green:

“Oh man, I think—put it in the bag. That’s what I think. Put it in the bag. Yours truly. That’s who I’m rocking with. Listen, I think Evan Mobley has had a great year. Dyson Daniels has had a great year. I think Zubac has had a great year. All those guys have had great years. I ain’t taking nothing away from them—and I see them on First-Team All-Defense. But I think DPOY should go to yours truly. I’ll take that.”
Draymond Green on Evan Mobley, Dyson DanielsApr 13, 2025YouTube

Hawks coach Quin Snyder said Saturday.

“So, I don’t look at it as a positional thing where (we) talk about backup this. It’s the same way you look at a wing. Dyson (Daniels) is not Hunt’s (De’Andre Hunter) backup, Zacch’s (Zaccharie Risacher) not Dyson’s backup. Bogi’s (Bogdan Bogdanovic) not Zacch’s backup. Like you have players, and the most important thing is (how) the team functions. And that means, when I say functions, it’s successful.”
Quin Snyder on Bogdan Bogdanovic, De'Andre Hunter, Dyson Daniels, Zaccharie RisacherOct 19, 2024Atlanta Journal-Constitution

Jim Eichenhofer:Larry Nance Jr. on Dyson Daniels/#Pelicans:

“Dyson is everywhere. Defensively, he’s NBA-ready today, and offensively, he’s got confidence. Everybody on this roster right now can help us win NBA games, and I don’t know if there are two other teams in the league that can say that.”
Larry Nance Jr on Dyson DanielsOct 26, 2022Twitter @Jim_Eichenhofer
