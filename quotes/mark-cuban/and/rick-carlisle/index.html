Best quotes

Statement from Rick Carlisle on his contract extension:

“Mark Cuban, Donnie Nelson and Dirk Nowitzki are the reason an extension like this is possible. I have best owner and general manager in sports, and one of the greatest players in NBA history to thank for this opportunity. There is much work to be done as we move forward.”
RCRick Carlisle on Dirk Nowitzki, Mark Cuban, Donnie NelsonNov 5, 2015ESPN.com

Rick Carlisle on the speculation about Mark Cuban being nominated for vice president:

“When all that stuff was being talked about, I was curious to see who was going to run with it. I can certainly see the appeal of why someone would want Mark. He's extremely visible, extremely intelligent, business-savvy. He understands the media. I think he has a great grasp on a lot of things in the world including politics. I was a little uncertain if anybody had enough money to pay him to do it. Those jobs don't pay a lot.”
RCRick Carlisle on Mark CubanJun 4, 2016Dallas Morning News
“I just have such great respect for [Mavs owner Mark Cuban] and everyone there, and I'm fortunate to move on to another great opportunity.”
RCRick Carlisle on Mark CubanJun 24, 2021ESPN
