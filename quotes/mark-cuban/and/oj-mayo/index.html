Said owner Mark Cuban on Mayo:

“I think he can be a star and I think O.J. knows that this is his make-or-break, who-am-I-really-going-to-be-in-this-league year. And Coach [Rick Carlisle] is going to give him that opportunity. We’ll see what happens.”
MCMark Cuban on OJ MayoSep 19, 2012Dallas Morning News

OJ Mayo:

“I felt it was the best situation for me. They have a good team, they have a great player in Dirk Nowitzki , they have a terrific owner with Mark Cuban and Coach Carlisle is unbelievable,I watched him since he coached in Indiana. After seeing Jason Kidd and Jason Terry go their separate ways, I felt it was a good opportunity for me, a young guard, to go and compete.”
OJ Mayo on Dirk Nowitzki, Mark CubanJul 24, 2012Sports Radio Interviews
