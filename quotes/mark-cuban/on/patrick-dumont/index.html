Mark Cuban:

“Well, it's going to be tough because we don't have enough three-point shooters, which is part of the problem, right? So, that was a long time complaining about that. It's not like Nico and I talking about it. It's Nico talking to Patrick, and me talking to Patrick, and it's his job to assimilate all that and make a decision.”
