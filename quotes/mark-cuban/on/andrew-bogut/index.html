Mark Cuban:

“As well as Deron was playing and even Andrew defensively ... we had to match up to our criteria of trying to position ourselves to get a lot better. Then you combine that with the fact that I've always said, when a lot of teams are tanking, you don't want to tank. And when there aren't many teams tanking and everybody's trying to compete, that's the best time to consider trying to go for a draft pick. You can try to play as well as you can and still be in competition for a great pick.”
MCMark Cuban on Andrew BogutFeb 26, 2017ESPN
