What George Karl has said about Carmelo Anthony

Every quote from George Karl about Carmelo Anthony in the archive, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

7 quotes·2010 to 2016·Latest quote Dec 22, 2016

Best quotes

This is what Karl said:

“I think the fans have always been respectful here. I think they want us to be the best basketball team. I think all cities want their players to be loyal to the city. So I think you’re probably going to see a little bit of both. There’s going to be a lot of Melo love and there will probably be some Melo anger. Asked what Anthony could do to win all of his fans back, Karl quipped: “Sign the extension.”
George Karl on Carmelo AnthonyOct 2, 2010Denver Post

When asked if Carmelo Anthony needs another star to help carry the load and win, his former coach George Karl said:

“I am not going to comment on that, cost me like $35,000 last year... I think Carmelo is a great player, has had some bad (luck) and I would hope that he figures it out here in New York -- that is what I have heard he wants.”
George Karl on Carmelo AnthonyMar 21, 2016ESPN.com

Karl wrote.

“He was the best offensive player I ever coached. He was also a user of people, addicted to the spotlight and very unhappy when he had to share it. “He really lit my fuse with his low demand of himself on defense. He had no commitment to the hard, dirty work of stopping the other guy. My ideal — probably every coach’s ideal — is when your best player is also your leader. But since Carmelo only played hard on one side of the ball, he made it plain he couldn’t lead the Nuggets, even though he said he wanted to. Coaching him meant working around his defense and compensating for his attitude.”
George Karl on Carmelo AnthonyDec 22, 2016New York Post
