Best quotes

When asked if he regretted how he phrased anything, Karl said

“I think the one thing is (the) fatherhood (comments). Fatherhood is very important to me, and I made that a target. It seems like that was the one thing, andI said it poorly, I wrote it poorly, it’s read poorly in the book. And my whole thing is the one thing I regret probably most is some of the mothers (being offended). Kenyon Martin’s mother is one of the great mothers, superstar mothers of the NBA, and I would never want to take anything away from some of the special families and also special mothers.”
George Karl on Kenyon MartinJan 10, 2017USA Today Sports

George Karl on Kenyon Martin:

“It made me sad… It disappoints me that I can get a person that angry…I want to love my players. I want to care for my players but I am also the leader and the policeman of my team… I thought Kenyon Martin was one of the brightest, defensive players I’ve ever coached, and I thought he would make a hell of a coach.”
George Karl on Kenyon MartinJun 12, 2023Twitter @FanDuelTV
“I'm shocked. Ty Lawson and Arron Afflalo are coming up as really good players. We won 53 games and had some pretty serious bombs go off from my health to Kenyon Martin's health. I think Melo is a very mature pro right now. He's had a great camp. He knows that us keeping him is a possibility, and my job is to make it the possibility.”
George Karl on Kenyon MartinOct 17, 2010Denver Post
