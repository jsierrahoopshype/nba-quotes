Pop on whether Nikola Jokic has revolutionized C position:

“When I think of revolutions, I think of people like Che Guevara, Vladimir Ilyich Lenin. I don’t really think of Jokic as far as revolutionary, so I think you’re a bit hyperbolic there. But he is one heck of a player.”

Coach Pop on Porter Jr. and Jokic:

“Porter’s a fine young talent and Jokic, he’s like a reincarnation of Larry Bird, he does everything.”

Spurs coachGregg Popovich on Nikola Jokic:
