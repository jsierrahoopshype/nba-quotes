Gregg Popovich on David Fizdale:

“You don't know what's going on in another organization, but you know the people. I know Fiz. Like I told him, he should have no trouble whatsoever being hired elsewhere at some point, sooner rather than later, because he did a hell of a job. He knows his stuff, he cares, he cares about community as much as he does on the court. You hate to see that, but I know what his value is and how much he cares. It's unfortunate that it happened. It doesn't do any good to blame – ‘It's because of this, it's because of that.' Everybody will just move on and hope that the team does well and that Fiz ends up well someplace else.”
GPGregg Popovich on David FizdaleNov 29, 2017ESPN

Tom Orsborn:Pop on Fizdale firing:

“Fiz is a fine young coach. Nobody likes to see those guys have a short tenure. He did a hell of a job,and I don’t think he is going to have any problem landing some place. He is a good man. He knows what he is doing, is tough minded. He will be fine.”
