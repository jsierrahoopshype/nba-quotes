Popovich:

“Let me just answer it this way: I’ve been the coaching the Spurs team for a while now, and you go down to the arena, coaches have a little office where you can sit, and think about the game and work yourself up into a lather and think about things that confuse the issue and forget that it’s just basketball.“In that room, there’s a chair, there’s a table, and there’s a lamp. There’s one picture on the wall. It’s been there for the last 18, 20 years. It’s of John Havlicek. So I do think about the legacy here.”
GPGregg Popovich on John HavlicekNov 2, 2015San Antonio Express-News
