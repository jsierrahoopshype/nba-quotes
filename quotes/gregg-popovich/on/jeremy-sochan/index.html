Pop on Jeremy Sochan's progression as PG:

“Well it's been 5 or 6 games, and he's not gonna be Chris Paul in 6 games. He's learning a lot and likes the challenge. Every game is an education for him—offense against that pressure is tough. Over time hopefully we'll handle that better.”
GPGregg Popovich on Chris Paul, Jeremy SochanNov 6, 2023reddit

More from Pop on Sochan:

“He’s got a lot of personality like Keldon Johnson does. And he is aggressive. I think it’s a good opportunity for him to play with those guys and see how he handles things when the lights go on, so to speak.”

Pop on Jeremy pre-game:

“It’s like a new world for him. Every night is an education – guys he’s guarding, guys guarding him, keeping the team together in whatever offensive situation we are in. It’s a lot to think about when that hasn’t really been your focus.”
