“Overall he's having a great year for anybody in the NBA. I saw something that was great. For somebody that is shooting 48% or better from the field, 40% from three and averaging 18 plus points per game or higher, there's only three players in the NBA doing that, and that's [Kevin] Durant, it's Kon Knueppel and it's Jamal Murray from Denver. And so those are two All-Star guys and he's in the same conversation with them, too. So it's pretty incredible what he's doing as a rookie, the historical feats that he's matching, but he's also just been really efficient as an NBA player.”

Hornets coach Charles Lee on how Kon Knueppel has handled the Rookie of the Year race with Cooper Flagg:

“I think he's done great. I don't even think it's close. I think Kon's done a really good job continuing to separate himself & impact winning & have consistency to him on both ends of the floor. I do think their friendly competition & the fact they come from the same basketball program adds to it. But for the most part, Kon is a humble competitor who I don't even think gets caught up in it. He's probably gonna be mad at me for even saying something like that because he just wants to focus on our team & win games & impact it in any way he possibly can.”
CLCharles Lee on Cooper Flagg, Kon KnueppelMar 3, 2026x.com
