Danny Ainge:

“I don’t believe that Gordon Hayward thinks he’s going to play. ... I think that Gordon just wants to show everyone, when he comes back next year, all the work he put in to get back to who he was when he got here, or even better player than before he got hurt.”
Danny Ainge on Gordon HaywardMar 8, 2018Twitter @ESPNForsberg
