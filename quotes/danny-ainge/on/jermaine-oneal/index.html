Ainge on Jermaine O'Neal returning for the 2011-12 season:

“He has every intention of coming back. I wouldn’t say -- something could happen that he won’t come back -- but I think... he has every desire to come back and play, and didn’t like the way it finished this year. He doesn’t want to end his career on that kind of note, he wants to be a much bigger contributor next year.”
Danny Ainge on Jermaine O'NealJun 22, 2011ESPN.com
