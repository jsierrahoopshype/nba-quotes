Best quotes

On whether Rondo can push the new Celtics back to the top: Kevin Garnett:

“Absolutely. I'm sure he's going to push them to make the team better, and (president of basketball operations Danny Ainge) is going to do just that. The franchise has always been used to winning. They have a new coach and new system up there, so I'm thinking that's going to be a plus. I wish them all the best, man. I have no ill will towards anything in Boston.”
Kevin Garnett on Danny AingeSep 10, 2013Booth Newspapers
“Some guys might not like it because they don’t get to play as much as they want. But when Robert Parish and Dennis Johnson, going back to those days, came to the Celtics in that era, they loved it here. “And Larry Bird, [Kevin] McHale. I could go on and on. KG [Kevin Garnett] still talks about his days in Boston. So I don’t think that’s anything new. It’s an exciting, wonderful place to play. Almost everybody in my experience in those 21 years has loved it here.”
Danny Ainge on Kevin Garnett, Larry Bird, Robert ParishMar 13, 2016Boston Globe

With recent focus being placed on whether Kevin Garnett is a dirty player, Ainge suggested that teams and players throughout the league — including the ones Garnett has robbed the right way — would be happy to have him.

“Alvin Gentry wishes that Channing Frye played like Kevin Garnett, would give anything if Channing Frye played with the passion and the heart and the intensity and the work ethic of Kevin Garnett,”
Danny Ainge on Channing Frye, Kevin GarnettFeb 10, 2011WEEI.com
