Gordon Hayward:

“When I was in Utah, I had this marketing scheme. I play League of Legends. I put out a tweet or an Instagram about being the best, like I can beat anybody one-on-one in the NBA. I can beat LeBron one-on-one, but I was teasing, saying in League of Legends. We happened to play them like a week afterwards, and I got a text from somebody in Cleveland saying, 'Dude, why'd you do that? LeBron's talking about it. Why would you piss him off out of all people?' We went and played the game, and I went back and forth with him and ended up hitting a game-winner against them at home in Utah. So that was a pretty cool feeling.”
Gordon Hayward on LeBron JamesAug 8, 2024YouTube
