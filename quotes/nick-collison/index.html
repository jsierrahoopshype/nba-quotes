What NBA players and coaches have said about Nick Collison

6 quotes about Nick Collison from 5 players, coaches and executives, newest first. Each one links to its original source.

Best quotes

Durant told The Vertical on Thursday.

“I didn’t say that – words about me telling Russell or Nick that I would stay or leave never came out of my mouth. We met as teammates, but no promises came out of it. In this day and age, I can’t control anything people claim out there. Someone can go out and say something random right now, and people will believe it. I never told Russell or Nick [Collison], ‘All right, guys, I’m coming back to the Thunder’ – and then a week later, I decide not to. Never happened. I don’t operate like that. I heard people say that story, but it’s not the truth.”
Kevin Durant on Nick Collison, Russell WestbrookJul 28, 2016Yahoo! Sports
“No matter if they talk to me or they're mad at me. Whether it's Sam Presti or Troy Weaver or Russell Westbrook or Nick Collison. Whether it's Wilson Taylor or Clay Bennett and his family, I love them from the bottom of my heart. We're not talking, but eventually we will.”
Kevin Durant on Nick Collison, Sam Presti, Troy Weaver, Russell Westbrook, Clay BennettNov 16, 2017Bleacher Report

Alex Joseph:Ronnie Price:

“Nick Collison is a quiet leader. He's already established that. I'll be able to step in and handle that, as well.”
Ronnie Price on Nick CollisonSep 23, 2016Twitter @alex_broseph
