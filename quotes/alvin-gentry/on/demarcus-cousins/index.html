New Orleans Pelicans coach Alvin Gentry told The Bay Area News Group.

“I enjoyed coaching DeMarcus. I think he is a talent and I think he wants to win. I think that if you got those two things right there, you have an opportunity to have some success. We had success with him. I wish him nothing but the best.”
