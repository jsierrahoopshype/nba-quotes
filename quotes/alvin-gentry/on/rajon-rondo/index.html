The New OrleansPelicans, who re-signed Jrue Holiday to a five-year, $126 million contract and inked a one-year deal with Rajon Rondo, plan to have both point guards in the starting lineup this upcoming season, according to head coach Alvin Gentry.

“I like Jrue off the ball to start the game as a scorer. I like Rondo being on the floor as a leader,”
