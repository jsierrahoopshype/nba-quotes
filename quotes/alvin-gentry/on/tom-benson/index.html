Alvin Gentry on Tom Benson:

“The thing I remember most, after every win he comes down from the locker room and gets in the circle and puts his hand in and 1, 2,3 Pelicans or whatever. I think our guys really enjoy that, and knew that he cared so much about our team and everything we were trying to do. His goal was to try and bring a championship to New Orleans, same that he did for the Saints.”
AGAlvin Gentry on Tom BensonMar 15, 2018ESPN

Pelicans coachAlvin Gentry on the death of Tom Benson:

“We lost a giant. The things that he's done, not just for the Pelicans and the Saints, but for the state of Louisiana. He's a self-made man that had the biggest heart you could ever imagine. In my case, as a coach of a team, you would only want the owner to do everything he can to possibly help you win a championship. I can say without a doubt Mr. B has done that.”
