Best quotes

“I think the only difference in him not being heavily, heavily in the conversation is obvious we haven't had the type of season that a Houston has had or a Toronto has had, you know with (DeMar) DeRozan and those guys. But other than that, what he's done and what he's meant to our season, it can't be expressed in words. He's truly an MVP candidate. We just don't have the wins that those teams have and I think that's the difference in where he is on the scale of MVP as opposed to Harden or DeRozan.”
