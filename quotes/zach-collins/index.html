Michael Malone on the Blazers, Denver's opponent tomorrow:

“I think Portland's really good. Last year they were decimated by injuries. I think Terry does a really good job as their coach. One of the best backcourts in the NBA with CJ McCollum and Damian Lillard, Carmelo Anthony, Jusuf Nurkic. They've got Rodney Hood back. Zach Collins back. They get Robert Covington. They're a good basketball team. I think they're going to have a much better season in the regular season than they had last year because they're going to be healthy.”
MMMichael Malone on Carmelo Anthony, Damian Lillard, CJ McCollum, Robert Covington, Rodney Hood, Jusuf Nurkic, Zach CollinsDec 15, 2020Twitter @HarrisonWind

Donovan on Bulls’ inability to uphold their playstyle through recent skid:

“I’m not looking at film saying, ‘Oh, Kevin Huerter’s out, Zach Collins is out. Boy, if HE was here, this would be different.’ I don’t believe that. Some of it’s, like, inbounding the basketball. What do these guys being out have to do with that? What is five guys running back in transition, talking and communicating, have to do with anybody being out?”
BDBilly Donovan on Zach Collins, Kevin HuerterDec 10, 2025x.com

At that point, Trevor (Ariza) was in the starting lineup, so the exciting thing is to see the guys out playing basketball again. I missed basketball.”
