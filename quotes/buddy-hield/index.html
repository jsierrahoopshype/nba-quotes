Tyrese Haliburton on adjusting without Buddy Hield:

“Yeah, it's been weird for sure. I mean, obviously, I knew at some point we wouldn't be able to play together for my whole career, but obviously with the trade and stuff, it's been different. And I think on the court is obvious, right, without him. He's so prominent in our offense and what we do with a lot of ghost screens and the way that we move, and like his presence shooting on the floor like that opens up a ton for me because you know you're not going to be as much in the Gap if you're guarding Buddy because you know who he is as a shooter. So it's been a little different on the court obviously, but I think off the court has been kind of the biggest adjustment for me.”
