AJ Dybantsa:

“The Wizards haven't been to the playoffs in some time, so I just want to make the playoffs year one. I think we have the team to do it, getting Anthony Davis, getting Trae Young and having our young core. But ultimate goal is I'll start getting D-Wade, I always wanted to be a Hall of Famer, but he says the goal I should have is to be a top 100 player. So I think 20 years from now, I answer the league 20 years from now, it will be the 100-year anniversary of the NBA. And you know, like top 75, they bring the 75 best players, I always says when they bring the 100 players out, that should be my goal instead of just being a Hall of Famer.”
AJ Dybantsa on Dwyane Wade, Anthony Davis, Trae YoungJun 29, 2026YouTube
“A lot of No. 1 picks come into a situation where they are ‘The Guy’ (and) everything’s kind of centered around them. But I have All-Star Hall-of-Famers on my team that I can (learn) under and get some knowledge from: Khris Middleton, Trae Young, AD and Deandre Ayton. So I don’t have to come in and carry this huge load. I think I can just bond with these guys and learn it slowly and learn how to play the right way.”
AJ Dybantsa on Khris Middleton, Deandre Ayton, Trae YoungSep 28, 2026New York Times
